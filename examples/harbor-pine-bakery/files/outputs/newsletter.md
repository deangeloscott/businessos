## Why the loaf takes 36 hours

On Thursday at 16:00 we mix and fold the dough. It rests in the cold until Saturday at 02:00, then it is shaped, proofed and baked from 04:00. That slow rest is the whole recipe. We bake every day and sell it the same day. What is left at closing goes to the harbour food pantry.

## Prices

You may have heard that a bakery nearby has lowered its sourdough price. We are keeping our loaf at $9.00. Flour costs us a little more than it did six weeks ago, and we would rather keep the loaf the same size and the ferment the same length.
