# Carousel: how our sourdough is made

**Channel:** Instagram. **Format:** five frames. **Why this post:** making-of videos are our best-saved posts, at 36 saves on average against 14 for product photos.

**Claims used:** "Our sourdough ferments for 36 hours" (approved, proof current) and "Everything is baked the day it is sold" (approved). Nothing here mentions price or rivals.

---

### Frame 1: the hook

**On the image:** a floured hand next to a dough tub.
**Text:** *36 hours from mixing to oven.*
**Caption line:** Swipe to see where the time goes.

### Frame 2: Thursday, 16:00

**On the image:** the dough being folded.
**Text:** *Thursday, 16:00. We mix and fold the dough.*

### Frame 3: the cold rest

**On the image:** trays in the walk-in fridge.
**Text:** *Then it rests in the cold until early Saturday. This is where the flavour comes from.*

### Frame 4: Saturday, 02:00 to 04:00

**On the image:** loaves being shaped and proofing.
**Text:** *Saturday, 02:00. Shaped and proofed. 04:00, into the oven.*

### Frame 5: the close

**On the image:** a loaf with a pre-order name tag.
**Text:** *Ready when we open at 6:30. Pre-order by Thursday evening.*
**Call to action:** Message us to reserve a Saturday loaf.
