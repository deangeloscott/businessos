# Competitor brief: four nearby bakeries

**Prepared this week for the owner. Sources are the files in `sources/`, named in each section.**

## What changed

Crumb & Co cut its sourdough loaf from $9.50 to $7.50 this week. Last week we were the cheapest of the five at $9.00. We no longer are, so we have retired the claim "our sourdough is the cheapest in Port Hollis". (`rival-menu-prices.md`)

## Each rival in a few lines

**Crumb & Co.** Bakery and cafe, open longest. One reviewer says the loaf "looks smaller than it used to be" since the price cut. That is a single review, so treat it as a lead and not a fact. (`rival-reviews.md`)

**Salt Rye Bakehouse.** Highest price and the best rating of the four rivals. Takes sourdough reservations by message. (`rival-menu-prices.md`, `rival-reviews.md`)

**The Daily Loaf.** Lowest rating of the five (4.2). Started an online pre-order form this month. Two recent reviews mention a long wait. (`rival-menu-prices.md`, `rival-reviews.md`)

**Little Anchor Pastries.** A pastry shop that sells sourdough on Fridays and Saturdays only. (`rival-menu-prices.md`)

## What it means for us

1. **We are still below three of the four rivals on price.** At $9.00 we sit under $10.00, $10.50 and $11.00.
2. **We do not need to match $7.50 to fill the oven.** Saturday pre-orders have grown for four weeks, from 31 to 47, and sourdough sold out before noon on the last three Saturdays. The owner has decided to hold $9.00 and test an offer instead of a cut. (`order-log.md`)
3. **Pre-orders are no longer ours alone.** Salt Rye Bakehouse and The Daily Loaf both take them now. We are still first in map results for "Saturday sourdough pre-order Port Hollis".
4. **Crumb & Co's hours are a gap for us.** They are open until 17:00 and we close at 14:00. We have not tested whether afternoon customers would come to us.
5. **Our profile is thin.** We list one category and 14 photos. Crumb & Co and The Daily Loaf list two categories and rank above us. (`local-search-results.md`)

## What we do not know

- Whether Crumb & Co's cut is temporary.
- Whether its loaf is smaller. One review says so.
- Why Crumb & Co ranks first in map results. Rating, reviews, categories and photos all differ, and we have not tested which one matters.

## Suggested next steps

- Keep checking all five prices weekly. The Local competitors list does this.
- Add a second category and new photos to our business profile, then watch the map rank.
- Run the two-week weekend test the owner is being asked to approve.
