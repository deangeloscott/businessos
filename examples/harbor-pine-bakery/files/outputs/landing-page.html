Why it takes 36 hours

We mix the dough on Thursday at 16:00 and let it rest slowly in the cold until Saturday at 02:00. It is shaped and proofed, then goes into the oven at 04:00. The long rest is what gives the crust its colour and the crumb its flavour.

Bread is baked the day it is sold. Anything left at closing goes to the harbour food pantry.

How to pre-order

  1. Pick your loaves. Plain sourdough is $9.00. Ask what the seeded loaf is doing this week.
  2. Tell us by Thursday evening. Message the shop or tell us at the counter. This page is an example, so there is no real order button.
  3. Collect on Saturday. Come any time from 7:00 to 10:30. Your loaf has your name on it.
