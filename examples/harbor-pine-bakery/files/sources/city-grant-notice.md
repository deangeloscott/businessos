# Port Hollis Small Business Grant: notice (example)

This file is part of AURA's example business. Harbor & Pine Bakery is made up, and so is everything here.

Copied this week from the city's grants page. The grant is invented.

- Up to $10,000 per business, once a year.
- Open to food businesses in Port Hollis with 1 to 15 employees that have traded for at least two years.
- Pays for kitchen equipment and delivery equipment. It does not pay wages or rent.
- The applicant pays 20% of the project cost.
- The application needs last year's revenue (2025), two supplier quotes and a one-page project description.
- Applications close at 17:00, 6 days after this copy was saved.
- Decisions are made about six weeks after closing.
