# Production log (example)

This file is part of AURA's example business. Harbor & Pine Bakery is made up, and so is everything here.

## How a sourdough loaf is made

| Step | When |
|---|---|
| Dough mixed and folded | Thursday 16:00 |
| Cold rest in the walk-in fridge | Thursday evening to Saturday 02:00 |
| Shaped and proofed | Saturday 02:00 to 04:00 |
| In the oven | Saturday from 04:00 |

Time from mixing to oven: 36 hours. The same schedule is used for the weekday loaves, one day earlier or later.

## Baking and selling

- Baking starts at 04:00 and the shop opens at 06:30.
- Bread and pastries are sold the day they are baked. Anything unsold at closing is donated to the harbour food pantry the same afternoon.
- Bread flour comes from Tidewater Mill, delivered weekly.
- Last Saturday: 120 sourdough loaves baked, 120 sold.
