# Customer Intelligence Defaults

## Knowledge Scope
Customer Intelligence provides operating knowledge for evidence-backed interpretation of what customers and prospects want, need, believe, fear, ask, compare, choose, reject, complain about, respond to, and value. Journey mechanics, competitor analysis, marketing persuasion, SEO demand, and product decisions are distinct problem areas whose relevant operating knowledge may be used directly by the active model when needed.

## Research
- Prefer direct customer evidence when the claim is about customer belief or decision reason. Behavioral evidence may support or challenge it but does not automatically establish why the customer acted.
- Separate customer-stated evidence from salesperson, support-agent, or analyst inference.
- Customer evidence may be multimodal: interviews/calls, transcripts, support conversations, reviews, screenshots/images, videos, surveys, emails, comments, CRM notes, product/usage evidence, and structured exports. Inspect the underlying evidence when available and preserve modality/acquisition limitations according to `core/policies/intelligence-foundation.md`.
- Before broad research, reuse sufficiently fresh and specific canonical Customer Insights.

## Decision Context and Motivations
- Where evidence supports it, identify how decision drivers differ by segment, awareness/knowledge state, journey/lifecycle stage, urgency, and situation.
- Motivation lenses such as desired gain, loss avoidance, certainty/risk reduction, control, speed, simplicity, effort reduction, financial outcome, status/identity, autonomy, belonging, or convenience are hypotheses to test against customer evidence—not personality labels or mandatory categories.
- Preserve customer language and observed choice behavior separately from the analyst's psychological interpretation. Do not claim a motivation is dominant merely because a persuasion framework contains it.

## Adjacent Evidence
Customer research may uncover durable observations about competitors, journey friction, product conditions, marketing, or search. Preserve a useful observation once with truthful provenance when future work benefits from it, then let the active model apply whichever operating knowledge is relevant. Do not create duplicate domain-specific Insights merely to simulate a handoff between AURA modules.

## Learning
Domain Learning captures what improves customer understanding for this business. Promote broader Business Learning only when evidence spans domains and supports the broader conclusion.

## Public Signals
- Public comments, reviews, forums, and social discussion are customer evidence sources, not permission to profile people. Analyze statements and experience themes rather than personality or sensitive traits.
- Link a public signal to a known customer only when the identity match is sufficiently explicit and the actual privacy/access constraints permit that use. The CRM/customer system remains the identity system of record.
- Treat testimonial/result candidates as ProofRecords with source and usage permission; a positive comment is not automatically an advertising endorsement.
## Standalone Mode
Customer Intelligence can operate with Core alone. Optional modules may provide useful operating knowledge or evidence, but their absence does not remove customer-research steps.
