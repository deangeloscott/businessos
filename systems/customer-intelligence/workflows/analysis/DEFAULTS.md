# Customer Analysis Defaults

- Define the population, period, segment, Offer, stage, and source coverage before comparing patterns.
- Preserve multiple contributing causes when evidence supports them; do not force one reason per customer or event.
- Check contradictory cases, missingness, survivorship/selection bias, and whether the evidence can support a causal statement or only an association.
- Publish a scoped Insight only when a downstream reader can tell what is believed, why, for whom/where it applies, confidence, and material contrary evidence. Otherwise maintain a knowledge gap or candidate Insight.
