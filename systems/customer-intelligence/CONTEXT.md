# Customer Intelligence

## Purpose
Continuously develop evidence-backed understanding of what customers/prospects want, need, believe, fear, expect, compare, reject, complain about, respond to, and value—and how those decision drivers differ by segment, awareness, journey/lifecycle context, and situation.

## Canonical Scope
Customer pains/desires/outcomes, objections, decision criteria, triggers, questions, language, win/loss, churn reasons, complaints, feature requests, sentiment, segment differences, and evidence-backed motivation/decision mechanisms such as gain, loss avoidance, certainty, control, effort, speed, financial outcome, status/identity, autonomy, belonging, or convenience when the evidence supports them.

## Explicit Non-Scope
Does not own organic demand, competitor strategy, journey/process optimization, content production, persuasion, product roadmap, or sales execution. Customer motivations remain evidence-bounded customer understanding; downstream systems decide how to use them.

## Shared behavior
Use Core canonical objects and policies. Consume current canonical intelligence before repeating broad research. Treat interviews/calls, transcripts, reviews, support/sales conversations, surveys, comments, screenshots/images/video, behavioral data, and structured records as possible evidence modalities. Preserve customer language and observed choice separately from analyst interpretation. Publish reusable Observations/Insights. Create Opportunities only for independent interventions inside this domain; delegate production through WorkRequests. Persist only domain-specific state under the active business instance.
