# Customer Intelligence

## Purpose
Continuously develop evidence-backed understanding of what customers/prospects want, need, believe, fear, expect, compare, reject, complain about, respond to, and value.

## Canonical Scope
Customer pains/desires/outcomes, objections, decision criteria, triggers, questions, language, win/loss, churn reasons, complaints, feature requests, sentiment, segment differences.

## Explicit Non-Scope
Does not own organic demand, competitor strategy, journey/process optimization, content production, persuasion, product roadmap, or sales execution.

## Shared behavior
Use Core canonical objects and policies. Consume current canonical intelligence before repeating broad research. Publish reusable Observations/Insights. Create Opportunities only for independent interventions inside this domain; delegate production through WorkRequests. Persist only domain-specific state under the active business instance.
