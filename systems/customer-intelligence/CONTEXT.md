# Customer Intelligence

## Purpose
Develop evidence-backed understanding of what customers and prospects want, need, believe, fear, expect, compare, reject, complain about, respond to, experience, and value—and how those decision drivers differ by segment, awareness, journey/lifecycle context, and situation.

## Canonical Scope
Customer pains/desires/outcomes, objections, decision criteria, triggers, questions, language, win/loss, churn reasons, complaints, feature requests, sentiment, segment differences, and evidence-backed motivation/decision mechanisms when the evidence supports them.

## Explicit Non-Scope
Does not own organic demand, competitor strategy, journey/process optimization, content production, persuasion, product roadmap, sales execution, runtime scheduling, or cross-domain routing. Customer motivations remain evidence-bounded customer understanding; the active model/user decides how to use them elsewhere.

## Shared behavior
Use Core canonical organization truth and policies. Reuse current customer evidence and Insights before repeating broad research. Interviews/calls, transcripts, reviews, support/sales conversations, surveys, comments, screenshots/images/video, behavioral data, and structured records may all be useful evidence depending on the question.

Preserve customer language, observed behavior/choice, source context, and analyst/model interpretation distinctly enough to avoid turning inference into customer truth. Publicly visible evidence is not permission for invasive identity profiling or unrestricted promotional reuse.

Use Customer Intelligence Playbooks and Workflows as reusable expert knowledge, not mandatory routes. Other work may directly use relevant Customer Insights and ProofRecords without a WorkRequest, relevance event, duplicate Opportunity, or handoff object merely because the work crosses an AURA knowledge boundary.

Persist only customer-specific evidence, Insights, ProofRecords, Learning, decisions, or monitoring context that future organizational work will materially benefit from.
