# SEO/AEO Defaults

## Domain Boundary
SEO/AEO owns valuable organic discovery across search, answer engines, AI interfaces, local discovery, and related organic surfaces. It does not own broad customer research, competitor strategy, content production, marketing persuasion, or customer-journey optimization.

## Mechanism
- Tie diagnostic search/AEO metrics to the active Objective and expected organic mechanism; do not optimize rankings, traffic, citations, or other mechanism metrics without business relevance.
- Reuse current Customer, Competitor, Industry, and shared SourceProfile intelligence. When it is unavailable, bounded SEO research is allowed for continuity: publish direct Observations and mark foreign-domain interpretation provisional.
- Organic experience alignment remains SEO-owned only when the causal problem is query/prompt intent → discovery presentation → landing experience. Route broader persuasion or journey friction to Marketing or Customer Optimization.

## Contextual Organic Competition
- Define the actual discovery surface and intent before selecting comparison targets. Organic competitors may differ across classic SERPs, AI/answer citations, local/map packs, directories/marketplaces, image/video search, and other surfaces.
- For local work, prioritize businesses actually competing in the relevant geography/service area and query/map-pack context. Do not benchmark a local business primarily against a national category giant unless the decision genuinely calls for a national/aspirational comparison.
- For each comparison set, distinguish direct business competitors from search/attention competitors and authoritative publishers. Analyze what is relevant to the surface rather than treating domain size/fame as a universal benchmark.
- Use geography, scale, market position, offer/customer overlap, and surface relevance to select peers; preserve proximity or other platform-specific factors as constraints when they cannot be changed.

## Execution and Verification
Missing provider capability creates manual work rather than deletion of the SEO process. For external mutation, use Core ChangeEvent and independent VerificationRecord rules.

## Learning
Feed verified OutcomeEvaluations into SEO Domain Learning. Promote to Business/System Learning only when evidence supports the broader scope.

## Standalone Mode
SEO/AEO can operate with Core alone using the bounded upstream-research rule above. Treat missing optional intelligence modules as enrichments, never as permission to claim their canonical domain conclusions.
