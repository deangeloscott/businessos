# SEO/AEO

## Purpose
Continuously improve valuable organic discovery across search engines, answer engines, AI interfaces, local discovery, and related organic surfaces.

## Canonical Scope
Organic demand, search/AEO visibility, technical search, search architecture, organic content requirements, authority, local organic discovery, contextual organic competition, SEO monitoring/incidents/measurement, and comparison of the pages/entities actually competing on the relevant search/answer/local surface.

## Explicit Non-Scope
Does not own broad customer/competitor/industry research, generic content creation, persuasion, or customer lifecycle optimization. It may use bounded upstream evidence for continuity but must not silently become those domains' semantic owner.

## Shared behavior
Use Core canonical objects and policies. Consume current canonical intelligence before repeating broad research. Select comparison targets for the actual intent, geography/service area, scale, market position, offer/customer overlap, and discovery surface; do not default to famous national brands when the business is competing in a local/map-pack context. Publish reusable Observations/Insights. Create Opportunities only for independent interventions inside this domain; delegate production through WorkRequests. Persist only domain-specific state under the active business instance.
