# SEO/AEO

## Purpose
Improve valuable organic discovery across search engines, answer engines, AI interfaces, local discovery, and related organic surfaces.

## Canonical Scope
Organic demand, search/AEO visibility, technical search, search architecture, organic content requirements, authority, local organic discovery, contextual organic competition, SEO monitoring/incidents/measurement, and comparison of the pages/entities actually competing on the relevant search/answer/local surface.

## Explicit Non-Scope
Does not own broad customer/competitor/industry research, generic content creation, persuasion, customer lifecycle optimization, runtime scheduling, or tool/provider control. It may use the relevant evidence and operating knowledge from those areas directly when useful without becoming their semantic owner.

## Shared behavior
Use Core canonical organization truth and policies. Reuse current organizational evidence before repeating broad research. Select comparison targets for the actual intent, geography/service area, scale, market position, Offer/customer overlap, and discovery surface; do not default to famous national brands when the business is actually competing in a local/map-pack context.

Treat rankings, search visibility, AI recommendations/citations, local visibility, links/mentions, traffic, and other discovery signals as potentially meaningful because they can increase exposure and downstream business opportunity. Keep those stages distinct from clicks, conversions, leads, revenue, and other outcomes when they have not actually been observed.

Use relevant Playbooks and Workflows as reusable expert knowledge, not mandatory routes. The active model/user may continue directly into content, marketing, customer, competitor, technical, or other work when that is the natural next step; a WorkRequest, Opportunity, or other handoff object is not required merely to cross an AURA knowledge boundary.

Persist only domain-specific state, evidence, decisions, Learning, or continuity that future organizational work will materially benefit from.
