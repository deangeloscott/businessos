# Discovery Feature Opportunity

## Purpose
Identify valuable result formats or discovery-surface features the business could legitimately qualify for or better serve.

## Business Outcome
Find feature-specific opportunities that improve useful discovery without confusing feature presence, eligibility, visibility, or ownership with guaranteed business value.

## Run When
Use when current search, local, shopping, image, video, news, discussion, rich-result, or other result-surface evidence makes a feature materially relevant to the decision.

## Process
1. Inspect the result ecosystems relevant to valuable customer demand and identify recurring feature or media types that materially shape the experience.
2. Judge whether each feature aligns with actual user intent and whether the business already has, or could legitimately create, useful eligible content, data, media, product information, or local information.
3. Inspect owned eligibility, structured information, media, product/local data, technical constraints, and relevant competitor/source examples only to the depth needed to understand the gap.
4. Distinguish eligibility from expected visibility and distinguish visibility from business value; do not infer that a feature should be pursued simply because it exists.
5. Identify the most useful content, media, structured-data, local, product, authority, or technical intervention when one is supported. Preserve an Opportunity only when durable coordination adds value.
6. If work is performed, evaluate the relevant surface and business outcome without implying guaranteed placement or permanent feature ownership.

## Proportionate Scope
Focus first on features repeatedly present for high-value, realistic demand and on evidence likely to change the intervention decision. Expand to broader feature research only when it can materially improve strategy or reveal a useful benchmark.

## Verification
- Feature presence, eligibility, user value, expected visibility, and likely business effect remain distinct.
- A detected feature does not itself require an intervention.
- Surface-specific evidence remains reproducible enough to re-check later when material.
