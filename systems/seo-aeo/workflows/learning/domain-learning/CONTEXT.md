# SEO/AEO Domain Learning

## Purpose
Turn sufficiently supported organic-discovery outcomes, corrections, contradictions, and repeated work into scoped reusable guidance for future SEO/AEO judgment.

## Business Outcome
Help future SEO/AEO work make better evidence-based choices for this organization without creating a tactic registry, lifecycle engine, or self-tuning optimization controller.

## Run When
Use when comparable outcomes, experiments, corrections, failures, authoritative guidance, or repeated SEO/AEO work provide enough evidence that remembering a conditional lesson would materially improve future quality or efficiency. Time passing alone is not a reason to run a learning cycle.

## Process
1. [AI] Retrieve potentially related SEO/AEO Learning and group genuinely comparable evidence by mechanism, search/answer surface, demand type, market, asset type, audience/business context, and other conditions that could change applicability. The capable model decides whether evidence concerns the same Learning, a refinement/supersession, or a distinct lesson; deterministic matching does not decide semantic identity.
2. [HYBRID] Separate plausible intervention effect from demand, algorithm/platform changes, competitor movement, seasonality, concurrent changes, measurement error, and other confounders using the strongest available causal evidence.
3. [AI] Identify repeatable mechanisms, failure modes, response conditions, and measurement heuristics. A publication, practitioner opinion, competitor behavior, one business result, popularity, or model confidence is not by itself proof of a generally valid tactic.
4. [AI] State the mechanism, prerequisites, applies-when, does-not-apply-when, supporting and contradictory evidence, uncertainty, external guidance constraints, and what evidence would revise the lesson. Do not create hard thresholds or universal rules unless the evidence actually supports them.
5. [AI] When evidence strengthens, weakens, contradicts, or supersedes existing guidance, choose the narrowest justified change to its confidence, applicability, maturity/status, or content. Preserve historical and contradictory evidence when it materially affects future interpretation; never invent replacement guidance merely to fill a gap.
6. [AI] Keep SEO-specific guidance scoped to organic discovery. A broader Business Learning is justified only when evidence across domains supports the broader claim. If evidence suggests canonical AURA operating knowledge should improve, `core.learning.workflow-evolution` may be useful as separate deliberate product-development knowledge; organization work does not silently rewrite AURA source.
7. [HYBRID] Create or update Learning only when forgetting the pattern would materially reduce future quality or efficiency. Deterministic helpers may validate and persist the model/user's chosen Learning; they do not decide semantic identity, maturity, applicability, contradiction, promotion, or deprecation.
8. [AI] If a changed lesson materially affects current organizational work, surface that fact through the smallest useful continuity mechanism rather than creating an automatic route or runtime event.

## Verification
- Learning scope does not exceed the supporting evidence.
- Supporting, contradictory, and superseding evidence remains traceable when material.
- Search/platform correlation is not treated as causal intervention effect without appropriate evidence.
- Applicability, uncertainty, and revision conditions are explicit enough for future work to use the lesson intelligently.
- No scheduler, tactic registry, lifecycle engine, automatic threshold tuner, tactic router, or deterministic semantic promotion/deprecation is encoded in Learning.
- No canonical AURA product source is silently mutated.

## Completion Criteria
- Future SEO/AEO work can reuse an evidence-backed conditional lesson that improves judgment.
- Any strengthening, narrowing, contradiction, supersession, or deprecation is justified by evidence and scoped no more broadly than that evidence supports.
