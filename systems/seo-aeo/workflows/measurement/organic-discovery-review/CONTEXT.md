# Organic Discovery Review

## Purpose
Give the organization an evidence-backed view of organic-discovery performance, explanations, current work, and justified priorities at the horizon required by the decision.

## Business Outcome
Help tactical, business, and strategic SEO/AEO decisions reflect real business value and current evidence without creating separate weekly/monthly/quarterly reporting machinery or an opaque composite score.

## Run When
Use when a current decision or review needs a consolidated SEO/AEO performance view. Choose a review horizon appropriate to the question—recent tactical changes, a meaningful business period, or a longer strategic horizon. AURA does not own the cadence or schedule.

## Process
1. [HYBRID] Define the decision, review horizon, relevant Objectives, and available evidence. Check data completeness, comparability, attribution limitations, sample size, seasonality, and other constraints before interpreting movement.
2. [HYBRID] Lead with the strongest available business outcomes—profit, revenue, qualified opportunities/leads, conversions, assists, retention/value effects, or clearly labeled proxies—then use qualified organic traffic and discovery visibility to explain rather than replace them.
3. [HYBRID] Add only the diagnostic layers that materially help the decision: search impressions/clicks/CTR/position/query coverage/SERP features; AI-answer mentions/recommendations/citations/referrals; local visibility; authority/reputation; crawl/index/technical state; demand; competitor movement; or other relevant evidence. Keep unlike dimensions separate enough to interpret rather than collapsing them into one score.
4. [HYBRID] Segment by market, offer, audience, topic/cluster, page/asset type, device, discovery surface, intervention, or other dimensions only when the segmentation can plausibly explain material change or alter a decision.
5. [AI] Explain gains/losses through the strongest supported mechanisms while separating observation from hypothesis. Consider demand, visibility, CTR/SERP, conversion, technical/index, authority, local/AEO, ecosystem, competitor, and business-context effects without forcing a cause where evidence is insufficient.
6. [AI] Scale depth to the horizon. For a tactical review, emphasize material recent changes, active work, incidents, blockers, and immediate evidence-backed next questions. For a business-period review, emphasize value contribution, material changes/experiments/costs, and the smallest set of justified priorities. For a strategic review, reassess demand, markets, competitors, surfaces, asset portfolio, architecture, authority/reputation, proven mechanisms, and structural investments or removals only to the depth that could change strategy.
7. [AI] Review current Opportunities, ChangeEvents, experiments, and relevant work by value, evidence, costs, actual constraints, and unresolved uncertainty—not by invented approval/autonomy state. Do not preserve a tactic or investment merely because it existed in an older plan.
8. [AI] Identify the smallest useful next decisions/questions with explicit business and evidence rationale. Other diagnosis, Learning, experiment, or execution Workflows may help, but the review does not automatically route, authorize, or create work.
9. [HYBRID] Preserve an organizational decision only when a decision is actually made and future work benefits from remembering it. Persist new MetricObservations only when the review computes durable measurement meaning not already represented by underlying evidence.

## Verification
- Business outcomes lead; proxies and diagnostic metrics remain labeled.
- Surface-specific and unlike measurements remain interpretable rather than becoming an opaque score.
- Material changes are distinguished from routine noise.
- Explanations distinguish evidence from hypotheses and make important measurement/attribution uncertainty visible.
- Review depth is proportionate to the decision horizon and stakes rather than mechanically determined by a calendar cadence.
- The review informs decisions without becoming a routing, approval, or scheduling layer.

## Completion Criteria
- A decision-maker can understand what organic discovery contributed, what materially changed, how reliable the evidence is, and what deserves attention or strategic change at the horizon relevant to the decision.
