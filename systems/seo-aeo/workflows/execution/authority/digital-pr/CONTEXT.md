# Digital PR

## Purpose
Create genuinely newsworthy, useful, or evidence-backed stories and resources that relevant publishers or communities may choose to reference.

## Business Outcome
Earn credible coverage, citations, links, referral attention, or authority by contributing something worth publishing rather than manufacturing link bait or outreach volume.

## Use When
Use when the organization has, or can responsibly create, original evidence, expert insight, meaningful news, analysis, public resources, or another contribution that is relevant to publishers and valuable to their audiences.

## Process
1. Start from a real business, audience, market, or topic opportunity rather than a generic desire for links.
2. Identify the publishers, journalists, communities, analysts, or other sources that matter and understand the questions, data needs, beats, news cycles, and formats they actually cover.
3. Develop a defensible angle using original data, expert knowledge, product or company news, analysis, research, public resources, or another source of genuine informational value. Do not manufacture novelty or unsupported conclusions.
4. Verify methodology, factual claims, sourcing, rights, privacy, confidentiality, and relevant legal/brand constraints before external use.
5. Package the material so a recipient can evaluate it efficiently: clear findings or story value, supporting evidence/methodology, useful visuals or source materials, expert availability where real, and an appropriate canonical destination.
6. If outreach is requested, tailor it to the recipient's actual beat/context and use the host's real communication tools. Track meaningful coverage, citations, links, referral/business outcomes, and Learning only to the extent they materially improve future work.

## Proportional Scope
Match research, asset production, and outreach depth to the strength of the story, relevance of the audience, stakes, and expected value. A smaller set of highly relevant sources is usually more useful than broad indiscriminate outreach.
