# Guest / Expert Contribution

## Purpose
Contribute genuine expertise to third-party audiences when the contribution itself has editorial, audience, or business value.

## Business Outcome
Earn credible visibility, relationships, mentions, citations, or referral attention by helping an external audience rather than publishing recycled SEO filler for a link.

## Use When
Use when a relevant publication, community, podcast, event, resource, or other third-party surface accepts expert contributions and the organization has something substantively useful to contribute.

## Process
1. Identify sources with relevant audiences and legitimate contribution models. Prioritize actual audience and editorial fit over superficial authority metrics.
2. Review editorial standards, topical gaps, author or guest requirements, disclosure expectations, and relevant linking or promotional norms.
3. Choose an angle grounded in demonstrable expertise, first-party experience, original evidence, or another real source of value. Do not fabricate credentials, outcomes, or novelty.
4. If outreach or pitching is requested, tailor the proposal to the source's audience and format. If accepted, produce original useful work that can stand on its own without relying on a backlink for value.
5. Keep bios, links, affiliations, claims, disclosures, and calls to action accurate and proportionate to the editorial context.
6. Preserve publication, relationship, referral/business impact, mentions, or Learning when future work materially benefits from it. Do not equate publication or a link with proven SEO or business impact.

## Proportional Scope
Invest in the sources and contribution opportunities with the strongest audience fit, credibility, and expected value. Broaden only when additional outreach or content materially improves the opportunity set.
