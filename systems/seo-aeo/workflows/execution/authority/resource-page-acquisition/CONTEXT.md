# Resource Page Acquisition

## Purpose
Earn inclusion on curated resource pages or directories when the organization or its resource genuinely belongs there.

## Business Outcome
Gain useful third-party discovery and referral value through legitimate curated inclusion rather than indiscriminate directory or resource-page submissions.

## Use When
Use when a relevant curated page serves the target audience, topic, market, or community and an owned resource, business, or location materially satisfies its purpose and inclusion criteria.

## Process
1. Identify curated pages or directories that are genuinely relevant to the target audience, topic, geography, profession, or market.
2. Inspect the page's purpose, inclusion criteria, existing resources, maintenance recency, editorial legitimacy, and whether the organization would actually improve the resource set.
3. Match a specific owned resource, business, location, tool, guide, or other appropriate destination to the page's real purpose. Do not force a fit simply because the page links externally.
4. Prepare the exact factual description, evidence, category information, and destination needed for evaluation. Keep all claims grounded in current organization truth.
5. If submission or outreach is requested, use the preferred legitimate channel and comply with the source's editorial rules. Do not imply entitlement to inclusion or conceal commercial relationships.
6. Verify any resulting listing or link for accuracy and destination integrity. Preserve material outcomes or future monitoring intent when useful; the host owns any recurring recheck.

## Proportional Scope
Focus on curated sources with strong audience relevance, legitimacy, and expected value. Avoid scaling submissions across low-value pages merely because they are easy to find.
