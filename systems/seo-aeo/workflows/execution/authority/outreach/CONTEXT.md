# Authority Outreach

## Purpose
Evaluate and pursue legitimate authority opportunities through relevant, personalized outreach and sound response handling.

## Business Outcome
Earn useful mentions, links, citations, relationships, or placements when they genuinely serve the audience and business, without manufacturing authority or compromising editorial integrity.

## Run When
Use when a relevant authority opportunity may reasonably benefit from direct outreach or when an existing outreach conversation needs a response.

## Process
1. [HYBRID] Verify the source, site, page, audience, and contact are real and relevant. Inspect editorial patterns, outbound links, ownership, spam indicators, paid-placement signals, conflicts, prior contact history, and any policy or reputational concerns that materially affect whether outreach is appropriate.
2. [AI] Determine what legitimate value the organization can provide and whether the opportunity fits the intended target page, audience, business objective, and authority mechanism. Prefer relevance and real ecosystem value over superficial authority metrics.
3. [AI] Judge expected value, likelihood, material cost, strategic leverage, and downside proportionately. Decline or deprioritize opportunities whose audience fit, editorial legitimacy, business relevance, or expected value does not justify pursuit.
4. [AI] Create a concise message grounded in the recipient's actual page/audience and the specific value being offered. Do not fabricate familiarity, relationships, evidence, urgency, or mutual benefit.
5. [HYBRID] Check material factual claims, brand fit, personalization, duplicate contact, communication rules, disclosure obligations, and opt-out history. The active host may draft, send, or otherwise execute only as allowed by the user's request and real external constraints; AURA does not add a separate approval lifecycle.
6. [HYBRID] If a reply arrives, interpret the actual response—such as interest, question, condition, paid offer, rejection, opt-out, wrong contact, or ambiguity—and answer from established evidence. Evaluate requested terms against editorial legitimacy, disclosure requirements, business value, material cost, and reputational risk.
7. [AI] Agree, counter, decline, ask for clarification, or surface material unknown/legal/commercial issues to the appropriate human judgment. Never conceal sponsorship, misrepresent a relationship, or accept a placement whose value depends on deception.
8. [HYBRID] Preserve material contact history, outcome, resulting link/mention/asset, cost or conditions, opt-out, and useful relationship context when remembering it improves future work. Follow up only when a further contact is reasonable; stop on opt-out, clear rejection, completion, or diminishing expected value.

## Verification
- The opportunity is relevant enough to justify contact rather than merely available.
- Outreach claims and personalization are grounded in real evidence.
- Paid or sponsored arrangements are handled transparently where applicable.
- Rejections and opt-outs are respected.
- Any resulting authority value is recorded as observed outcome, not assumed SEO impact.
