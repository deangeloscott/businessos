# Unlinked Brand Mention Recovery

## Purpose
Turn legitimate existing brand or entity references into accurate links when a link would improve the source for its audience.

## Business Outcome
Recover useful referral and authority value from real existing mentions without treating every mention as an entitlement to a backlink.

## Use When
Use when a third-party source accurately references the organization, product, person, research, resource, or other owned entity but lacks a useful destination link or points to the wrong one.

## Process
1. Find accessible mentions of relevant organization-owned entities and deduplicate them enough to avoid repeated outreach to the same source/context.
2. Confirm that the mention actually refers to the owned entity and that a correct destination would add reader value. Do not assume a name match is sufficient.
3. Evaluate context before outreach. Sensitive, negative, disputed, or reputationally risky mentions may call for a different response or no contact at all.
4. Identify an appropriate contact or correction channel and preserve confidence/provenance when it materially affects follow-up.
5. If outreach is requested, make a concise factual request tied to the exact page and useful destination without implying obligation, fabricated familiarity, or SEO motives as reader benefit.
6. Respect nonresponse, rejection, editorial independence, and opt-out. Preserve a resulting correction/link, meaningful relationship context, or Learning when useful later; do not treat the resulting link as proof of ranking or business impact.

## Proportional Scope
Prioritize mentions where entity certainty, source relevance, reader value, and expected business benefit are high. Do not pursue every unlinked mention merely because it exists.
