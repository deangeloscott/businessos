# Topical Coverage

## Purpose
Close meaningful information gaps that prevent a page from satisfying its intended task, without padding the page or duplicating other assets.

## Business Outcome
Make important pages more complete, useful, and trustworthy where additional coverage genuinely helps the user or business objective.

## Use When
Use when relevant demand, customer questions, owned expertise, evidence, or strong competing resources suggest the page is missing material information needed for its intended task.

## Process
1. Define the page's primary intent boundary so 'more coverage' does not turn into uncontrolled topic expansion.
2. Compare the current page with real demand questions, customer/sales/support evidence, owned expertise, important entities, and relevant competing coverage.
3. Classify missing subtopics as required for task completion, helpful but optional, irrelevant, or better served by another asset.
4. Gather the evidence, first-party facts, examples, or original inputs needed for any material additions rather than synthesizing unsupported filler.
5. Add only sections that improve task completion, trust, differentiation, qualification, or a legitimate next decision.
6. Link to a deeper dedicated asset when that produces a better experience than bloating the current page.
7. Before adding substantial overlapping coverage, check whether another owned asset already has the stronger claim to that intent and use the Cannibalization Workflow when ownership is genuinely ambiguous.

## Proportional Scope
Stop when the page can satisfy its intended task at the required quality. Broaden only when additional coverage has a reasonable chance of materially improving the user decision or changing the competitive conclusion.
