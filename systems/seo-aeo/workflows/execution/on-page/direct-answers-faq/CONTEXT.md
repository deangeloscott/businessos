# Direct Answers / FAQ

## Purpose
Answer recurring customer questions directly where doing so improves understanding, task completion, qualification, or discovery.

## Business Outcome
Make useful answers easier for people and answer/search systems to find without creating one page per phrase variation or adding FAQ material simply to target a feature.

## Use When
Use when real customer, search, support, sales, review, site-search, or answer-surface evidence shows recurring questions that the current experience does not answer well enough.

## Process
1. Gather real questions from the sources relevant to the business and preserve exact wording/surface/timing when those observations matter to later analysis.
2. Map each question to the best existing or planned destination based on intent and user journey rather than creating a separate asset for every wording variation.
3. Prioritize questions that remove material uncertainty, help a user complete a task, improve qualification, or address a real decision barrier.
4. Write the direct answer first, then add only the nuance, evidence, examples, caveats, or next steps the question actually needs.
5. Use an FAQ presentation when it improves the page for users. Do not assume FAQ markup or formatting will produce a particular search or answer feature.
6. Evaluate helpfulness through user behavior, conversion/progression, support reduction, or search/AI visibility when those signals are relevant to the objective.

## Proportional Scope
Answer the questions that materially matter to the page or journey. Avoid bloating pages with exhaustive question lists merely because more prompts can be collected.

## Verification
- Material factual answers remain grounded in business truth or reliable evidence.
- Search/answer visibility is not treated as proof that an answer is accurate or commercially valuable.
