# Media Discoverability

## Purpose
Make important image and video Assets discoverable, understandable, performant enough for their use, and correctly connected to the pages and customer needs they support.

## Business Outcome
Improve valuable image/video discovery and page usefulness without optimizing media metadata or compression in ways that degrade meaning, quality, trust, or conversion.

## Run When
Use when image/video Assets materially contribute to search/discovery, explanation, proof, product understanding, local discovery, or conversion and their delivery/discoverability may be limiting that value.

## Process
1. Identify the high-value media, where/how it is hosted or delivered, the surrounding page/context, the customer task it serves, and the discovery surfaces that materially matter.
2. Check crawl/accessibility, surrounding explanatory context, descriptive alt/caption text where appropriate, dimensions/aspect, file delivery, lazy loading, thumbnails/previews, and page relevance only to the extent those factors affect the job.
3. For video, verify that primary playable content, thumbnails, metadata/structured data where supported, transcript or surrounding context, and indexable landing experience accurately represent the media.
4. Optimize weight, format, delivery, and loading behavior when performance is materially limiting the experience, while preserving the fidelity required for comprehension, proof, product evaluation, or trust.
5. Keep visible descriptions, metadata, structured information, and the actual media content consistent; do not add keyword-heavy labels or markup that overstates what the Asset contains.
6. Re-check relevant image/video discovery and page/business outcomes when the intervention is material enough to justify measurement.

## Proportionate Scope
Prioritize media that carries real customer/business value or is reused across important templates. Do not optimize every decorative Asset or generate extensive metadata for media unlikely to affect discovery or decisions.

## Verification
- Descriptions and structured information accurately represent the media.
- Performance optimization does not materially degrade the quality needed for the media’s job.
- Discovery metrics remain distinct from page engagement, conversion, and business value.
