# Local Media Optimization

## Purpose
Maintain accurate, useful visual assets for real locations, services, and local customer decisions.

## Business Outcome
Improve local trust, understanding, and discovery with authentic media rather than decorative volume or fabricated representations of a real place.

## Use When
Use when a location/profile/page lacks important visual evidence, existing media is stale or misleading, or local customers would materially benefit from clearer visual context.

## Process
1. Inventory relevant photos and videos by location, subject, freshness, rights, quality, and where they are currently used.
2. Identify customer-useful gaps such as exterior/entrance, interior, team, products/services, accessibility, parking, amenities, work examples, or other decision-relevant views.
3. Use authorized real assets. Do not fabricate documentary imagery of an actual location, employee, completed work, product availability, or customer experience.
4. Prepare accurate filenames, alt text, captions, context, and platform-supported metadata only where they improve accessibility, interpretation, or maintainability.
5. If publication is requested and the host has the real channel access, publish to the correct location/profile/page and verify the association. Otherwise prepare the asset/package without claiming publication.
6. Revisit stale, incorrect, or problematic owner/user media when it materially affects customer understanding or reputation. AURA may preserve monitoring intent; the host owns recurrence.

## Proportional Scope
Prioritize media that answers important local customer questions or corrects material gaps. Do not produce or upload media merely to maximize profile completeness.
