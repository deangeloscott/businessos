# Funding & Impact

Secure the money and support your work needs, and show honestly what it achieves.

## Common jobs
- Build or refresh the reusable case for support and grant-ready facts → `funding.case.case-for-support`
- Find grants worth applying for → `funding.grants.prospecting`
- Write a grant application or letter of inquiry → `funding.grants.application`
- Manage an award: reports, deadlines, compliance and renewal → `funding.grants.award-management`
- Plan a fundraising appeal or giving campaign → `funding.supporters.appeal`
- Keep donors giving and deepen relationships → `funding.supporters.stewardship`
- Recruit and keep volunteers → `funding.supporters.volunteers`
- Decide what to measure and set up outcome tracking → `funding.impact.measurement`
- Write an impact or annual report → `funding.impact.report`
- Raise investment → `funding.investment.raise`

## Boundary
Funding & Impact owns fundraising and impact operating knowledge, not accounting, legal or tax advice, donor or grant software, or payments. Reuse Content, Marketing, Customer and Industry knowledge directly where a task needs it.

## AI execution
Do not load this entire system. Retrieve the smallest relevant context and choose, combine, adapt, skip or replace methods with model and user judgment. `process-map.json` is a navigation index, not an execution order.
