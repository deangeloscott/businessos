# Funding & Impact Defaults

## Knowledge Scope
Funding & Impact provides operating knowledge for securing grants, donations, membership support, volunteer time and investment, and for measuring and reporting impact. Persuasive writing, content production, customer journeys and research methods are distinct problem areas whose operating knowledge the active model may use directly.

## Fit Before Effort
- Pursue funders, supporters and investors whose actual priorities, past giving and eligibility match the organization. Misfit is the most common and most preventable reason funding requests fail.
- A funder's past awards (for example in public filings or award databases) show what it really funds; weigh them alongside its stated priorities.
- Estimate effort against likely value and odds before committing to a long application or campaign.

## Evidence and Honesty
- Every statistic, outcome, testimonial and claim must trace to the organization's own evidence or a cited source. Mark estimates as estimates.
- Distinguish outputs from outcomes, and contribution from attribution. Present what the organization can support, and name what it is still learning.
- Never invent urgency, beneficiaries, matching gifts, endorsements or results.

## Reuse and Learning
- Draw on and update the organization's case for support, BusinessClaims and ProofRecords instead of rewriting facts for each request; tailor them to each audience rather than recycling generic text.
- Record decisions, awards, declines, gifts and feedback as they happen. Preserve Learning only when the evidence supports a reusable lesson (for example, why applications to a type of funder succeed or fail).

## People and Privacy
- Supporters, volunteers and beneficiaries are people, not segments to exploit. Use stories only with recorded permission and respect communication preferences.
- Keep personal and financial details of individuals out of shared knowledge, collections that may be shared, and Exchange packages.

## Standalone Mode
When other AURA areas are absent, use the organization's saved context, public research and the person's own records. Keep unverified facts provisional rather than presenting them as established.
