# Donor Stewardship and Retention

## Purpose
Keep donors giving and deepen their support by thanking them well, showing what their gifts did, and building relationships appropriate to each donor.

## Business Outcome
Higher donor retention, more second gifts, more monthly and major donors, and fewer lapsed supporters.

## Run When
Use to set up or improve how the organization thanks and keeps donors, to win back lapsed donors, or to cultivate donors who could give more. For the ask itself, use Fundraising Appeal.

## Process
1. [AI] Establish the current picture from the person's donor records where available: how many donors give again, how many first-time donors return, average gift, monthly donors, lapsed donors, and how quickly gifts are thanked. Unknown numbers stay unknown.
2. [AI] Focus first on the biggest leak. First-time donors are usually the least likely to give again, and retention rises sharply with each additional gift, so the weeks after a first gift matter most.
3. [AI] Design the stewardship journey: a prompt, personal thank-you (including any gift receipt or acknowledgment donors need for tax purposes under local rules); a welcome for new donors; reports back that show what gifts achieved (using real outcomes and permitted stories); recognition that fits the donor; and an invitation to deepen involvement (monthly giving, volunteering, events) at a respectful pace.
4. [HYBRID] Identify donors who warrant personal attention (larger, frequent, long-standing or lapsing) and plan individual cultivation with the person, without pressure or invasive research.
5. [AI] Plan lapsed-donor reactivation with an honest update and a specific, modest ask.
6. [DETERMINISTIC] Track retention, second-gift rate and monthly donors as measurements over time, linked to any related goal, and preserve Learning when a change clearly helps.

## Proportionate Scope
A small organization might start with a same-week thank-you, a welcome email and one impact update a year. Larger programs can add segmentation and personal cultivation.

## Verification
- Every donor is thanked promptly and hears what their support achieved.
- Plans use real numbers where available and say when numbers are unknown.
- Donor privacy and communication preferences are respected.
