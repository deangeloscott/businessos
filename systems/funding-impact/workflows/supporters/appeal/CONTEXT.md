# Fundraising Appeal

## Purpose
Plan and write a fundraising appeal or giving campaign (year-end, giving day, emergency, membership drive or project campaign) that moves supporters to give.

## Business Outcome
More gifts and more donors from a clear, honest, donor-centered ask, with results recorded so the next appeal performs better.

## Run When
Use for a planned appeal or campaign. For thanking and keeping existing donors, use Donor Stewardship; for grant funders, use the grant workflows.

## Process
1. [AI] Define the goal (amount, number of donors, monthly sign-ups), the audience and their history with the organization, the timing and channels (mail, email, social, text, events, phone) available.
2. [AI] Choose one clear need and what a gift makes possible, from the organization's real programs and evidence. A specific, credible picture of impact beats a list of everything the organization does.
3. [AI] Write donor-centered copy: the donor as the one who makes the difference ("you" more than "we"), one true story used with permission, the specific need and what a gift will do, an explicit ask with suggested amounts where appropriate, and an easy way to give. Use the Content and Marketing knowledge areas for craft and channel-specific formats.
4. [HYBRID] Segment where it matters: first-time versus repeat donors, lapsed donors, monthly donors, volunteers and major donors deserve different wording and asks. Respect communication preferences.
5. [AI] Plan the sequence across channels and reminders, and the thank-you that follows each gift (hand off to Donor Stewardship).
6. [HYBRID] Hand over the send-ready appeal in the requested channel, including the giving link or a marked place for the person to supply it. Reject invented urgency, fake matching gifts, guilt or exaggerated need. Every claim must be true.
7. [DETERMINISTIC] After the campaign, record results by segment and channel (response, average gift, new and returning donors) and preserve Learning when supported.

## Proportionate Scope
A small organization may need one email and one social post. A year-end campaign may need a letter, emails, posts and follow-ups. Match effort to the goal and audience size.

## Verification
- One clear need and one explicit ask; claims and stories are true and permitted.
- Messages fit each segment and channel.
- Results are recorded for the next appeal.
