# Volunteer Recruitment and Retention

## Purpose
Recruit the volunteers the organization needs, welcome them well, and keep them coming back.

## Business Outcome
Enough reliable, engaged volunteers for the work, with fewer people drifting away after their first few shifts.

## Run When
Use when the organization needs more volunteers, loses volunteers quickly, or is setting up or improving its volunteer program.

## Process
1. [AI] Define the roles needed: tasks, skills, time commitment, schedule, location, and what volunteers gain. For recruitment, deliver a publishable role description with a clear way to sign up.
2. [AI] Plan recruitment where suitable people already are: existing supporters, clients' communities, local groups, schools, employers, faith communities and volunteer platforms.
3. [AI] Design a welcome that makes the first experience go well: easy sign-up, a clear first shift, a first-shift message naming the meeting place, contact and what to bring, someone to greet them, enough training to feel capable, and a follow-up after the first shift. Many volunteers stop after early experiences where nobody followed up or their time felt wasted.
4. [AI] Plan retention around what keeps volunteers: feeling capable, feeling part of the group, being kept informed, being thanked and recognized, and seeing the difference their work makes.
5. [HYBRID] Respect safety, screening and legal requirements that apply to the roles, such as work with children or vulnerable people; refer specifics to the organization's policies and qualified advisers.
6. [DETERMINISTIC] Track volunteers recruited, first-shift return, active volunteers and hours over time, and preserve Learning when a change clearly helps.

## Proportionate Scope
A small organization may need one role description, a sign-up form and a welcome routine. A larger program may add roles, schedules, training and recognition events.

## Verification
- Roles are clear and honest about the commitment.
- New volunteers are welcomed and followed up after their first shift.
- Screening and safety requirements are respected.
