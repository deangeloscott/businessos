# Case for Support and Grant-Ready Facts

## Purpose
Build and keep current the organization's reusable case for support and the verified facts every grant application, appeal, report and pitch draws on.

## Business Outcome
Each new funding request starts from accurate, current, evidence-backed material and is tailored to its audience instead of rewritten from scratch or recycled as generic text.

## Run When
Use before a first grant application or major appeal, when core facts are scattered or stale, or after a significant change in programs, results, leadership or finances. Skip when a current case exists and the task only needs tailoring.

## Process
1. [AI] Gather what the organization already has: saved Business context, BusinessClaims, ProofRecords, measurements, past applications, reports and website text.
2. [AI] Assemble the reusable facts funders and supporters commonly ask for: legal name and status, mission and vision, history, programs and who they serve, geography, the need addressed with sourced data, outputs and outcomes with dates, leadership and governance, staff and volunteers, budget size and funding mix, partners, and notable past funding.
3. [HYBRID] Confirm each fact's source and date with the person. Save stable facts as BusinessClaims with their authority and evidence, and outcome evidence or stories as ProofRecords with permission status. Mark gaps and estimates plainly.
4. [AI] Write the case for support: the need, the organization's distinctive approach and why it works, evidence of results, what support makes possible and what would be lost without it, and a clear invitation. Keep the core under a few pages so it can be adapted.
5. [AI] Prepare short reusable versions (one paragraph, one page, and the common application answer lengths) derived from the same facts.
6. [DETERMINISTIC] Record when facts and the case were last confirmed so future work can tell when they need review.

## Proportionate Scope
A new or small organization may need one page of verified facts and a short case. Expand detail when the organization pursues larger or more varied funding.

## Verification
- Every number, outcome and claim traces to a source or is marked as an estimate.
- Outputs and outcomes are distinguished; stories have recorded permission.
- Facts are saved where future applications and reports will find them, with a confirmation date.
