# Funding & Impact

## Purpose
Help an organization secure the money its work needs (grants, donations, membership support, volunteer time and investment) and show honestly what that work achieves.

## Canonical Scope
The organization's case for support and reusable fundraising facts, grant prospecting, applications and award management, supporter appeals and stewardship, volunteer recruitment and retention, impact measurement and reporting, and investor fundraising.

## Explicit Non-Scope
Does not own accounting, legal or tax advice, donor or grant management software, payment processing, or securities compliance. Customer journeys, persuasion craft, content production and research methods belong to their own areas; reuse their operating knowledge directly when a funding task needs it.

## Shared behavior
Reuse saved organizational truth before asking again: mission, programs, people served, outcomes, finances, leadership and past funding. Save durable facts once as BusinessClaims and evidence as ProofRecords, so every application, appeal and report draws on the same verified material and improves it.

Every number, outcome and story in funding material must trace to evidence the organization actually has. Keep outputs (activities delivered) distinct from outcomes (change achieved), and never inflate either. Personal stories of beneficiaries, donors and volunteers need recorded permission; keep personal details out of shared knowledge and Exchange packages.

Funders, donors and investors differ by type, field and place. Treat general patterns as starting points and confirm the actual requirements, cycles and preferences of the specific funder or supporter. Deadlines and eligibility rules change; verify them close to submission.

The active model and harness choose tools: web research, public filings and databases, grant platforms, donor CRMs and connectors are all optional sources. Real submission, payment, messaging and scheduling stay with the person and their systems.
