# Customer Intervention Defaults

- Establish baseline transition rate/time-to-value or other relevant journey metric before intervention when measurement is feasible.
- State the causal mechanism: what friction is being changed, why that should alter progression, and which metric should move first.
- Define customer/business guardrails and rollback/stopping conditions before broad or difficult-to-reverse changes.
- If the intervention primarily requires persuasion or communication, keep the Opportunity here only when the underlying problem is journey progression and delegate Marketing/Content work through WorkRequest.
