# Customer Optimization

Improve how customers progress from interest through purchase, onboarding, success, retention, repeat, expansion, referral, and recovery while protecting real customer value.

## Common jobs
- Map or understand a customer journey → `workflows/journey/`
- Instrument important transitions or customer-value signals → `workflows/instrumentation/`
- Diagnose friction, dropoff, churn, or another root cause → `workflows/diagnosis/`
- Improve conversion, checkout, onboarding, adoption, retention, expansion, referral, or another customer transition → the relevant specialist family under `workflows/`
- Design or carry out a cross-step intervention → `workflows/intervention/`
- Improve a real sales/customer handoff → `customer-optimization.intervention.sales-handoff`
- Bounded journey or churn monitoring → `workflows/monitoring/`
- Measure transitions, time-to-value, customer value, and business outcomes → `workflows/measurement/`
- Test uncertain improvements → `workflows/experimentation/`
- Reusable Customer Optimization Learning → `workflows/learning/`

## Boundary
Customer Optimization owns journey, progression, and value-realization operating knowledge, not customer psychology truth, generic persuasion, or the external CRM/product/billing runtime. Reuse Customer Intelligence, Marketing, Offer, product/service, and measurement evidence directly where relevant.

A WorkRequest is not needed to move reasoning between AURA areas. Use one only for a real durable organizational handoff across actors, sessions, or time. The harness and external systems own workflow execution, customer messaging, scheduling, account permissions, and operational automation.

## AI execution
Do not load this entire system. Retrieve the smallest relevant context and choose, combine, adapt, skip, or replace useful methods with model/user judgment. `process-map.json` is a lightweight browse/navigation index, not runtime execution order or a Workflow graph.
