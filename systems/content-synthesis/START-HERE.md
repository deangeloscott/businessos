# Content Synthesis

Turn useful ideas, evidence, requirements, and organizational context into communication that fits the audience, medium, platform, and consumption context.

## Common jobs
- Find or understand strong and emerging content mechanisms → `workflows/intelligence/` and `workflows/research/`
- Build a reusable communication brief when useful → `content.intake.content-brief`
- Choose platform/format or broader content strategy → `workflows/strategy/`
- Adapt one idea natively across contexts → `workflows/adaptation/`
- Produce article, video, carousel, infographic, GIF, image, podcast, avatar video, presentation, audio, or other Assets → `workflows/production/`
- Create useful derivative scripts, clips, captions, thumbnails, or supporting forms → `content.production.derivative-package`
- Render or assemble media with relevant host capabilities → `workflows/rendering/`
- QA, fact, rights, brand, and editorial review → `workflows/qa/`
- Publish when explicitly requested and the active harness has the real capability/permission → `workflows/publishing/`
- Evaluate performance and preserve reusable Content Learning → `workflows/measurement/` and `workflows/learning/`

## Boundary
Content Intelligence studies communication and creative mechanisms. Customer, competitor, industry, SEO, Offer, Brand, marketing, and journey truth remain reusable organizational context rather than instructions from separate internal controllers.

Content may directly consume relevant Insights, ProofRecords, SEO requirements, persuasion requirements, and prior Assets. It does not require an AURA WorkRequest merely because another area supplied context. Use a WorkRequest only for a real durable organizational handoff across actors, sessions, or time.

The active model/user chooses, combines, adapts, skips, or replaces useful methods. The harness owns rendering, platform APIs, scheduling, publishing mechanics, retries, and permissions.

## AI execution
Do not load this entire system. Retrieve the smallest relevant context and use the knowledge that improves the actual communication job. `process-map.json` is a browse/composition aid, not runtime execution order.
