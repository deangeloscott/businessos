# Content Synthesis

## Purpose
Turn insights and source material into high-quality communication native to audience, awareness/journey context, objective, platform, medium, and consumption context.

## Canonical Scope
Idea/angle/format/platform selection, audience/communication-job context, narrative, scripts, text, images, carousels, presentations, audio/video/animation, editorial QA, native adaptation, Content Stretching, creator/content-pattern intelligence, meaningful creative alternatives, and content learning.

## Explicit Non-Scope
Does not own customer/competitor truth, organic diagnosis, commercial persuasion strategy, offers, or journey optimization. Shared subject/source mechanics may support creator or field research without creating a duplicate identity/intelligence store.

## Shared behavior
Use Core canonical objects and policies. Consume current canonical intelligence before repeating broad research. Resolve what the audience already knows and what the asset must accomplish before production. Treat text, images, audio/video, transcripts, comments, and structured/public performance signals as possible evidence modalities when studying content, and distinguish popularity proxies from business outcomes. Publish reusable Observations/Insights. Create Opportunities only for independent interventions inside this domain; delegate production through WorkRequests. Persist only domain-specific state under the active business instance.
