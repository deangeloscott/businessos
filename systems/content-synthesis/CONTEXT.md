# Content Synthesis

## Purpose
Turn useful ideas, evidence, requirements, and organizational context into high-quality communication native to the audience, awareness/journey context, objective, platform, medium, and consumption context.

## Canonical Scope
Idea/angle/format/platform selection, audience and communication-job context, narrative, scripts, text, images, carousels, presentations, audio/video/animation, editorial QA, native adaptation, Content Stretching, creator/content-pattern intelligence, meaningful creative alternatives, content measurement, and content Learning.

## Explicit Non-Scope
Does not own customer/competitor truth, organic diagnosis, commercial persuasion strategy, Offers, journey optimization, runtime rendering infrastructure, publishing permissions, or platform scheduling. Shared subject/source methods may support creator or field research without creating duplicate identity or intelligence stores.

## Shared behavior
Use Core canonical organization truth and policies. Reuse current customer, competitor, industry, SEO, marketing, Brand, proof, and prior-Asset context before repeating broad research. Resolve what the audience already knows, what the communication must accomplish, and how the medium/platform changes the experience only to the depth useful for the actual asset.

Text, images, audio/video, transcripts, comments, and public or first-party performance signals can all provide evidence when studying content. Views, retention, shares, saves, comments, repeated winning formats, and similar signals can be meaningful evidence of attention or communication performance; keep them distinct from downstream leads, revenue, or other business outcomes when those are not observed.

Use Content Playbooks and Workflows as reusable expert knowledge, not mandatory production pipelines. The active model/user may combine, adapt, skip, or replace methods and may continue directly from SEO requirements, Marketing strategy, Customer Insights, or another source of context without a WorkRequest merely because another AURA area contributed knowledge.

Persist only content-specific evidence, briefs, Assets, Insights, Learning, decisions, or continuity that future organizational work will materially benefit from. The active harness owns actual rendering, file/tool mechanics, platform APIs, publishing, scheduling, retries, and permissions.
