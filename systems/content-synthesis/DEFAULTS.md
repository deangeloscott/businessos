# Content Synthesis Defaults

## Domain Boundary
Content Synthesis owns how a validated idea should be expressed for an audience, objective, platform, medium, and consumption context. It does not redefine customer truth, competitor truth, offers, SEO diagnosis, marketing persuasion strategy, or journey ownership.

## Native Expression
- Do not treat adaptation as resize, truncate, or repost. Reconsider hook, structure, pacing, depth, visuals, language, proof, and action for each medium.
- Use a current PlatformProfile when platform mechanics materially affect the output. If the profile is missing or stale, refresh it or explicitly operate with provisional assumptions.

## Production
Preserve factual meaning and evidence lineage through every derivative Asset. Public claims remain subject to Brand, compliance, proof, and approval requirements.

## Content Intelligence
- Study trending/strong content to extract mechanisms, not to copy protected expression or assume popularity proves transferability. Separate topic, hook, structure, creative, creator advantage, distribution, and paid amplification.
- Validate suspected trends across comparable examples before turning them into reusable Learning. Prefer relative performance/context over raw vanity metrics.
- A customer/news/competitor/search signal becomes a Content Opportunity only when content is an independent valuable intervention. If another domain already owns the Opportunity and requests production, use the WorkRequest.

## Derivatives
Derivative scripts, clips, captions, thumbnails, audio, GIFs, and other variants normally remain linked Assets under the same originating Opportunity; create another Opportunity only for a genuinely separate communication intervention.
## Standalone Mode
When upstream intelligence modules are absent, use current Business Context, supplied source material, existing Core intelligence, and bounded content-specific research. Do not invent Customer, Competitor, Industry, SEO, Marketing, or Customer Optimization Insights on their behalf.
