# Content Synthesis Defaults

## Knowledge Scope
Content Synthesis provides operating knowledge for expressing a supported idea for an audience, objective, platform, medium, and consumption context. It should not silently redefine customer truth, competitor truth, Offer truth, SEO diagnosis, marketing persuasion strategy, or journey state; the active model can use those other bodies of operating knowledge directly when the job needs them.

## Audience and Communication Job
- Before meaningful production, resolve what the audience already knows, the asset's communication job, likely funnel/journey role, desired next action, and the amount of context the medium must carry.
- TOF/MOF/BOF is a useful shorthand when relevant, not a rigid taxonomy: the same format or placement may need very different hooks, depth, proof, and CTAs depending on whether it is earning attention, building understanding/trust, or supporting a decision.
- Use evidence-backed customer motivations/objections when available. Content may use a labeled provisional hypothesis when needed but must not invent customer psychology as fact.

## Native Expression
- Do not treat adaptation as resize, truncate, or repost. Reconsider hook, structure, pacing, depth, visuals, language, proof, and action for each medium.
- Use a current PlatformProfile when platform mechanics materially affect the output. If the profile is missing or stale, refresh it or explicitly operate with provisional assumptions.

## Production
Preserve factual meaning and evidence lineage through every derivative Asset. Public claims must stay within established Brand/business truth, applicable proof, and actual legal/platform/organizational constraints.
- When creative choice would materially improve the result, produce up to three genuinely distinct options/territories (for example different hook, motivation, structure, or visual mechanism) rather than near-duplicate rewrites. Recommend the strongest when evidence supports a recommendation; do not manufacture variants when one answer is clearly sufficient.

## Content Intelligence
- Study trending/strong content to extract mechanisms, not to copy protected expression or assume popularity proves transferability. Separate topic, hook, structure, creative, creator advantage, distribution, and paid amplification.
- Reuse shared SourceProfiles/subject watches and multimodal evidence rather than rebuilding creator/source identity from scratch. Inspect transcript, visual/audio execution, thumbnail/frame, post/comment, or other modality when it materially carries the mechanism; record limitations when only a fallback representation was available.
- Validate suspected trends across comparable examples before turning them into reusable Learning. Prefer relative performance/context over raw vanity metrics.
- A customer/news/competitor/search signal warrants a separate Content Opportunity only when content itself is a distinct durable intervention worth remembering. Otherwise the active model may use the evidence directly while producing the requested content. A `WorkRequest` is only for a real durable handoff between people/models/sessions/teams, never an internal domain-to-domain request.

## Content Stretching
- When the job is to extend one useful source idea across formats/channels, preserve the core meaning while creating native derivatives for their distinct audiences/contexts. Long-to-short is common but not mandatory; reverse/expansion paths are valid when evidence and the communication job support them.
- Keep derivatives linked to the originating evidence/Insight/Opportunity when that lineage materially helps the organization learn which transformation and context actually worked.

## Derivatives
Derivative scripts, clips, captions, thumbnails, audio, GIFs, and other variants normally remain linked Assets under the same originating organizational work when such linkage is useful; create another Opportunity only for a genuinely separate durable intervention.
## Standalone Mode
When optional upstream intelligence modules are absent, use current Business Context, supplied source material, existing Core intelligence, and bounded content-specific research. Preserve direct evidence and qualified interpretation truthfully rather than inventing unavailable canonical conclusions.
