# Content Production Defaults

- Confirm the source Insight/Opportunity, audience, objective, platform/medium, desired action, proof requirements, and current PlatformProfile before production.
- The production packet must be executable: content/script plus media/visual instructions, technical/render requirements, required source/proof links, and acceptance criteria appropriate to the format.
- Final QA checks factual meaning, Brand, accessibility/usability where relevant, platform integrity, and rendered output—not only the draft text.
