# Content Production Defaults

- Confirm the source Insight/Opportunity, audience, objective, platform/medium, desired action, proof requirements, and current PlatformProfile before production.
- Select an execution method after capability preflight. A proven specialist Skill/tool may be used when it improves the result under current cost, authorization, and constraints; otherwise the native process remains responsible for the complete deliverable. Missing optional tooling never lowers the content standard.
- Produce the promised medium when a compatible renderer/generator is available. When the contract permits graceful degradation and rendering is unavailable, create a production-ready fallback that contains the same substantive audience content plus the direction another professional needs to render/record it. A summary, outline, keyword checklist, or description of future work is not the deliverable.
- The production packet must be executable: complete content/script, medium-native units and sequence, media/visual/audio instructions, numeric timing or dimensions where relevant, technical/render requirements, resolvable source/proof pointers, CTA/next step, packaging/metadata, and acceptance criteria appropriate to the format. Do not cite an internal standard, benchmark, dataset, or public source unless it exists in loaded/captured evidence.
- Inspect the actual audience-facing artifact, not only its supporting metadata. Check continuity from promise through proof and action, and remove internal contract, Run, qualification, or implementation language from the deliverable.
- Final QA checks factual meaning and claims, Brand, editorial quality, originality, accessibility/usability, platform integrity, and the rendered or fallback output. Record concrete findings/corrections against the exact Asset version rather than asserting that QA occurred.

## Medium-specific native floor

- Article deliverables contain finished reader-facing copy at useful depth, not a brief introduction or expanded outline. The structural completion floor is 500 words; the contract may require more whenever the reader task cannot be fulfilled honestly at that length.
- Audio/podcast fallbacks include the episode premise and listener promise, audience/listening context, title, complete spoken script or genuinely sufficient host notes, at least four concrete timecodes across multiple ordered segments, explicit transitions/audio cues, evidence/source/pronunciation notes, CTA, edit direction, and show notes/platform packaging.
- Presentation fallbacks include the audience/setting/objective and numeric duration, an actual slide-by-slide narrative with at least six complete slides, substantive visible copy/data, per-slide visual direction, speaker notes, source/proof attribution, CTA/decision close, and presentation-level QA. A list of proposed slide topics is only an outline.
- Video/animation/demo fallbacks include the complete audience message plus scene/shot/state sequence, visuals, audio/narration, timing, transitions/interactions, platform specifications, and edit/render notes.
- Image/carousel/infographic fallbacks include final copy/data plus frame/canvas sequence, layout hierarchy, visual direction, dimensions/safe areas, accessibility text, source attribution, and export instructions.
