# Content Domain Learning

## Purpose
Turn repeated content outcomes into scoped reusable guidance about format, angle, structure, depth, pacing, creative, production, and platform-native communication.

## Business Outcome
Help future content communicate more effectively for the intended audience, objective, platform, and consumption context without mistaking attention metrics for universal creative rules.

## Run When
Use when enough comparable content outcomes, corrections, failures, or repeated production work exists to support a reusable pattern. Do not run merely because a periodic learning cycle is due.

## Process
1. [AI] Compare results within genuinely comparable platform/audience/objective/format/distribution contexts rather than one engagement leaderboard.
2. [HYBRID] Account for distribution, account growth, trend/timing, paid amplification, topic demand, audience mix, and other confounders before attributing effect to format or creative choices.
3. [AI] Identify repeatable communication/production mechanisms, failure modes, and conditions where they do or do not appear useful.
4. [AI] Keep communication Learning distinct from Marketing persuasion claims and Customer truth unless the evidence genuinely supports those broader interpretations.
5. [AI] State applicability, negative cases, uncertainty, evidence refs, and what future outcomes would revise the lesson.
6. [HYBRID] Create/update Content Learning only when it materially improves future work. Deterministic helpers validate/persist the chosen Learning; semantic maturity/applicability remain model/user judgments.

## Verification
- Popularity/engagement alone does not establish business effectiveness.
- Platform/audience/context differences are preserved.
- Learning does not become a fixed creative rule, scheduler, or automatic production directive.

## Completion Criteria
- Future content work can reuse a conditional evidence-backed communication lesson without flattening creative judgment or other domain truths.
