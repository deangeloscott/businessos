# Content Ecosystem Radar

## Purpose
Discover emerging content formats, distribution behaviors, creator patterns, platform mechanics, and communication tactics, then determine what mechanisms are actually supported and transferable.

## Business Outcome
Help the business adapt content earlier and more intelligently without copying creators, chasing every trend, or treating every attention signal as proven downstream business impact.

## Run When
Use on demand for content intelligence or when platform/creator/content behavior may have materially changed.

## Process
1. [HYBRID] Reuse existing trend scans, creator monitoring, content-performance evidence, Domain Learning, and SourceProfiles before searching again.
2. [AI] Discover the formats, hooks, structures, pacing, visual/audio patterns, distribution mechanics, creator behaviors, topic packaging, or cross-niche mechanisms that could materially inform the current content decision. Draw on Core source-discovery knowledge when it improves coverage or provenance.
3. [HYBRID] Preserve representative source evidence and use Core triangulation plus trend validation when useful to distinguish one viral item, repeated imitation, sustained multi-source adoption, platform change, and measured owned-content response. Do not require ceremonial triangulation when the evidence needed for the decision is already sufficient.
4. [AI] Separate transferable mechanism from protected expression or creator identity. Creative-pattern extraction may help when the abstraction matters; do not turn imitation into a routed tactic.
5. [AI] Evaluate freshness and applicability by platform, audience, account state, Objective, format, production context, and distribution conditions. Views, shares, watch behavior, reach, engagement, and repeated winning patterns can be meaningful evidence of attention, exposure, or distribution opportunity; distinguish those leading outcomes from qualified action, revenue, profit, or causal business impact when those downstream outcomes have not been observed.
6. [AI] Decide what the evidence warrants next: ignore, watch, investigate, try a bounded owned-content hypothesis, use the mechanism in production, revise Learning, or do nothing. Relevant Content Playbooks/Workflows are optional methods, not automatic destinations.
7. [AI] Where owned outcomes exist, compare them against distribution, trend/timing, paid amplification, topic demand, account growth, and plausible alternatives before attributing effect to format/creative.
8. [DETERMINISTIC] Persist only material Observation/Insight evidence and exact references selected by the model/user. Content Learning changes only when semantic judgment and outcome evidence support reusable scoped guidance.

## Verification
- Popularity, novelty, attention/distribution opportunity, mechanism plausibility, and downstream business effectiveness remain distinguishable without dismissing useful leading signals.
- Pattern transfer never authorizes copying source expression.
- No Opportunity/WorkRequest or production route is created merely because a trend/mechanism was observed.

## Completion Criteria
- Material content discoveries have traceable evidence, a scoped mechanism/applicability interpretation, and any suggested next method remains model/user judgment rather than AURA routing state.
