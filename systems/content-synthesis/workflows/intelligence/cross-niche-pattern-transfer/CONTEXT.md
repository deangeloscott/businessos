# Cross-Niche Creative Pattern Transfer

## Purpose
Adapt a validated creative mechanism from another niche or category to the business's own audience and subject without copying the original content.

## Business Outcome
Expand the business's creative repertoire with proven or promising structures that competitors may not yet be using while preserving originality and audience relevance.

## Run When
Run when a Content Insight/Learning from an adjacent or dissimilar niche appears potentially transferable to an active audience, Objective, or content need.

## Process
1. [AI] State the source pattern at the mechanism level and identify which parts are universal versus dependent on the source niche, creator, product, or platform.
2. [AI] Map the mechanism to the target audience's real question, tension, desired outcome, proof needs, sophistication, and platform consumption context.
3. [HYBRID] Reject transfers that require false equivalence, unsupported claims, irrelevant emotional triggers, copied language/visual identity, or a weak audience mechanism.
4. [AI] Generate at least two original manifestations using the business's own evidence, examples, ProofRecords, products, and language rather than substituting nouns into the source content.
5. [HYBRID] Evaluate platform fit, brand fit, expected audience value, differentiation, and whether the pattern is better treated as an experiment than a standard.
6. [DETERMINISTIC] Search existing Content Opportunities before creating a new one; attach the pattern to existing work when it serves the same intervention.
7. [HYBRID] Publish a scoped Content Insight and create a candidate Content Opportunity only when the transfer represents an independent valuable communication intervention.
