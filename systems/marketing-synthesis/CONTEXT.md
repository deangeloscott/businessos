# Marketing Synthesis

## Purpose
Turn intelligence, offers, proof, positioning, objections, awareness, and performance into persuasive commercial assets that move the right audience toward valuable action.

## Canonical Scope
Positioning/message synthesis, value proposition, proof, objections, offer presentation, campaigns, ads, landing pages, VSLs, webinars, email, quizzes, lead magnets, sales enablement.

## Explicit Non-Scope
Does not own customer research, competitor research, organic discovery, editorial content strategy generally, customer journey mechanics, media buying, or CRM operations.

## Shared behavior
Use Core canonical objects and policies. Consume current canonical intelligence before repeating broad research. Publish reusable Observations/Insights. Create Opportunities only for independent interventions inside this domain; delegate production through WorkRequests. Persist only domain-specific state under the active business instance.
