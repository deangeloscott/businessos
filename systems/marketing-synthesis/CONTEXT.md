# Marketing Synthesis

## Purpose
Turn current intelligence, Offers, proof, positioning, objections, awareness, decision context, and performance into persuasive commercial strategy and assets that move the right audience toward valuable action.

## Canonical Scope
Persuasion diagnosis, positioning/message synthesis, value proposition, proof, objections, Offer presentation/diagnosis, awareness/funnel-specific messaging, evidence-backed motivation/decision mechanisms, campaigns, ads, landing pages, VSLs, webinars, email, quizzes, lead magnets, sales enablement, creative territories/variants, and measurement-ready commercial work.

## Explicit Non-Scope
Does not own customer research, competitor research, organic discovery, editorial content strategy generally, customer journey mechanics, media buying, CRM operations, runtime scheduling, or authorization. Organization marketing doctrine/preferences guide valid choices but do not create facts, claims, guarantees, scarcity, urgency, or permission.

## Shared behavior
Use Core canonical organization truth and policies. Reuse current customer, competitor, business, proof, Offer, and performance evidence before repeating broad research. Apply sound marketing judgment, then incorporate Brand/operator doctrine where useful.

Resolve audience awareness/knowledge state, funnel or journey role, desired action, objections, proof/risk needs, Offer/value exchange, acquisition context, and message continuity to the depth needed for the actual persuasion job. When useful, create genuinely distinct strategic options rather than cosmetic variants.

Use Marketing Playbooks and Workflows as reusable expert knowledge, not mandatory routes. The active model/user may continue directly into content creation, customer work, experiments, publishing, or another relevant method without a WorkRequest or duplicate Opportunity merely because the work crosses an AURA knowledge boundary.

Persist only domain-specific evidence, briefs, Assets, decisions, Learning, or continuity that future organizational work will materially benefit from.
