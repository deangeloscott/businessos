# Marketing Synthesis

## Purpose
Turn intelligence, offers, proof, positioning, objections, awareness, decision context, and performance into persuasive commercial systems/assets that move the right audience toward valuable action.

## Canonical Scope
Persuasion diagnosis, positioning/message synthesis, value proposition, proof, objections, offer presentation/diagnosis, awareness/funnel-specific messaging, evidence-backed motivation/decision mechanisms, campaigns, ads, landing pages, VSLs, webinars, email, quizzes, lead magnets, sales enablement, creative territories/variants, and measurement-ready commercial execution packages.

## Explicit Non-Scope
Does not own customer research, competitor research, organic discovery, editorial content strategy generally, customer journey mechanics, media buying, or CRM operations. Organization marketing doctrine/preferences guide valid choices but do not create facts, claims, guarantees, scarcity/urgency, or authorization.

## Shared behavior
Use Core canonical objects and policies. Consume current canonical intelligence before repeating broad research. Apply baseline sound marketing judgment, then incorporate governed Brand/operator doctrine where applicable. Resolve audience awareness/knowledge state, funnel/journey role, desired action, objections, proof/risk needs, Offer/value exchange, acquisition context, and message continuity before synthesis. When useful, create up to three genuinely distinct strategic options rather than cosmetic variants. Publish reusable Observations/Insights. Create Opportunities only for independent interventions inside this domain; delegate production through WorkRequests. Persist only domain-specific state under the active business instance.
