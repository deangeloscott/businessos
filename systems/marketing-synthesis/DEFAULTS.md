# Marketing Synthesis Defaults

## Domain Boundary
Marketing Synthesis owns commercial persuasion: positioning expression, messaging, proof architecture, objection handling, offer presentation, CTA, campaign concepts, and conversion assets. It does not own customer truth, competitor truth, actual Offer terms, journey mechanics, media buying, or general content craft.

## Persuasion Context
- Start from the desired commercial action, acquisition/placement context, audience, awareness/knowledge state, funnel/journey role, decision criteria, objections, proof/risk needs, Offer, and message continuity.
- Treat awareness and funnel role as related but distinct. A cold/top-of-funnel asset, a problem/solution-aware middle-of-funnel asset, and a product/most-aware bottom-of-funnel asset may use the same medium while requiring different hooks, proof, depth, and next actions.
- Use evidence-backed motivation/decision mechanisms when useful: desired gain, loss avoidance, certainty/risk reduction, control, speed, simplicity, effort reduction, financial outcome, status/identity, autonomy, belonging, convenience, or other supported drivers. These are lenses, not a mandatory taxonomy or permission to profile people.
- Diagnose whether the limiting mechanism is persuasion before creating a Marketing Opportunity; route process/checkout/onboarding friction to Customer Optimization.
- Public claims require support from canonical business facts or traceable evidence.

## Marketing Doctrine and Baseline Excellence
- Apply sound general marketing judgment even when the organization has not supplied a named framework. AURA should pursue clarity, relevance, credible differentiation, proof discipline, appropriate friction reduction, message match, and truthful offers as baseline quality.
- Organization/operator doctrine may prefer approaches or frameworks (for example direct response, brand-led marketing, particular offer/value lenses, or named practitioner methods). Resolve those through governed Brand/PreferenceProfile/BusinessClaim context according to their meaning; do not hard-code one school of marketing as universal truth.
- Preferences guide valid choices but never establish product capabilities, customer psychology, guarantees, discounts, scarcity, urgency, proof, or authorization.

## Offer and Value Reasoning
- Evaluate the desired customer outcome, confidence/likelihood the buyer can reasonably perceive, time-to-value/delay, and customer effort/sacrifice as useful diagnostic dimensions when the evidence makes them relevant. This is a reasoning lens, not a literal value equation or requirement to inflate a bundle.
- Bonuses, guarantees/risk reversal, naming, payment terms, scarcity, and urgency are legitimate only when they solve a real decision barrier and are operationally true, supportable, and authorized. Never manufacture countdowns, capacity limits, outcomes, testimonials, refund promises, or other enhancers to make an offer look stronger.

## Creative Choice
- When meaningful creative uncertainty exists, create up to three genuinely distinct strategic options/territories rather than near-duplicate rewrites. Distinction may come from motivation, problem/outcome framing, mechanism, proof, structure, visual system, or funnel role.
- Recommend a strongest option when evidence supports one, but preserve legitimate user choice. Do not create variants merely to hit a count.

## Research
- Reuse current Customer, Competitor, Industry, SEO, Content, shared SourceProfile intelligence, and Business Learning before repeating broad upstream research.
- When current field evidence matters, study comparable ads/content/landing paths/offers to extract mechanisms and calibrated performance proxies; do not treat visibility, engagement, longevity, or volume as proof of profitability or transferability.
- Marketing does not build a duplicate crawler, customer-research database, creator identity system, or competitor monitor. Use shared evidence and upstream semantic owners, then apply persuasion judgment.

## Standalone Mode
When Customer, Competitor, Industry, SEO, Content, or Customer Optimization modules are absent, use current Business Context, supplied evidence, existing Core objects, and bounded research sufficient for the persuasion decision. Keep foreign-domain interpretation provisional and do not create canonical Insights for an uninstalled owner.
