# Marketing Domain Learning

## Purpose
Turn repeated commercial-persuasion outcomes into scoped reusable guidance about positioning, message, proof, offer presentation, objection handling, creative concepts, and persuasion structure.

## Business Outcome
Help future marketing make better evidence-based persuasion choices for the relevant audience, awareness, offer, proof, and acquisition context.

## Run When
Use when enough comparable OutcomeEvaluations, corrections, failures, or repeated marketing work exists to support a reusable persuasion pattern. Time passing alone does not trigger a learning cycle.

## Process
1. [AI] Group genuinely comparable treatments by audience, awareness/stage, offer, channel/traffic context, creative context, and persuasion mechanism.
2. [HYBRID] Prefer controlled/causally informative evidence and account for media, targeting, audience mix, journey, seasonality, offer changes, and other confounders before attributing response to messaging.
3. [AI] Identify repeatable message/proof/offer/creative mechanisms and the contexts or negative cases where they fail.
4. [AI] Keep observed behavioral response distinct from claims about customer psychology/motivation unless direct Customer evidence supports those interpretations.
5. [AI] State applicability, contradictory evidence, uncertainty, and what would revise the lesson. Broader Business Learning is warranted only when cross-domain evidence supports the broader conclusion.
6. [HYBRID] Create/update Marketing Learning only when it materially improves future persuasion work. Deterministic helpers validate/persist; they do not decide semantic maturity, scope, or promotion.

## Verification
- Conversion/response correlation is not automatically causal.
- One winning campaign/creative does not become a universal rule.
- Learning does not encode routing, automatic campaign execution, or scheduled self-optimization.

## Completion Criteria
- Future marketing can reuse a scoped evidence-backed persuasion lesson while retaining model judgment about the current audience, offer, channel, and task.
