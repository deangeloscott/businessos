# Advertorial

## Purpose
Create clearly disclosed editorial-style persuasion that educates while transparently serving a commercial objective.

## Business Outcome
Increase the likelihood of the desired commercial action through an evidence-backed advertorial that matches audience awareness, Offer, proof, and acquisition context.

## Run When
Use when an advertorial is useful to remove a commercial persuasion gap or create the required conversion asset. An Opportunity or real durable WorkRequest may provide context but is not required.

## Process
1. Define audience, publisher/context, problem, insight/story, evidence, Offer, and disclosure requirements.
2. Choose an editorial frame such as investigation, case/story, comparison, guide, or expert explanation that can be truthfully supported.
3. Maintain clear advertising/sponsorship disclosure and avoid impersonating independent journalism.
4. Build a narrative that delivers informational value before and while connecting to the commercial solution.
5. Fact-check comparative/causal claims and ensure the landing destination continues the promise.
6. Use relevant Content operating knowledge and the active harness's real media/document production capabilities directly, and define measurement appropriate to the business objective. Persist a WorkRequest only for a real durable organizational handoff.

## Proportionate Scope
Use only the research, narrative depth, proof, and production treatment needed to deliver genuine informational value and support the commercial decision. Do not inflate editorial complexity to disguise the advertising purpose.

## Verification
- Commercial sponsorship or intent is disclosed appropriately for the real context.
- Comparative and causal claims are evidence-backed.
- The advertorial provides genuine audience value and does not masquerade as independent journalism.
