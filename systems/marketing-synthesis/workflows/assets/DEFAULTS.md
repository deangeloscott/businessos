# Marketing Asset Defaults

- Maintain message continuity from acquisition source through promise, proof, Offer, CTA, and next step.
- Every major claim should have a reason to believe: proof, mechanism, demonstration, authority, customer evidence, or explicit limitation.
- Handle the objections and decision criteria material to this audience/stage; do not add generic persuasion sections simply because a template contains them.
- Separate copy/persuasion failure from form, checkout, scheduling, product, or operational friction before changing the asset.
