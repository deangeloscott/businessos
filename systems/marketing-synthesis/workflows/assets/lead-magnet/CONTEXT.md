# Lead Magnet

## Purpose
Create an exchange-worthy asset that solves a bounded valuable problem and naturally relates to the next commercial step.

## Business Outcome
Increase the likelihood of the desired commercial action through an evidence-backed lead magnet that matches audience awareness, Offer, proof, and acquisition context.

## Run When
Use when a lead magnet is useful to remove a commercial persuasion gap or create the required conversion asset. An Opportunity or real durable WorkRequest may provide context but is not required.

## Process
1. Define audience problem/job, urgency, current alternatives, and what useful standalone outcome can be delivered.
2. Select the format/mechanism that best delivers that outcome: checklist, template, calculator, guide, diagnostic, dataset, mini-course, toolkit, or another suitable form.
3. Ensure the asset provides real standalone value rather than merely previewing the sales pitch.
4. Design the content/interaction and natural bridge to the Offer based on the next unresolved problem rather than forcing a commercial CTA into every section.
5. Define lead capture, consent, tracking, and real customer-journey transition only when they are actually relevant to the requested asset and acquisition path.
6. Use relevant Content operating knowledge and the active harness's real production capabilities directly for the chosen format. Persist a WorkRequest only when a real organizational handoff must survive the current actor/session.

## Proportionate Scope
Choose the smallest format and depth that can deliver a genuinely useful outcome for the audience. Expand only when additional content or interaction materially increases standalone value or qualification—not to make the asset look more substantial.

## Verification
- The lead magnet earns the requested exchange through real standalone utility.
- Customer-facing claims and the bridge to the Offer remain truthful.
- Production complexity matches the job rather than a generic lead-magnet template.
