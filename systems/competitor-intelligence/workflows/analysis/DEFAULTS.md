# Competitor Analysis Defaults

- Compare like with like: market, audience, product tier, time period, and observed funnel stage must be explicit where they affect interpretation.
- Record direct facts separately from inferred strategy. A repeated tactic is not evidence of effectiveness without outcome, persistence, spend, customer, search, or other supporting signals.
- When sources disagree, prefer fact-type authority and freshness; preserve the disagreement when it cannot be resolved.
