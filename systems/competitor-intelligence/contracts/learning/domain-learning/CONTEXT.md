# Competitor Intelligence Learning

## Purpose
Improve competitor monitoring, change detection, source reliability, tactic interpretation, and strategic inference.

## Business Outcome
Improve competitive decisions through evidence-backed competitor intelligence learning, without mistaking observed activity for proven effectiveness.

## Run When
Run during periodic learning cycles or after sufficient OutcomeEvaluations/corrections accumulate.

## Process
1. [AI] Review major competitor Insights against subsequent competitor behavior and downstream outcomes.
2. [HYBRID] Identify which sources/signals reliably predicted material change versus noise.
3. [AI] Capture recurring competitor-response patterns and conditions where tactic-effectiveness inference proved reliable/unreliable.
4. [HYBRID] Avoid turning one competitor's success into a broad best practice.
5. [DETERMINISTIC] Update domain Learning maturity/applicability.
