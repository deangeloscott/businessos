# Competitor Intelligence

## Purpose
Continuously understand relevant competitors and comparison targets, their state, strategic movement, strengths, vulnerabilities, and evidence of apparent tactic effectiveness in the context of a specific business decision.

## Canonical Scope
Competitor identity, decision-specific competitive sets, products, pricing, packaging, offers, positioning, messaging, funnels, campaigns, customer sentiment, strategic/operating change, whitespace, and evidence-backed comparison roles such as direct, substitute, emerging, aspirational/category benchmark, attention/content, or local/search-surface competitor.

## Explicit Non-Scope
Does not own search rankings/backlinks/AI visibility, customer truth, broad industry events, or downstream campaign/content/product decisions. Shared Core subject monitoring may collect competitor evidence but does not become a second owner of competitor meaning.

## Shared behavior
Use Core canonical objects and policies. Reuse resolved SourceProfiles and current canonical intelligence before repeating broad research. Select comparison cohorts for the actual geography, audience, offer, scale, market position, business model, and channel/surface at issue. Publish reusable Observations/Insights. Create Opportunities only for independent interventions inside this domain; delegate production through WorkRequests. Persist only domain-specific state under the active business instance.
