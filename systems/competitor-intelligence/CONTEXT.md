# Competitor Intelligence

## Purpose
Continuously understand competitors, their state, strategic movement, strengths, vulnerabilities, and evidence of apparent tactic effectiveness.

## Canonical Scope
Competitor identity, products, pricing, packaging, offers, positioning, messaging, funnels, campaigns, customer sentiment, strategic change, whitespace.

## Explicit Non-Scope
Does not own search rankings/backlinks/AI visibility, customer truth, broad industry events, or downstream campaign/content/product decisions.

## Shared behavior
Use Core canonical objects and policies. Consume current canonical intelligence before repeating broad research. Publish reusable Observations/Insights. Create Opportunities only for independent interventions inside this domain; delegate production through WorkRequests. Persist only domain-specific state under the active business instance.
