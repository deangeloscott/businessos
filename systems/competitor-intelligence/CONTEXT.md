# Competitor Intelligence

## Purpose
Understand relevant competitors and comparison targets, their current state, strategic movement, strengths, vulnerabilities, and evidence of apparent tactic effectiveness in the context of real business decisions.

## Canonical Scope
Competitor identity, decision-specific competitive sets, products, pricing, packaging, Offers, positioning, messaging, funnels, campaigns, customer sentiment, strategic/operating change, whitespace, and evidence-backed comparison roles such as direct, substitute, emerging, aspirational/category benchmark, attention/content, or local/search-surface competitor.

## Explicit Non-Scope
Does not own search rankings/backlinks/AI visibility, customer truth, broad industry events, downstream campaign/content/product decisions, runtime scheduling, or execution control. Shared Core or another Operating Area may collect competitor-related evidence without becoming a second owner of broad competitor meaning.

## Shared behavior
Use Core canonical organization truth and policies. Reuse current SourceProfiles and canonical intelligence before repeating broad research. Select comparison cohorts for the actual geography, audience, Offer, scale, market position, business model, and channel/surface at issue.

Keep observed competitor behavior, inferred strategy, proxy performance signals, and actual evidence of effectiveness distinct. Strong repeated visibility or market behavior can be useful evidence, but it does not expose a competitor's private economics or prove causality.

Use Competitor Playbooks and Workflows as reusable expert knowledge, not mandatory routes. The active model/user may use competitor evidence directly in strategy, SEO, marketing, product, customer, or other work without creating WorkRequests, relevance signals, duplicate Opportunities, or routing objects merely to cross an AURA boundary.

Persist only competitor-specific evidence, state, Insights, Learning, decisions, or monitoring context that future organizational work will materially benefit from.
