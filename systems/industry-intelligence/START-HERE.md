# Industry Intelligence

Understand external developments that could materially change a business decision, risk, opportunity, or communication.

## Common jobs
- News, regulation, research, technology, market, or social-signal review → `workflows/monitoring/` and `workflows/research/`
- Public industry/social discussion review → `industry.monitoring.social-discussion`
- Cluster or verify an evolving real-world event → `workflows/event/`
- Materiality, business impact, threat/opportunity, scenario, or audience implication → `workflows/analysis/`
- Explain “what happened” vs “what it may mean for this audience/business” → `industry.analysis.audience-implication`
- Fast-moving material development → `industry.analysis.rapid-response`
- Source discovery/mapping for an industry question → `workflows/source-mapping/`
- Reusable Industry Learning → `workflows/learning/`

## Boundary
Industry Intelligence owns reusable operating knowledge for understanding external developments and preserving durable evidence-backed Industry state. Social discussion is a signal, not factual verification. Other AURA domains may directly use relevant Industry evidence and Insights; they do not need a handoff packet, routing event, or delegated WorkRequest merely to reason from the same organizational knowledge.

The active model/user chooses which methods are useful next. The active harness/runtime owns browsing/tools, scheduling, notifications, execution, and delegation mechanics.

## AI execution
Do not load this entire system. Retrieve the smallest relevant context and choose, combine, adapt, skip, or replace useful methods with model/user judgment. Candidate Playbooks and `process-map.json` are navigation/composition help, not semantic routing authority or runtime order.
