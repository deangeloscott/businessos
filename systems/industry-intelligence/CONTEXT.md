# Industry Intelligence

## Purpose
Detect and interpret external developments capable of materially affecting the business, customers, competitors, strategy, operations, or communication.

## Canonical Scope
Industry events, regulation, research, technology/platform shifts, economic/category developments, cultural changes, threats, opportunities, materiality and impact.

## Explicit Non-Scope
Does not own persistent competitor strategy, customer interpretation, search strategy, or downstream execution assets.

## Shared behavior
Use Core canonical objects and policies. Consume current canonical intelligence before repeating broad research. Publish reusable Observations/Insights. Create Opportunities only for independent interventions inside this domain; delegate production through WorkRequests. Persist only domain-specific state under the active business instance.
