# Industry Intelligence

## Purpose
Detect and interpret external developments capable of materially affecting the business, customers, competitors, strategy, operations, or communication.

## Canonical Scope
Industry events, regulation, research, technology/platform shifts, economic/category developments, cultural changes, threats, opportunities, materiality and impact—including material operating signals such as funding, M&A, executive changes, hiring/layoffs, partnerships, expansion, category/product moves, and other current developments when they affect broader business context.

## Explicit Non-Scope
Does not own persistent competitor strategy, customer interpretation, search strategy, or downstream execution assets. Shared Core subject monitoring may surface event evidence, while Industry Intelligence retains ownership of broad event verification/materiality/impact.

## Shared behavior
Use Core canonical objects and policies. Reuse current canonical intelligence and relevant SourceProfile/watch checkpoints before repeating broad research. Verify material developments across the source modalities needed for the event and separate confirmed facts from impact/scenario interpretation. Publish reusable Observations/Insights. Create Opportunities only for independent interventions inside this domain; delegate production through WorkRequests. Persist only domain-specific state under the active business instance.
