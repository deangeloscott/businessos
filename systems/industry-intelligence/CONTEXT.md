# Industry Intelligence

## Purpose
Detect and interpret external developments capable of materially affecting the business, customers, competitors, strategy, operations, or communication.

## Canonical Scope
Industry events, regulation, research, technology/platform shifts, economic/category developments, cultural changes, threats, opportunities, materiality and impact—including operating signals such as funding, M&A, executive changes, hiring/layoffs, partnerships, expansion, category/product moves, and other current developments when they materially affect broader business context.

## Explicit Non-Scope
Does not own persistent competitor strategy, customer interpretation, search strategy, downstream execution assets, runtime scheduling, or cross-domain routing. Shared Core monitoring may surface event evidence while Industry Intelligence retains the reusable expertise for broad event verification, materiality, and impact interpretation.

## Shared behavior
Use Core canonical organization truth and policies. Reuse current intelligence, relevant SourceProfiles, and remembered monitoring/checkpoint context before repeating broad research. Verify material developments across the source modalities appropriate to the event and keep confirmed facts, source confidence, inferred impact, scenarios, and recommendations distinct.

Use Industry Intelligence Playbooks and Workflows as reusable expert knowledge, not mandatory routes. Other work may directly consume relevant Industry Observations and Insights without a WorkRequest, routing event, or delegated handoff merely because the implication belongs to another business area.

Persist only external-development evidence, Industry state, Insights, Learning, decisions, or monitoring context that future organizational work will materially benefit from. Monitoring intent may be remembered; the active harness/runtime owns actual recurrence and notifications.
