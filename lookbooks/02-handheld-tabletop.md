# Scale 02: Handheld & Tabletop E-Commerce Lookbook
### Master Archetypes for Products 6 to 18 Inches (15–45 cm)
*Categories: CPG, Food & Beverage, Specialty Coffee/Tea, Everyday Carry (EDC) Tech, Home Fragrance, Pantry Essentials*

---

## Overview & Scale Dynamics
This is the heartland of consumer packaged goods (CPG) and direct-to-consumer commerce. Products at this scale are held in one or two hands, live on countertops, desks, and shelves, and are purchased frequently. 

Key conversion friction points include taste/scent uncertainty, doubts about packaging durability (e.g. glass breaking in transit), scale mismatches, and distinguishing artisanal quality from mass-market industrial filler.

---

### Archetype 01: The Amazon & Catalog Compliant Packshot
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   Amazon, Google Shopping & Shopify Main Hero (Slot 1). Flawless, distraction-free packaging fidelity. Instant product identification at 160px mobile thumbnail scale.
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   Pure seamless neutral white cyclorama (RGB 255, 255, 255). Product centered horizontally, sitting squarely on the floor plane. Zero extraneous props, badges, or graphics.
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 85mm Prime.  
   * *Aperture:* f/8 to f/11 (complete front-to-back sharpness from front label to rear cap).  
   * *Lighting:* Dual 4x4ft softboxes positioned at 45° to provide even label illumination with a delicate gradient highlight down the curved packaging shoulder. Subtle overhead kicker separating the bottle/bag silhouette from the white background.  
   * *Shadow:* A sharp, razor-thin black contact shadow directly under the packaging footprint, feathering into an authentic 8% soft grey penumbra.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   Crisp readability of matte paper bag folds, gloss varnish on typography, or cold glass reflection proving packaging premiumness.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   Product occupies 85% of total frame height. Exactly calibrated 5200K daylight white balance to ensure label colors match the physical packaging exactly.

### Archetype 02: The Luminous Morning Kitchen
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   PDP Slot 3 (Lifestyle & Habit Projection) and Top-of-Funnel Social. Connects the product to the comforting, aspirational ritual of morning domesticity.
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   Honed Carrara marble or warm white oak butcher block kitchen countertop. In the soft-focus background: a speckled ceramic mug, a pour-over cone or linen towel, and muted Scandinavian cabinetry with soft daylight streaming in.
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 50mm Prime at eye level.  
   * *Aperture:* f/2.8 (shallow depth of field; product is tack-sharp, background dissolves into soft, creamy bokeh).  
   * *Lighting:* Low-angle 45° directional natural morning sunlight streaming from an off-camera window, casting elongated, warm diagonal shadows. Gentle 30% ambient fill on the shadow side to maintain label readability.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   Olfactory and gustatory simulation: amber liquid catching sunlight, visible steam rising from an adjacent cup, natural organic wood grain.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   Product sits in the lower-right third of the frame. Generous negative space in the upper left reserved for mobile UI text overlays. Label text remains 100% legible despite atmospheric lighting.

### Archetype 03: The Kinetic Pour & Dynamic Viscosity
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   PDP Slot 2 or Paid Social Video/GIF/Still. Overcomes taste and sensory hesitation by capturing the physical dynamics of the product in action.
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   Clean studio kitchen or architectural stone block. The bottle, carton, or spout is captured mid-pour, with liquid or powder actively streaming into a glass, bowl, or ceramic vessel.
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 85mm Macro.  
   * *Shutter Speed / Strobe:* High-speed sync strobe (1/4000s freeze) capturing individual droplets, air bubbles, and fluid ribbons without blur.  
   * *Aperture:* f/5.6.  
   * *Lighting:* Intense back-rim lighting to illuminate through the liquid stream, making it glow from within.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   Visuomotor salivary response: glistening surface tension, micro-bubbles churning in the glass, rich velvety texture, cold condensation beads breaking on the exterior.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   The pouring bottle must retain 100% accurate brand packaging branding. Stream must look naturally fluid, not gelatinous or synthetic.

### Archetype 04: The Countertop Flex (Lived-In Foodie Pride)
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   Social Ads & Community Proof. Positions the product not just as food, but as a design-forward lifestyle accessory that consumers proudly display on their kitchen counters.
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   An active home chef's prep area: stainless steel counter or butcher block, flanked by authentic culinary prep elements (flaky sea salt in a ceramic cellar, a wooden spoon resting on a saucer, a freshly sliced lemon, micro-crumbs).
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 35mm or 50mm Prime.  
   * *Aperture:* f/4.  
   * *Lighting:* Bright, crisp midday natural kitchen light with punchy, high-contrast character.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   Freshness, culinary mastery, effortless everyday luxury; tactile matte packaging standing out proudly against utilitarian kitchen equipment.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   The hero product must dominate 60% of the visual hierarchy; kitchen props must never obscure the label or compete in saturation.

### Archetype 05: The In-Hand Grip & Scale Anchor
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   PDP Slot 4 (Scale & Ergonomics). Permanently eliminates customer anxiety around package size, volume, and ease of handling.
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   A casual, authentic human hand naturally holding the product upright, unscrewing the cap, or pouring from it. Shot in a bright, modern domestic or outdoor setting.
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 50mm Prime.  
   * *Aperture:* f/4.  
   * *Lighting:* Clean, directional daylight balancing skin tones with packaging color.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   Tactile ergonomic reassurance: showing how fingers wrap around the bottle/can, the satisfying mechanical scale, proof that the product is neither tiny nor unwieldy.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   Hand must be realistically proportioned to standard human anatomy. Zero AI knuckle distortion or rubbery skin artifacts. Nails and skin styled cleanly and neutrally.

### Archetype 06: The Direct-Flash Bodega / Kitchen Candid
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   Paid Social (TikTok / Meta Pattern-Interrupt). Drives high click-through rates by feeling like an authentic friend's unvarnished photo rather than a polished commercial advertisement.
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   Casual, retro, or urban food setting: diner booth, linoleum kitchen counter, or grocery bag on a stoop.
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 28mm or 35mm wide-angle prime.  
   * *Aperture:* f/8.  
   * *Lighting:* Hard, point-source direct flash mounted on-camera. Deep, razor-sharp black shadow cast immediately behind the product. High contrast, film-like grain.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   Nostalgia, raw authenticity, unpretentious fun, mouthwatering late-night craving.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   Flash must not wash out typography on the packaging. 4:5 vertical crop safe for mobile feed feeds.

### Archetype 07: The Ingredients Knolling (Deconstructed Recipe)
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   PDP Slot 2 or 5 (Transparency & Quality Proof). Visually proves the clean, high-grade, real-food ingredients inside the product without relying on boring text bullet points.
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   Top-down 90° overhead flat-lay (knolling) on a neutral stone, butcher paper, or matte linen backdrop. The hero package is centered, surrounded by neatly arranged, geometric piles of the actual raw ingredients (e.g., single-origin coffee cherries, whole vanilla beans, raw almonds, organic cacao nibs).
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 50mm Prime mounted overhead, perfectly perpendicular to the surface.  
   * *Aperture:* f/8 for edge-to-edge flatness and uniform sharpness.  
   * *Lighting:* Large overhead diffused softbox providing soft, even, shadow-free illumination across all ingredients.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   Purity, craftsmanship, and raw culinary beauty. The tactile contrast between smooth packaging and raw textured spices/seeds.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   Ingredients must be strictly 1:1 with the actual formulation. Package typography must remain oriented correctly for easy reading.

### Archetype 08: The "Us vs. Them" Formulation Split
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   PDP Slot 6 & Bottom-of-Funnel Conversion. Visually dismantles cheap commercial alternatives by showing color, density, or ingredient superiority side-by-side.
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   A clean 50/50 split on a polished white surface. On the left: your product poured into clear glassware showing rich natural color and density. On the right: a generic store brand showing thin, artificially colored, watery separation.
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 85mm Prime.  
   * *Aperture:* f/8 for equal sharpness on both glasses.  
   * *Lighting:* Balanced 5400K clinical daylight with back-fill illuminating fluid clarity.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   Immediate visual justification for why your product costs 30% more: richer hue, higher viscosity, natural sedimentation, zero artificial clarity.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   Comparison must be truthful and grounded in real laboratory testing. Clear text safe zones at the top for labels ("Our 100% Cold Brew" vs "Concentrate + Water").

### Archetype 09: The Unboxing & Mailer Integrity
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   PDP Slot 7 & Post-Purchase Reassurance. Eliminates fear of shipping damage (e.g. broken bottles) and proves gifting appeal.
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   A freshly opened custom corrugated shipping box resting on an entryway console table or kitchen island. The protective pulp insert or custom branded tissue is peeled back, revealing the products nested securely in pristine condition.
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 45mm Prime at a 45° angle looking into the box.  
   * *Aperture:* f/4.  
   * *Lighting:* Warm natural daylight streaming from an open doorway or window.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   The visceral thrill of delivery day: the crisp pull of cardboard tear-strips, pristine product labels arriving unblemished, thoughtful sustainable packing materials.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   Packaging structure shown must reflect the exact mailer used in fulfillment.

### Archetype 10: The 4-Shot Merchandising Contact Sheet
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   Amazon Secondary Image & Mobile Quick-Shop. Packs 4 critical angles into a single image to give analytical buyers 100% confidence in a single glance.
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   A seamless 2x2 grid on a consistent neutral off-white background:
   * *Top Left:* Front hero packshot.
   * *Top Right:* 45° dynamic perspective with cap off.
   * *Bottom Left:* Back label showing crisp, readable ingredients and nutrition facts.
   * *Bottom Right:* Macro close-up on formula/texture or seal.
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 85mm Prime.  
   * *Aperture:* f/11 (uniform sharpness across all four panels).  
   * *Lighting:* Identical 5200K daylight balance across all four quadrants to ensure perfect visual rhythm.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   Information completeness; absolute transparency; zero hidden angles or small-print deception.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   Back-label panel text must be digitally sharp and 100% legible when zoomed on mobile screens.

### Archetype 11: The Natural Patina & Seasoning Maturation
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   PDP Slot 5 & Heritage Proof (Coffee Gear, EDC Knives, Copper Cookware, Leather Sleeves). Proves that natural materials don't wear out—they mature gracefully and gain personal character over years of daily ritual.
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   Warm craftsman workshop or sunlit kitchen butcher block. A side-by-side or split composition: a brand-new pristine item on the left, next to the identical item after 3 years of daily use on the right, displaying a deep, rich, golden-brown patina, softened edges, and heirloom beauty.
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 85mm Prime.  
   * *Aperture:* f/5.6.  
   * *Lighting:* Warm, low-contrast natural daylight grazing across the textured, weathered surfaces.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   The deep romance of heirloom ownership: brass darkening into rich bronze, oiled leather softening to the touch, seasoned cast iron glistening with non-stick maturity.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   Aging must look like dignified, authentic material seasoning—never broken, chipped, or degraded.
