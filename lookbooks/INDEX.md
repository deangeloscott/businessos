## 1. How This Library Works

This library is a **drop-in, zero-overhead visual operating system** for digital commerce. It provides **66 pre-engineered photographic archetypes** organized across **6 physical product scales**, plus a universal 10-dialect brand styling engine.

Instead of engineering complex image prompts from scratch, an operator or AI model simply selects:
1. **The Product Scale File** (`01` through `06`) matching the product's physical dimensions.
2. **The Desired Archetype** (each engineered to dismantle a specific customer objection or fulfill a platform requirement).
3. *(Optional)* **A Brand Dialect** from `DIALECTS.md` to shift lighting, mood, color grade, and material palette toward a specific brand personality (e.g., *Quiet Luxury*, *Playful Utility*, *Subversive Rebellion*, or *Sensorial Glow*).

```
┌─────────────────────────┐     ┌─────────────────────────┐     ┌─────────────────────────┐
│   1. PRODUCT SCALE      │  +  │    2. ARCHETYPE         │  +  │   3. BRAND DIALECT      │
│ (e.g. 06-mobility-      │     │ (e.g. Look 02: Kinetic  │     │ (e.g. Cyber-Kinetic     │
│  transport.md)          │     │  Urban Commute)         │     │  from DIALECTS.md)      │
└─────────────────────────┘     └─────────────────────────┘     └─────────────────────────┘
                                             │
                                             ▼
                        [STUDIO-GRADE, BRAND-ALIGNED PRODUCT IMAGE]
```

## 3. Cognitive Framing: Awareness Stages & Temporal Horizons

This library does **not** hard-code or artificially pigeonhole archetypes to marketing theories. Models and operators possess full creative and strategic latitude to orchestrate imagery according to context. However, two proven cognitive frameworks provide exceptional leverage when choosing or tailoring archetypes:

### Eugene Schwartz's 5 Stages of Awareness
When determining what story an image should tell, consider the buyer's cognitive state:
1. **Unaware:** The buyer doesn't know they have a problem. Needs pattern-interrupting visual poetry, aspirational lifestyle, or emotional intrigue (*Archetypes: Dynamic Velocity, Volumetric Illumination, Macro Sensory Climax*).
2. **Problem Aware:** The buyer feels the friction (e.g. knee pain on a commute, dull dry skin, cluttered counters, bad sleep). Needs imagery illustrating ergonomic relief, comfort, and restorative sanctuary (*Archetypes: Hand-Scale Ergonomics, Cockpit Interface, Domestic Ritual*).
3. **Solution Aware:** The buyer knows solutions exist but is comparing approaches (e.g. cold plunge vs ice bath; selvedge raw denim vs stretch jeans). Needs visual proof of superior mechanism, pure ingredients, and active engineering (*Archetypes: Microscopic Actives, Magnetic Teardown, Component Exploded View*).
4. **Product Aware:** The buyer knows your product specifically, but has conversion-blocking hesitations (fit, dimensions, cleaning difficulty, return fears). Needs definitive de-risking imagery (*Archetypes: Dimensional Orthographics, Water/Mud Torture Tests, Clean-Down Simplicity, Unboxing Mailers*).
5. **Most Aware:** The buyer knows the offer and is ready to buy or repurchase. Needs frictionless clarity, bundle value, and aesthetic confirmation (*Archetypes: Compliant Studio Hero, Regimen Bundle Trio, Merchandising Contact Sheets*).

### Temporal Horizons: The Dimension of Time
High-converting commerce imagery does not merely capture a product at "Time Zero" (brand-new out of the box). It visualizes the product across its full lifecycle:
* **Horizon 0 (Unboxing / Arrival):** Crisp tear-strips, pristine folds, letterpress tags (*Archetype 10 in Scales 02, 03; Archetype 09 in Scale 02*).
* **Horizon 1 (Active Daily Ritual):** Steam, pouring oil, kinetic commute, foaming lather (*Archetypes 02, 04, 06 across scales*).
* **Horizon 2 (Maturity & Patina — 1 to 5 Years):** Rich vegetable-tanned leather burnishing, raw selvedge honeycomb fades, seasoned copper/brass, stable active serums (*Archetype 11 across scales*).
* **Horizon 3 (Decadal Legacy — 10+ Years / 10,000 Miles):** Noble silvered cedar heartwood, commercial brass espresso group heads, intact titanium frame welds (*Archetype 10 & 11 in Scales 04, 05, 06*).

## 5. Universal Prompt Assembly Protocol

To execute any archetype via natural language with an AI assistant or image generation tool, combine the 3 layers into a single cohesive instruction:

```text
[SCALE ARCHETYPE] + [PRODUCT INVARIANTS] + [OPTIONAL BRAND DIALECT]
```

### Example 1: Micro-Intimate Skincare (Scale 01 + Sensorial Glow)
* **Archetype:** `01-micro-intimate.md` → **Archetype 03 (Viscosity, Slump & Micro-Macro Texture Swatch)**
* **Product Invariants:** 50ml frosted glass jar of ceramide barrier repair balm with a pale sage interior formula and debossed brushed aluminum lid.
* **Dialect Modifier:** *Sensorial Glow* (from `DIALECTS.md`)
* **Combined Prompt Instruction to Model:**
  > *"Execute Archetype 03 (Viscosity & Micro-Macro Texture Swatch) from `01-micro-intimate.md` for our 50ml frosted glass jar of barrier balm. Use the Sensorial Glow dialect from `DIALECTS.md`: place the jar on a bone-plaster surface bathed in 5200K soft diffused skylight with low 2:1 contrast. Smear a glossy, buttery ribbon of pale sage cream across the plaster surface directly beside the jar, catching a luminous specular highlight that proves ultra-hydrating density without greasy heaviness."*

### Example 2: Micro-Mobility E-Bike (Scale 06 + Earthy Gorpcore)
* **Archetype:** `06-mobility-transport.md` → **Archetype 02 (The Kinetic Urban Commute / Panning Velocity)**
* **Product Invariants:** All-terrain matte sage green e-bike with fat knobby tires, integrated rear cargo rack, and black battery pack housed inside the hydroformed downtube.
* **Dialect Modifier:** *Earthy Gorpcore* (from `DIALECTS.md`)
* **Combined Prompt Instruction to Model:**
  > *"Execute Archetype 02 (Kinetic Commute / Panning Velocity) from `06-mobility-transport.md` for our matte sage green all-terrain e-bike. Apply the Earthy Gorpcore dialect from `DIALECTS.md`: capture the rider gliding along a damp gravel trail through an old-growth Pacific Northwest cedar forest. Camera tracking parallel at 20 mph with a 1/40s shutter speed; bike frame, knobby tires, and rider wearing a charcoal technical shell are tack-sharp, while the mist-draped mossy conifers blur into kinetic streaks. Wet gravel reflects diffused 6000K overcast mountain daylight."*

### Example 3: Countertop Appliance (Scale 04 + Quiet Luxury)
* **Archetype:** `04-countertop-appliances.md` → **Archetype 11 (The 10-Year Workhorse & Heavy-Cycle Resilience)**
* **Product Invariants:** Dual-boiler manual espresso machine with mirror-polished stainless steel chassis, walnut steam knobs, and dual brass pressure gauges.
* **Dialect Modifier:** *Quiet Luxury* (from `DIALECTS.md`)
* **Combined Prompt Instruction to Model:**
  > *"Execute Archetype 11 (The 10-Year Workhorse) from `04-countertop-appliances.md` for our dual-boiler espresso machine. Apply the Quiet Luxury dialect from `DIALECTS.md`: stage the machine on an unsealed dark charcoal soapstone kitchen counter in an architect-designed limestone kitchen. Use soft raking afternoon daylight from a side window creating deep, quiet chiaroscuro. The machine displays authentic, dignified operational patina: subtle burnishing on the brass group head lever and satin buffing on the stainless drip tray, with pristine 9-bar pressure gauges and a ceramic cup catching rich hazelnut espresso drops."*
