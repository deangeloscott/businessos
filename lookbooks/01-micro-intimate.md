# Scale 01: Micro & Intimate E-Commerce Lookbook
### Master Archetypes for Products Under 6 Inches (< 15 cm)
*Categories: Skincare, Serums, Cosmetics, Fine Jewelry, Fragrance, Supplements, Watches*

---

## Overview & Scale Dynamics
At this physical scale, buyers cannot inspect fine seams, feel product weight, smell botanical extracts, or test skin absorption. Purchases are held back by fears of skin irritation, cheap synthetic fillers, poor clasp durability, or mismatched expectations on fluid volume.

Every archetype in this scale is engineered for **macro fidelity, tactile simulation, and zero-distortion product geometry**.

---

### Archetype 01: The Compliant Hero Packshot
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   Amazon & Google Shopping Main Image compliance (Slot 1). Primary objective is instantaneous, frictionless identification of packaging shape, volume, and label clarity at 160px mobile thumbnail scale.
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   Pure seamless non-reflective cyclorama background (calibrated RGB 255, 255, 255). Product centered horizontally and positioned in the vertical lower 60% to ground the composition. Zero background props or distractions.
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 90mm or 105mm True Macro lens.  
   * *Aperture:* f/8 to f/11 for front-to-back focal sharpness (entire label and cap in crisp focus).  
   * *Lighting:* Dual balanced softboxes at 45° with diffused frontal fill and subtle kicker rim light to cleanly separate packaging edges from white paper.  
   * *Shadow:* A razor-thin, near-black contact occlusion shadow directly beneath the bottle base, diffusing into a soft 5% gray penumbra. No floating object look.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   Subtle specular highlight along the shoulder of the bottle/jar indicating glass or polished aluminum density; crisp label embossing texture.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   Product occupies 82% to 85% of total frame area. Exact Pantone packaging color calibrated under 5500K daylight balance. Label typography must remain 100% faithful to reference vectors.

### Archetype 02: The Visuomotor Swatch & Viscosity Proof
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   PDP Slot 2 or 5. Directly eliminates the "I can't feel it" objection by triggering premotor cortex touch simulation. Proves serum viscosity, cream richness, or powder micro-milling.
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   Frosted white acrylic plate, matte ceramic tile, or unsealed honed limestone slab. A single generous, tactile smear or droplet of the product is placed directly adjacent to the open jar or dropper tip.
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 105mm Macro at 1:1 reproduction ratio.  
   * *Aperture:* f/4 (ultra-sharp focus on the apex of the cream smear or liquid bead, with soft, creamy background falloff on the packaging).  
   * *Lighting:* Raking low-angle side lighting at 20° from the left to sculpt the microscopic ridges, bubbles, and peaks of the formula swatch.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   Glossy, dewy specular highlights on the formula apex; visible richness and cohesion of the emulsion; a bead of liquid hanging from a glass pipette with unbroken surface tension.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   Formula color and opacity must accurately represent real chemical composition (e.g. golden jojoba oil vs milky peptide emulsion). Formula swatch occupies 40% of frame, packaging 50%.

### Archetype 03: The Botanical / Raw Extraction
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   Brand storytelling and ingredient authority. Visually justifies premium pricing by showing the raw botanical, mineral, or active ingredients from which the product derives.
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   Textured raw stone or wet slate surface. The hero product sits grounded in the center, flanked by 2–3 deconstructed raw ingredient specimens (e.g. broken vanilla pods, fresh rosemary sprigs, cold-pressed sea buckthorn berries, crushed pink Himalayan salt).
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 85mm Prime.  
   * *Aperture:* f/5.6.  
   * *Lighting:* Soft, natural overcast skylight (5600K) coming from a 60° overhead angle. Gentle contrast with rich deep greens and natural earth tones.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   Olfactory simulation: visible moisture on botanical petals, broken seeds displaying oily sheen, microscopic crystals catching light.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   Raw ingredients must be strictly authentic to formulation (no decorative fruit that isn't an ingredient). Ingredients must not overshadow the hero product (product maintains 65% visual weight).

### Archetype 04: The Vanity Altar (Domestic Luxury In-Situ)
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   PDP Slot 3 (Lifestyle & Habit Projection). Allows the customer to visualize the product permanently living on their luxury bathroom counter or bedroom dresser.
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   Honed Carrara marble bathroom vanity or unlacquered brass tray. Background features soft out-of-focus elements: textured waffle linen hand towel, a minimalist architectural ceramic vase with a single dried stem, subtle subway tile reflection.
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 50mm Prime.  
   * *Aperture:* f/2.8 for cinematic shallow depth of field.  
   * *Lighting:* Warm natural morning sunlight filtering through bathroom frosted glass at an angle, creating soft rectangular light patches across the vanity surface.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   Warm, clean, steamy bathroom atmosphere; pristine reflective glass; the comforting physical weight of a morning self-care ritual.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   Upper left quadrant preserved as clean negative space (for Shopify/Meta promotional headline text). The product must remain in tack-sharp focus while vanity props melt into soft bokeh.

### Archetype 05: The Fingertip Touch & Application
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   PDP Slot 2 or Paid Social Hook. Demonstrates human scale, skin absorption, and effortless everyday application.
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   A cropped macro composition focused on a model's natural hand or collarbone. The product bottle is held naturally, or a single drop is resting on the pad of an index finger ready for application.
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 85mm Macro.  
   * *Aperture:* f/4.  
   * *Lighting:* Diffused beauty dish or massive octabox directly above and in front, casting flattering, shadowless light across skin while preserving micro-texture.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   Natural, un-airbrushed skin texture (visible pores, authentic hydration); a glistening droplet of serum resting on the fingertip showing zero grease, just pure liquid lightness.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   Strict anti-AI guardrail: Zero distorted fingers or unnatural wrist angles. Model styling must match brand demographic (minimalist neutral manicure, clean hydration).

### Archetype 06: The Caustic Light & Glass Architecture
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   Top-of-funnel luxury brand hero image (Fragrance, Serums, Fine Spirits). Celebrates bottle industrial design, glass thickness, and liquid purity.
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   Polished stone, glass slab, or shallow pool of pristine clear water. The translucent or amber glass bottle is lit from behind to cast intricate, dancing light refractions (caustics) across the surface.
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 105mm Macro.  
   * *Aperture:* f/5.6.  
   * *Lighting:* Intense, narrow directional beam (snooted hard key light) hitting the back of the bottle, projecting golden or emerald liquid caustics onto the foreground plane.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   Visual weight and temperature of cold glass; the pure, luminous golden refraction of oil or perfume liquid; clean optical brilliance.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   Backlighting must not blow out or obscure label typography. Label printing must remain crisp and readable against the illuminated liquid background.

### Archetype 07: The Palm & Pocket Scale Anchor
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   PDP Slot 4 (Spatial De-risking). Permanently resolves customer confusion about bottle or jewelry dimensions by comparing it against universal human anatomy.
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   An open, resting adult palm holding the product upright or resting flat, or a casual shot of the product slipping into a tailored denim pocket or luxury leather pouch.
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 50mm Prime.  
   * *Aperture:* f/4 for balanced depth between palm and packaging.  
   * *Lighting:* Clean, neutral 5400K daylight from an open window.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   Immediate cognitive scale realization: *"Ah, it's about 4 inches tall and fits perfectly in my hand."* Reassurance of portable, travel-friendly convenience.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   The human hand must be proportionally standard (no deceptive lens distortion making the product look larger or smaller than its true physical ounces/milliliters).

### Archetype 08: Direct-Flash Night-Out Editorial
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   Paid Social (TikTok / Meta Feed pattern-interrupt). Cuts through stale beige wellness feeds with vibrant, high-fashion, youth-culture energy.
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   A night-out setting: retro diner booth, vintage car dashboard, or black lacquer nightclub vanity. Staged as if captured mid-celebration or mid-touch-up.
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 35mm Prime.  
   * *Aperture:* f/8.  
   * *Lighting:* Hard, direct on-camera speedlight flash. Sharp black cast shadows directly behind the product, 0% ambient fill. Film emulation tone with subtle grain.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   Auditory and sensory memory of social nightlife: high energy, glossy lipstick sheen, the click of a compact mirror, the crisp pop of a flash.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   Flash exposure must not wash out packaging text. The product must remain unmistakably the sharpest object in the frame despite the candid environment.

### Archetype 09: The Clinical Formulation Split
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   PDP Slot 6 (Differentiation Proof). Provides undeniable visual evidence of ingredient purity and concentration versus conventional diluted competitors.
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   A clean 50/50 vertical or horizontal laboratory split. On the left: your product's rich, golden, unrefined cold-pressed oil droplet. On the right: a thin, watery, artificial mineral oil comparison.
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 100mm Macro.  
   * *Aperture:* f/8 for equal sharpness on both comparison samples.  
   * *Lighting:* Neutral clinical lab lighting (5500K) over pure white surgical acrylic.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   Visceral perception of value: visible density, natural color saturation, and unbroken surface tension proving high-potency active ingredients.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   Strict honesty guardrail: Claims must be evidence-grounded. Text labels ("Our 100% Cold-Pressed" vs "Diluted Filler") must have dedicated high-contrast banner zones.

### Archetype 10: The Regimen Trio (Routine Bundle Staging)
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   Cart-upsell & Bundle PDPs. Sells the complete 3-step system (Cleanse, Treat, Moisturize) as a unified daily habit rather than isolated single purchases.
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   A graduated tiered stone pedestal (honed travertine or fluted concrete). The three products are sequenced logically from left to right in order of usage (Step 1, Step 2, Step 3).
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 70mm Prime.  
   * *Aperture:* f/5.6 to maintain focus across all three staggered bottles.  
   * *Lighting:* Cohesive directional key light ensuring matching shadow angles across all three packaging silhouettes.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   The psychological satisfaction of completeness, structural order, and synergistic daily transformation.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   Relative bottle heights must be strictly true to scale (e.g. 200ml cleanser is noticeably taller than the 30ml eye serum). Fixed camera pitch (15°) preserves horizon harmony.

---

### Archetype 11: The Temporal Stability & Shelf-Life Proof
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   PDP Slot 5 & Customer Reassurance (Skincare, Active Serums & Supplements). Eliminates the silent fear: *"Will this vitamin C turn brown, separate, or oxidize before I can finish the bottle?"*
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   Clean clinical or pharmaceutical marble bench. A side-by-side or documented timeline demonstration: a freshly opened bottle alongside a bottle opened 6 months prior, showing identical golden clarity, zero discoloration, and zero emulsion separation.
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 105mm Macro.  
   * *Aperture:* f/8.  
   * *Lighting:* Calibrated 5500K clinical backlighting through the amber or opaque glass, proving formulation stability.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   Deep biochemical trust: the reassuring amber glass blocking UV rays; airless pump mechanism preventing oxidation; pristine, unblemished freshness.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   Stability claims must be grounded in real shelf-life test data. Typography areas reserved for testing metrics (e.g. "98% Active Potency at Month 6").
