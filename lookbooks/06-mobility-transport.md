# Scale 06: Micro-Mobility, Vehicles & Adventure Transport Lookbook
### Master Archetypes for Micro-Mobility, Transport & Overland Systems
*Categories: E-Bikes, Electric Scooters, Rooftop Overland Tents, Cargo Trailers, Adventure Rigs, Skateboards, OneWheels*

---

## Overview & Scale Dynamics
Micro-mobility and adventure transport represent an explosive, high-ticket e-commerce sector ($1,000 to $8,000+). These products are mechanical, electrical, and kinetic—purchased to navigate city streets, conquer mountain trails, or transform a vehicle into an outdoor campsite.

Shoppers face high-stakes functional doubts:
1. *Battery & Range Anxiety:* "How do I charge it? Is the battery removable?"
2. *Spatial / Storage Dread:* "Can I fit this in my apartment, car trunk, or elevator?"
3. *Kinetic Stability & Sizing:* "Will this feel stable at 20 mph? Does it fit my height?"
4. *Weather & Durability Dread:* "Can I ride this in the rain, or will the electronics short out?"

Every archetype here is engineered with **kinetic velocity physics, modular utility proof, and real-world scale**.

### Archetype 01: The Compliant Studio Side-Profile Hero
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   Shopify & Catalog Main Hero (Slot 1). Flawless, distortion-free view of frame geometry, wheelbase proportions, tire tread, and drive-train components.
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   Pure seamless neutral white cyclorama (RGB 255, 255, 255). The e-bike, scooter, or trailer is positioned in true parallel side-profile (drive-side facing the camera), resting solidly on its tires.
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 85mm Prime positioned 15 feet back at axle height to eliminate perspective keystoning (both wheels appear identical in size).  
   * *Aperture:* f/8 to f/11 for corner-to-corner mechanical sharpness.  
   * *Lighting:* Commercial automotive studio softbox array casting long, smooth highlight ribbons along the top tube, down tube, and wheel rims.  
   * *Shadow:* Crisp, razor-thin black contact shadow directly under both tire contact patches.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   Machined mechanical precision: the tautness of the drive chain/belt, the cold density of hydroformed aluminum tubing, the rugged rubber tire tread.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   Vehicle occupies 82% to 85% of total frame width. Frame decals, motor brand badges, and battery housing must be 100% faithful to CAD specs.

### Archetype 02: The Kinetic Urban Commute (Panning Velocity)
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   PDP Slot 2 & Paid Social Hero Ad. Visualizes the exhilarating speed, freedom, and effortless momentum of gliding through city traffic.
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   A dynamic urban protected bike lane or downtown bridge during morning golden hour. The rider is captured in active motion at 20 mph.
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 50mm Prime mounted on a tracking chase vehicle.  
   * *Shutter Speed:* 1/30s to 1/60s panning shutter. The vehicle frame, handlebars, and rider’s torso are tack-sharp, while the background cityscape, asphalt, and wheel spokes blur into smooth horizontal motion streaks.  
   * *Lighting:* Low-angle morning sunlight casting golden highlights along the frame and rider's helmet.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   Kinetic wind in your face, the silent whir of a brushless hub motor, the effortless conquest of city traffic.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   Rider must wear safety gear (helmet, clean commuter wardrobe). Frame must remain tack-sharp despite motion-blurred background.

4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   The crisp tactile squeeze of hydraulic brake levers; the high-resolution readability of the digital speedometer in full sunlight.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**

### Archetype 11: The Ergonomic Geometry, Standover & Dimensional Fit Blueprint
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   PDP Slot 4 & Sizing De-risking (E-Bikes, Scooters, Cargo Rigs & Overland Systems). Eliminates return friction and cart abandonment caused by fit anxiety: *"Will this frame fit someone 5'3" or 6'2"? What is the exact standover height and reach?"* Provides definitive sizing clarity for confident checkout.
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   A clean, technical studio environment with a subtle architectural CAD grid background (RGB 245, 245, 248). The vehicle is presented in pure side-profile orthographic projection with crisp, high-contrast graphic measurement dimension lines:
   * Standover clearance (ground to top-tube).
   * Minimum and maximum saddle adjustment range.
   * Handlebar reach and cockpit angle.
   * Wheelbase and total bumper-to-bumper length.
   * Transparent ergonomic human silhouettes (e.g. 5'2" and 6'3" riders) illustrating comfortable knee flexion and relaxed upright spine posture.
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens / Perspective:* 135mm Telephoto at axle height (or true parallel 3D CAD orthographic projection) with zero keystoning or wheel distortion.  
   * *Aperture:* f/11 for absolute corner-to-corner mechanical sharpness.  
   * *Lighting:* Clean, shadowless high-key technical lighting with high-contrast graphic callouts.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   Engineered reassurance and personalized ergonomic fit: instantly visualizing oneself straddling the frame, effortlessly putting a foot down at traffic stops, and feeling zero lower-back strain.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   Dimensions must be mathematically accurate to manufacturer CAD specs in both Imperial (inches) and Metric (cm). All text labels and dimension arrows must remain within the center-80% safe zone for mobile legibility.
