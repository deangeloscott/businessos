# Cross-System Intelligence

Publish canonical intelligence once; subscribe/evaluate relevance; query before researching; request refresh from the semantic owner; use WorkRequest for delegated execution; use Initiative for genuinely distinct coordinated Opportunities.

## Signals to content
A customer comment, ProofRecord, Industry Insight, competitor/search observation, or Content trend may be evaluated for communication value. If another domain already owns the Opportunity and only needs production, Content receives a WorkRequest and does not create a duplicate Opportunity. If content itself is an independent valuable intervention, Content may create its own Opportunity.

## Industry to content
Industry Intelligence owns event facts and broad business/audience implication. Content Synthesis owns native expression. Keep “what happened,” “why it matters,” and “how we communicate it” as separate reasoning layers.
