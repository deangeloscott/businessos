# Competitive Intelligence

Understand competitors, substitutes, competitive movement, and supported implications.

**Ask for the outcome in normal language.** Playbooks below are useful end-to-end frames, not commands the user must select. The model may use their entry Workflows, other AURA Workflows, external Skills, or another sound method.

## Playbooks

### Competitor Research

Identify the competitors and substitutes that matter, understand what they are doing, compare the business against them, and derive supported implications.

*Try:* “Research our competitors and tell me what they are doing better than us.”

**Common entry Workflow:** [Competitive Position & Landscape](../../systems/competitor-intelligence/workflows/analysis/competitive-position/CONTEXT.md)

### Competitor Monitoring

Keep important competitive movement current and distinguish observed changes from unsupported assumptions about effectiveness or intent.

*Try:* “Tell me what materially changed with our competitors since we last looked.”

**Common entry Workflow:** [Competitive Ecosystem Radar](../../systems/competitor-intelligence/workflows/intelligence/ecosystem-radar/CONTEXT.md)

## Common Workflows

These reusable procedures may support one or more Playbooks or be used independently. They are not a mandatory sequence.

- **[Competitor Research Plan](../../systems/competitor-intelligence/workflows/research/plan/CONTEXT.md)** — Resolve a competitive question with bounded evidence.
- **[Adaptive Competitive Source Coverage](../../systems/competitor-intelligence/workflows/research/adaptive-source-coverage/CONTEXT.md)** — Adapt research depth and evidence-source coverage to the competitive decision.
- **[Competitive Ecosystem Radar](../../systems/competitor-intelligence/workflows/intelligence/ecosystem-radar/CONTEXT.md)** — Discover important competitive movements and tactic claims, verify what is observed versus supported, and preserve material implications for direct reuse.
- **[Competitive Position & Landscape](../../systems/competitor-intelligence/workflows/analysis/competitive-position/CONTEXT.md)** — Build a decision-useful competitive landscape and position using the relevant evidence and specialist methods.
- **[Competitor Discovery](../../systems/competitor-intelligence/workflows/discovery/competitive-set/CONTEXT.md)** — Identify current direct and substitute competitors.
- **[Competitor Entity Resolution](../../systems/competitor-intelligence/workflows/discovery/entity-resolution/CONTEXT.md)** — Resolve competitor domains and public profiles to the correct canonical entity.
- **[Emerging Competitor Discovery](../../systems/competitor-intelligence/workflows/discovery/emerging-competitors/CONTEXT.md)** — Identify new competitors entering the relevant customer choice set.
- **[Competitor Profile](../../systems/competitor-intelligence/workflows/analysis/profiling/CONTEXT.md)** — Build a current evidence-backed competitor profile.
- **[Pricing Intelligence](../../systems/competitor-intelligence/workflows/analysis/pricing/CONTEXT.md)** — Understand current competitor pricing and material changes.
- **[Competitor Offer Comparison](../../systems/competitor-intelligence/workflows/analysis/offer-comparison/CONTEXT.md)** — Compare customer-facing offer economics and risk.
- **[Competitor Capability Comparison](../../systems/competitor-intelligence/workflows/analysis/capability-comparison/CONTEXT.md)** — Compare capabilities in customer context.
- **[Positioning Intelligence](../../systems/competitor-intelligence/workflows/analysis/positioning/CONTEXT.md)** — Understand competitor positioning and message.
- **[Funnel Intelligence](../../systems/competitor-intelligence/workflows/analysis/funnels/CONTEXT.md)** — Capture and compare observable acquisition funnels.
- **[Advertising Intelligence](../../systems/competitor-intelligence/workflows/analysis/advertising/CONTEXT.md)** — Understand observable competitor advertising patterns.
- **[Competitor Content Strategy](../../systems/competitor-intelligence/workflows/analysis/content-strategy/CONTEXT.md)** — Understand competitor content strategy.
- **[Competitor Customer Sentiment](../../systems/competitor-intelligence/workflows/analysis/customer-sentiment/CONTEXT.md)** — Understand recurring customer praise and complaints.
- **[Competitor Tactic Validation](../../systems/competitor-intelligence/workflows/analysis/tactic-validation/CONTEXT.md)** — Determine what the evidence supports about whether a tactic appears effective and why.
- **[Competitor Strength and Weakness Assessment](../../systems/competitor-intelligence/workflows/analysis/strength-weakness/CONTEXT.md)** — Assess scoped competitor strengths and vulnerabilities.
- **[Strategic Change Detection](../../systems/competitor-intelligence/workflows/analysis/strategic-change/CONTEXT.md)** — Detect and interpret strategic movement.
- **[Competitive Implication Analysis](../../systems/competitor-intelligence/workflows/analysis/competitive-implications/CONTEXT.md)** — Translate supported competitor evidence into scoped implications the current business work can use directly.

## Working principle

Use the fewest instructions and Workflows necessary to repeatedly achieve the desired outcome at the required truth and quality standard. Preserve non-obvious expertise, evidence requirements, and real constraints; let the capable model/harness choose implementation details, tools, providers, external Skills, and orchestration unless the implementation itself matters.
