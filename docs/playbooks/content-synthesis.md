# Content Synthesis

Turn useful ideas, evidence, and source material into strong audience-appropriate content.

**Ask for the outcome in normal language.** Playbooks below are useful end-to-end frames, not commands the user must select. The model may use their entry Workflows, other AURA Workflows, external Skills, or another sound method.

## Playbooks

Turn audience context, evidence, ideas, performance signals, and communication goals into a strong content approach before or across specific formats.

## Common Workflows

These reusable procedures may support one or more Playbooks or be used independently. They are not a mandatory sequence.

- **[Content Brief](../../systems/content-synthesis/workflows/intake/content-brief/CONTEXT.md)** — Define a complete content production job from the smallest useful current evidence and constraints.
- **[Content Audience and Consumption Context](../../systems/content-synthesis/workflows/strategy/audience-context/CONTEXT.md)** — Define audience and consumption context.
- **[Angle Development](../../systems/content-synthesis/workflows/strategy/angle/CONTEXT.md)** — Choose a useful angle for the source idea.
- **[Platform & Format Selection](../../systems/content-synthesis/workflows/strategy/format-platform/CONTEXT.md)** — Select platform/format based on the communication job.
- **[Platform-Native Adaptation](../../systems/content-synthesis/workflows/adaptation/platform-native/CONTEXT.md)** — Express an idea natively for a specific platform.
- **[Content Ecosystem Radar](../../systems/content-synthesis/workflows/intelligence/ecosystem-radar/CONTEXT.md)** — Discover emerging content formats, creator patterns, distribution mechanics, and communication tactics; validate what is durable enough to test or learn from.
- **[Trending Content Discovery](../../systems/content-synthesis/workflows/intelligence/trending-content-discovery/CONTEXT.md)** — Find and contextualize unusually strong or trending content without treating popularity as proof of business effectiveness.
- **[Creator Content Monitoring](../../systems/content-synthesis/workflows/intelligence/creator-monitoring/CONTEXT.md)** — Build or refresh evidence-backed creator/channel understanding and transferable creative mechanisms when useful; recurring execution belongs to the active runtime.
- **[Creative Pattern Extraction](../../systems/content-synthesis/workflows/intelligence/creative-pattern-extraction/CONTEXT.md)** — Extract reusable creative mechanisms without copying protected expression.
- **[Cross-Niche Creative Pattern Transfer](../../systems/content-synthesis/workflows/intelligence/cross-niche-pattern-transfer/CONTEXT.md)** — Adapt a validated mechanism from another niche when the audience/context fit is defensible.
- **[Content Performance Pattern Analysis](../../systems/content-synthesis/workflows/intelligence/content-performance-analysis/CONTEXT.md)** — Learn what this business's content actually appears to do well using available outcome evidence.
- **[Article Production](../../systems/content-synthesis/workflows/production/article/CONTEXT.md)** — Produce a complete article.
- **[Newsletter Production](../../systems/content-synthesis/workflows/production/newsletter/CONTEXT.md)** — Produce a complete newsletter.
- **[Short-Form Video Production](../../systems/content-synthesis/workflows/production/short-video/CONTEXT.md)** — Produce platform-native short video.
- **[Long-Form Video Production](../../systems/content-synthesis/workflows/production/long-video/CONTEXT.md)** — Produce complete long-form video.
- **[Podcast Episode Production](../../systems/content-synthesis/workflows/production/podcast/CONTEXT.md)** — Produce audio/podcast content.
- **[Carousel / Slideshow Production](../../systems/content-synthesis/workflows/production/carousel/CONTEXT.md)** — Produce a sequential visual carousel.
- **[Presentation / Slideshow Production](../../systems/content-synthesis/workflows/production/presentation/CONTEXT.md)** — Produce a clear presentation/slideshow.
- **[Infographic Production](../../systems/content-synthesis/workflows/production/infographic/CONTEXT.md)** — Produce an evidence-backed infographic.
- **[Image / Graphic Production](../../systems/content-synthesis/workflows/production/image/CONTEXT.md)** — Produce an image/graphic.
- **[Animation / Motion Production](../../systems/content-synthesis/workflows/production/animation/CONTEXT.md)** — Produce explanatory animation.
- **[GIF / Looping Motion Production](../../systems/content-synthesis/workflows/production/gif/CONTEXT.md)** — Produce concise looping motion/GIF.
- **[AI Avatar Video Production](../../systems/content-synthesis/workflows/production/avatar-video/CONTEXT.md)** — Produce an authorized AI-avatar video.
- **[Customer Case Study](../../systems/content-synthesis/workflows/production/case-study/CONTEXT.md)** — Produce a verified customer case study.
- **[Demonstration Asset Production](../../systems/content-synthesis/workflows/production/demo/CONTEXT.md)** — Produce a credible demonstration.
- **[Publish or Schedule Content Asset](../../systems/content-synthesis/workflows/publishing/publish-asset/CONTEXT.md)** — Publish or schedule the requested content Asset through the active host and verify the resulting external state when practical.

## Working principle

Use the fewest instructions and Workflows necessary to repeatedly achieve the desired outcome at the required truth and quality standard. Preserve non-obvious expertise, evidence requirements, and real constraints; let the capable model/harness choose implementation details, tools, providers, external Skills, and orchestration unless the implementation itself matters.
