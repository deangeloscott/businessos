# Customer Intelligence

Understand customers, prospects, needs, language, decisions, and experiences from appropriate evidence.

**Ask for the outcome in normal language.** Playbooks below are useful end-to-end frames, not commands the user must select. The model may use their entry Workflows, other AURA Workflows, external Skills, or another sound method.

## Playbooks

### Customer Research

Resolve an important customer knowledge need with evidence appropriate to the decision, using the relevant research and analysis workflows.

*Try:* “Research our customers and tell me what they care about most.”

**Common entry Workflow:** [Customer Research Plan](../../systems/customer-intelligence/workflows/research/plan/CONTEXT.md)

### Voice of Customer

Build reusable evidence-backed understanding of the language, pains, desires, objections, outcomes, and decision criteria customers actually express.

*Try:* “Build a voice-of-customer view we can use in our product and marketing work.”

**Common entry Workflow:** [Voice-of-Customer Synthesis](../../systems/customer-intelligence/workflows/analysis/voice-of-customer/CONTEXT.md)

## Common Workflows

These reusable procedures may support one or more Playbooks or be used independently. They are not a mandatory sequence.

- **[Customer Research Plan](../../systems/customer-intelligence/workflows/research/plan/CONTEXT.md)** — Resolve a customer knowledge need with evidence sufficient for the decision.
- **[Customer Signal Ecosystem Radar](../../systems/customer-intelligence/workflows/intelligence/ecosystem-radar/CONTEXT.md)** — Detect changing customer needs, language, objections, expectations, and use cases; corroborate them; and compare external signals with first-party evidence when useful.
- **[Customer Interview Intelligence](../../systems/customer-intelligence/workflows/evidence-collection/interviews/CONTEXT.md)** — Collect and code neutral, decision-relevant customer interview evidence.
- **[Survey Intelligence](../../systems/customer-intelligence/workflows/evidence-collection/surveys/CONTEXT.md)** — Design, field, and interpret a survey with defensible coverage.
- **[Sales Conversation Evidence Collection](../../systems/customer-intelligence/workflows/evidence-collection/sales-conversations/CONTEXT.md)** — Extract direct customer evidence from sales conversations.
- **[Support Intelligence Mining](../../systems/customer-intelligence/workflows/evidence-collection/support/CONTEXT.md)** — Extract recurring customer problems and questions from support evidence.
- **[Public Conversation Collection](../../systems/customer-intelligence/workflows/evidence-collection/public-conversation/CONTEXT.md)** — Collect and interpret public customer/audience evidence without treating public visibility as permission for invasive profiling.
- **[Voice-of-Customer Synthesis](../../systems/customer-intelligence/workflows/analysis/voice-of-customer/CONTEXT.md)** — Produce reusable customer language and themes.
- **[Pain, Desire & Outcome Analysis](../../systems/customer-intelligence/workflows/analysis/pain-desire/CONTEXT.md)** — Identify pains, desires, and desired outcomes.
- **[Objection Analysis](../../systems/customer-intelligence/workflows/analysis/objections/CONTEXT.md)** — Identify and scope customer objections.
- **[Customer Decision Driver Analysis](../../systems/customer-intelligence/workflows/analysis/decision-drivers/CONTEXT.md)** — Identify decision criteria, triggers, tradeoffs, and disqualifiers.
- **[Customer Job and Desired Outcome Analysis](../../systems/customer-intelligence/workflows/analysis/jobs-outcomes/CONTEXT.md)** — Identify customer jobs and desired progress.
- **[Win/Loss Analysis](../../systems/customer-intelligence/workflows/analysis/win-loss/CONTEXT.md)** — Explain why comparable opportunities are won or lost.
- **[Churn Reason Analysis](../../systems/customer-intelligence/workflows/analysis/churn/CONTEXT.md)** — Explain customer-stated and evidence-backed reasons for leaving.
- **[Customer Feature Need Analysis](../../systems/customer-intelligence/workflows/analysis/feature-needs/CONTEXT.md)** — Interpret feature requests as underlying needs rather than roadmap commands.
- **[Customer Segmentation Intelligence](../../systems/customer-intelligence/workflows/analysis/segmentation/CONTEXT.md)** — Identify meaningful customer differences and propose segment refinement where justified.
- **[Customer Insight Refresh](../../systems/customer-intelligence/workflows/analysis/insight-refresh/CONTEXT.md)** — Keep Customer Insights current and contradiction-aware when new evidence makes review useful.
- **[Customer Research Gap Review](../../systems/customer-intelligence/workflows/monitoring/research-gaps/CONTEXT.md)** — Run a bounded review for decision-critical customer evidence gaps and identify the smallest useful research action; recurrence belongs to the active runtime.

## Working principle

Use the fewest instructions and Workflows necessary to repeatedly achieve the desired outcome at the required truth and quality standard. Preserve non-obvious expertise, evidence requirements, and real constraints; let the capable model/harness choose implementation details, tools, providers, external Skills, and orchestration unless the implementation itself matters.
