# Customer Optimization

Improve the customer journey from qualification and purchase through value, retention, expansion, recovery, and referral.

**Ask for the outcome in normal language.** Playbooks below are useful end-to-end frames, not commands the user must select. The model may use their entry Workflows, other AURA Workflows, external Skills, or another sound method.

## Playbooks

### Customer Journey Optimization

Understand the journey, identify the most important progression problem, diagnose the likely cause, improve it, and evaluate what changed.

*Try:* “Figure out where customers are getting stuck and improve the most important part of the journey.”

**Common entry Workflow:** [Journey Bottleneck Prioritization](../../systems/customer-optimization/workflows/diagnosis/bottleneck-prioritization/CONTEXT.md)

### Retention and Churn

Understand why customers leave or fail to realize value, improve the relevant experience, and evaluate durable retention rather than manipulating short-term staying behavior.

*Try:* “Figure out why customers are churning and what we should improve.”

**Common entry Workflow:** [Retention Optimization](../../systems/customer-optimization/workflows/intervention/retention/CONTEXT.md)

## Common Workflows

These reusable procedures may support one or more Playbooks or be used independently. They are not a mandatory sequence.

- **[Customer Journey Mapping](../../systems/customer-optimization/workflows/journey/mapping/CONTEXT.md)** — Define the business-specific customer journey and measurable transitions.
- **[Journey Instrumentation](../../systems/customer-optimization/workflows/journey/instrumentation/CONTEXT.md)** — Instrument journey states and transitions.
- **[Journey Instrumentation Data Quality](../../systems/customer-optimization/workflows/instrumentation/data-quality/CONTEXT.md)** — Verify journey measurement is trustworthy.
- **[Customer Optimization Ecosystem Tactic Radar](../../systems/customer-optimization/workflows/intelligence/ecosystem-radar/CONTEXT.md)** — Discover external customer-journey tactics, verify the evidence and active-business applicability, and identify what may be worth investigating, adapting, or testing without automatic intervention.
- **[Journey Transition Analysis](../../systems/customer-optimization/workflows/journey/transition-analysis/CONTEXT.md)** — Find progression, drop-off, and delay patterns.
- **[Journey Bottleneck Prioritization](../../systems/customer-optimization/workflows/diagnosis/bottleneck-prioritization/CONTEXT.md)** — Prioritize the journey bottleneck worth solving.
- **[Journey Root-Cause Diagnosis](../../systems/customer-optimization/workflows/diagnosis/root-cause/CONTEXT.md)** — Diagnose why a transition is failing before intervention.
- **[Conversion Optimization](../../systems/customer-optimization/workflows/intervention/conversion/CONTEXT.md)** — Improve pre-purchase progression where journey mechanics are limiting.
- **[Lead Qualification Optimization](../../systems/customer-optimization/workflows/intervention/qualification/CONTEXT.md)** — Improve qualification process and customer fit.
- **[Sales Handoff Optimization](../../systems/customer-optimization/workflows/intervention/sales-handoff/CONTEXT.md)** — Reduce lead/customer context loss, delay, and failure across real sales handoffs.
- **[Scheduling & No-Show Optimization](../../systems/customer-optimization/workflows/intervention/scheduling/CONTEXT.md)** — Improve scheduling/attendance progression.
- **[Checkout Optimization](../../systems/customer-optimization/workflows/intervention/checkout/CONTEXT.md)** — Improve purchase/checkout progression.
- **[Onboarding Optimization](../../systems/customer-optimization/workflows/intervention/onboarding/CONTEXT.md)** — Move customers from purchase/start to meaningful value.
- **[Activation Optimization](../../systems/customer-optimization/workflows/intervention/activation/CONTEXT.md)** — Increase successful activation/value milestones.
- **[Time-to-Value Analysis](../../systems/customer-optimization/workflows/measurement/time-to-value/CONTEXT.md)** — Measure and reduce time to meaningful customer value.
- **[Customer Value and Relationship Economics](../../systems/customer-optimization/workflows/measurement/customer-value/CONTEXT.md)** — Understand mutually valuable customer relationships, lifecycle economics, high-value patterns, and value-at-risk without reducing value to revenue alone.
- **[Adoption Optimization](../../systems/customer-optimization/workflows/intervention/adoption/CONTEXT.md)** — Build sustained value-driving adoption.
- **[Customer Success Process Optimization](../../systems/customer-optimization/workflows/intervention/customer-success/CONTEXT.md)** — Improve customer-success process mechanics.
- **[Churn Diagnosis & Prevention](../../systems/customer-optimization/workflows/intervention/churn/CONTEXT.md)** — Diagnose and reduce avoidable churn.
- **[Retention Optimization](../../systems/customer-optimization/workflows/intervention/retention/CONTEXT.md)** — Improve durable retention through customer value.
- **[Renewal Optimization](../../systems/customer-optimization/workflows/intervention/renewal/CONTEXT.md)** — Improve healthy renewal readiness/progression.
- **[Repeat Purchase Optimization](../../systems/customer-optimization/workflows/intervention/repeat-purchase/CONTEXT.md)** — Increase appropriate repeat purchase.
- **[Upsell / Cross-Sell Optimization](../../systems/customer-optimization/workflows/intervention/upsell-cross-sell/CONTEXT.md)** — Improve appropriate additional purchase progression.
- **[Account / Customer Expansion Optimization](../../systems/customer-optimization/workflows/intervention/expansion/CONTEXT.md)** — Expand successful customer relationships when eligible.
- **[Referral Optimization](../../systems/customer-optimization/workflows/intervention/referral/CONTEXT.md)** — Generate advocacy at appropriate value moments.
- **[Service Recovery](../../systems/customer-optimization/workflows/intervention/service-recovery/CONTEXT.md)** — Recover active failures and prevent recurrence.
- **[Customer Churn Risk Monitoring](../../systems/customer-optimization/workflows/monitoring/churn-risk/CONTEXT.md)** — Review interpretable churn-risk evidence and distinguish risk likelihood from value-at-risk; recurrence belongs to the active runtime.
- **[Lifecycle Experiment Design](../../systems/customer-optimization/workflows/experimentation/lifecycle-test/CONTEXT.md)** — Design or analyze a bounded journey intervention test when experimentation materially improves the decision.
- **[Cohort Retention Analysis](../../systems/customer-optimization/workflows/measurement/cohort-retention/CONTEXT.md)** — Understand retention patterns across cohorts.
- **[Customer Journey Health Monitoring](../../systems/customer-optimization/workflows/monitoring/journey-health/CONTEXT.md)** — Run a bounded journey-health review for material deterioration, improvement, or unresolved evidence; recurrence belongs to the active runtime.
