# Funding & Impact

Secure grants, donations, volunteers and investment, and show honestly what the work achieves.

**Ask for the outcome in normal language.** Playbooks below are useful end-to-end frames, not commands the user must select. The model may use their entry Workflows, other AURA Workflows, external Skills, or another sound method.

## Playbooks

### Grant Funding

Find grants the organization is eligible for and likely to win, write funder-aligned applications from verified facts, manage awards, and learn from each decision.

*Try:* “Find grants we qualify for and help us apply to the best ones.”

- [Find grants](../../systems/funding-impact/workflows/grants/prospecting/CONTEXT.md)
- [Prepare a case for support](../../systems/funding-impact/workflows/case/case-for-support/CONTEXT.md)
- [Write an application](../../systems/funding-impact/workflows/grants/application/CONTEXT.md)
- [Manage an award](../../systems/funding-impact/workflows/grants/award-management/CONTEXT.md)

### Supporter Fundraising

Raise and keep support from individual donors, members and volunteers through honest appeals, prompt thanks, reporting back and relationship building.

*Try:* “Help us keep more of our donors and plan our year-end appeal.”

**Common entry Workflow:** [Donor Stewardship and Retention](../../systems/funding-impact/workflows/supporters/stewardship/CONTEXT.md)

### Impact Measurement and Reporting

Decide which outcomes show the difference the work makes, measure them practically, and report results honestly to supporters and funders.

*Try:* “Help us show the impact of our programs with real numbers.”

**Common entry Workflow:** [Impact Measurement](../../systems/funding-impact/workflows/impact/measurement/CONTEXT.md)

### Business Financing and Investor Fundraising

Choose a financing route, prepare the evidence a lender or investor needs, and follow the request through diligence, agreement and funding.

*Try:* “Help us prepare for and run our seed round.”

**Common entry Workflow:** [Business Financing and Investor Fundraising](../../systems/funding-impact/workflows/investment/raise/CONTEXT.md)

## Common Workflows

These reusable procedures may support one or more Playbooks or be used independently. They are not a mandatory sequence.

- **[Case for Support and Grant-Ready Facts](../../systems/funding-impact/workflows/case/case-for-support/CONTEXT.md)** — Keep a reusable, evidence-backed case for support and grant-ready facts current.
- **[Grant Prospecting and Fit](../../systems/funding-impact/workflows/grants/prospecting/CONTEXT.md)** — Find and prioritize grants the organization is eligible for and likely to win.
- **[Grant Application](../../systems/funding-impact/workflows/grants/application/CONTEXT.md)** — Write a funder-aligned application and learn from the decision.
- **[Award Management and Reporting](../../systems/funding-impact/workflows/grants/award-management/CONTEXT.md)** — Meet award reporting and compliance obligations and build the funder relationship.
- **[Fundraising Appeal](../../systems/funding-impact/workflows/supporters/appeal/CONTEXT.md)** — Plan and write a fundraising appeal or giving campaign.
- **[Donor Stewardship and Retention](../../systems/funding-impact/workflows/supporters/stewardship/CONTEXT.md)** — Retain donors and deepen their support through thanks, reporting back and cultivation.
- **[Volunteer Recruitment and Retention](../../systems/funding-impact/workflows/supporters/volunteers/CONTEXT.md)** — Recruit, welcome and keep volunteers.
- **[Impact Measurement](../../systems/funding-impact/workflows/impact/measurement/CONTEXT.md)** — Define the outcomes that matter and how to measure them.
- **[Impact Report](../../systems/funding-impact/workflows/impact/report/CONTEXT.md)** — Report impact honestly to supporters and funders.
- **[Business Financing and Investor Fundraising](../../systems/funding-impact/workflows/investment/raise/CONTEXT.md)** — Choose a financing route and prepare the lender or investor request through funding.
## Working principle

Use the fewest instructions and Workflows necessary to repeatedly achieve the desired outcome at the required truth and quality standard. Preserve non-obvious expertise, evidence requirements, and real constraints; let the capable model/harness choose implementation details, tools, providers, external Skills, and orchestration unless the implementation itself matters.
