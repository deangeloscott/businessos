# Marketing Synthesis

Create and improve persuasive strategy, campaigns, offers, and customer-facing marketing assets.

**Ask for the outcome in normal language.** Playbooks below are useful end-to-end frames, not commands the user must select. The model may use their entry Workflows, other AURA Workflows, external Skills, or another sound method.

## Playbooks

### Marketing Strategy and Messaging

Develop or improve positioning, messaging, value proposition, mechanism, proof, objection handling, and offer presentation around current customer and business truth.

*Try:* “Improve how we position and explain this offer so qualified buyers understand why it matters.”

**Common entry Workflow:** [Messaging Architecture](../../systems/marketing-synthesis/workflows/strategy/messaging/CONTEXT.md)

### Campaign Development

Build a coherent campaign concept and the useful persuasive work needed to carry it across the relevant customer-facing surfaces.

*Try:* “Build a campaign around our strongest customer insight and offer.”

**Common entry Workflow:** [Campaign Concept Development](../../systems/marketing-synthesis/workflows/campaigns/campaign-concept/CONTEXT.md)

### Offer Design & Optimization

Diagnose and improve how value is packaged, priced, de-risked, and presented so the offer is more compelling without inventing unsupported value or urgency.

*Try:* “Improve the offer itself, not just the copy around it.”

**Common entry Workflow:** [Offer Diagnosis](../../systems/marketing-synthesis/workflows/offer/diagnosis/CONTEXT.md)

### Advertising Creative & Copy

Create persuasive ad concepts/copy/creative requirements matched to audience, awareness, funnel/journey role, channel/placement context, current field evidence where useful, and destination.

*Try:* “Create the ads we need for this job.”

**Common entry Workflow:** [Advertising Creative & Copy](../../systems/marketing-synthesis/workflows/assets/ads/CONTEXT.md)

## Working principle

Use the fewest instructions and Workflows necessary to repeatedly achieve the desired outcome at the required truth and quality standard. Preserve non-obvious expertise, evidence requirements, and real constraints; let the capable model/harness choose implementation details, tools, providers, external Skills, and orchestration unless the implementation itself matters.
