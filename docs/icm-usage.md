# ICM-Style Usage

AURA is inspired by In-Context Memory / filesystem-oriented agent principles: durable readable memory, minimal relevant context, human inspectability/editability, reusable operating knowledge, and deterministic utilities for mechanical integrity.

AURA applies those ideas to organizational continuity rather than building an execution runtime. Durable organizational objects preserve useful facts, evidence, decisions, Assets, measurements, Learning, unresolved work, and other meanings that future work should remember. Playbooks and Workflows preserve reusable business operating knowledge.

A capable model/harness can retrieve the relevant organizational memory and operating knowledge, perform the work with its normal tools and Skills, then preserve the durable meaning that should survive the session. Different models/providers may produce different quality, but AURA does not depend on one model vendor or require its own provider/tool control plane.

Operating areas such as Customer Intelligence, Competitor Intelligence, Industry Intelligence, SEO/AEO, Content Synthesis, Marketing Synthesis, and Customer Optimization are bodies of reusable knowledge and domain-owned organizational meaning. They can reuse shared evidence directly when relevant; AURA does not require an internal event bus, routing network, service contract, or duplicated cross-domain state to connect them.
