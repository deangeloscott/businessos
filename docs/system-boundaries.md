# System Boundaries

| Fundamental question | Canonical owner |
|---|---|
| What is true about the business? | Core |
| What do customers want, need, believe, choose, reject, and say? | Customer Intelligence |
| What are competitors doing and what does it mean competitively? | Competitor Intelligence |
| What changed externally and why does it matter? | Industry Intelligence |
| How do we win valuable organic discovery? | SEO/AEO |
| How should this idea be communicated for this audience/platform/medium? | Content Synthesis |
| How should intelligence be turned into persuasion and commercial action? | Marketing Synthesis |
| How can customer progression and value realization improve? | Customer Optimization |

Artifact type does not determine Opportunity ownership. A landing page can be SEO-, Marketing-, or Customer Optimization-owned depending on the diagnosed business problem; Content may execute production.
