# A weekly client report

A fictional example for a marketing team deciding whether to keep a shorter inquiry form.

**Week ending October 23, 2026**  
The inquiry page received 240 visits and 18 completed forms. The previous week it received 250 visits and 15 forms. Completion rose from 6% to 7.5%. Source: the two weekly page and form exports.

**What changed after review**  
The sales lead marked two of this week's entries as duplicates. The corrected count is 16 distinct inquiries. The form still received 18 submissions. Qualified leads and sales are unknown because the CRM export is missing.

**Decision**  
Keep the shorter form for another week. Ask sales for the CRM export before shifting budget. With two weeks of data, we cannot tell how much the form change contributed.

### Use this setup

[Open Weekly client reporting on the Innovation Exchange](https://viraltrac.com/aura/community/?contribution=example-client-reporting) and choose **Download package**. In AURA, open **Settings → Share or import → Import a package**, review the method and choose your workspace. Only the reusable instructions are in the package.

To start your own report, give your AI your reporting period, sources and the decision the report should inform. For example, “Use Weekly client reporting with these two exports. Should we keep the shorter form?”
