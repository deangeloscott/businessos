This organization sells its expertise or sells to other businesses. Sales usually involve fewer, larger decisions, several people on the buying side, and trust built through proof. Find out who the ideal client is, how they buy, how long it takes, and where good clients have come from, and remember them.

Case studies, clear positioning, referrals and timely outreach tend to matter more than broad reach. Keep each client's private information private; share results only as the client permits.
