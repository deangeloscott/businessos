This organization pursues a mission rather than profit. How it is funded varies widely (individual donors, membership, earned revenue, events, grants, government contracts), so find out its actual funding mix before assuming any one path, and remember it. Grants have their own focus area, used only when grants matter.

Supporters, volunteers, beneficiaries and partners are distinct audiences. Impact reporting should use numbers the organization can support. Protect beneficiaries' privacy and dignity in any story or data.
