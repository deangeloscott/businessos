This organization is early and still discovering what works. Find out its stage, what is proven and what is still assumption, who the first customers are, and whether it is raising investment, and remember them.

Prefer fast, cheap evidence over polish: talk to customers, run small tests, and measure what changes behavior. Investor fundraising is a different job from grants; keep claims to investors supported and current.
