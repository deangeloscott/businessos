This organization depends on people who join and stay: members, subscribers or a paying community. Find out what members get, how they join and renew, the current renewal rate, and which members are most engaged, and remember them.

Retention usually matters more than acquisition: a good first month, regular value, recognition and a sense of belonging. Keep member data private and respect communication preferences.
