# Start Here — ViralTrac AURA

**Alpha · v0.1.0**  
**AURA = Agentic Understanding and Reinforcement Architecture.**

AURA is the organization-owned memory and operating-knowledge layer around a capable AI. It helps the AI understand the business, reuse relevant evidence and prior work, apply useful playbooks, and preserve what should matter later.

AURA is **not** the model, agent harness, tool runtime, scheduler, provider router, permission system, or business decision-maker.

## The normal way to use AURA

### 1. Give the folder to a capable AI/agent

Download/unzip AURA or clone the repository, then give the AI/agent harness access to the folder.

### 2. Tell it about the business and what you want

For example:

> Use ViralTrac AURA for my business. My company is Acme, our website is acme.com, and I want to increase qualified leads. Use what we already know, figure out the highest-value work, and help me get it done.

If useful context already exists in AURA, the agent should reuse it instead of asking again.

### 3. Talk normally

You do not need to pick a playbook first. Try things like:

- “What should we work on first?”
- “What are our competitors doing better than us?”
- “Why are customers dropping out before buying?”
- “What changed in our industry that matters?”
- “Find our biggest SEO/AEO opportunities.”
- “Turn this customer insight into a strong campaign.”
- “Create a presentation for this proposal.”
- “What have we learned from the work we've already done?”

## What AURA should do underneath

For useful organizational work, the agent should generally:

1. identify the active organization;
2. retrieve the smallest relevant AURA context, evidence, prior decisions, results, and Learning;
3. use an AURA playbook when it is a strong fit without forcing the work into it;
4. use the harness's real tools, models, Skills, subagents, integrations, and other capabilities normally;
5. do the substantive work;
6. preserve only material organization-owned meaning that future work would benefit from;
7. keep AURA state truthful, traceable, and isolated to the correct organization;
8. measure outcomes and promote Learning when evidence supports it.

The model/harness remains responsible for browsing, files, APIs, rendering, credentials, scheduling, retries, provider choice, orchestration, and other execution mechanics.

## What you may still be asked for

The agent should ask when a material business fact, credential, real external permission/scope boundary, or consequential business choice cannot be responsibly resolved from the request, AURA, or available systems.

Unknown information should remain unknown rather than being invented.

## Alpha status

AURA's architecture and product-integrity layer are validated, but the product is still being actively improved through real-work qualification. Playbooks, retrieval, Learning, interfaces, and usability may change before 1.0.

## Optional enhancements

AURA can benefit from stronger or specialist models, browser/search/API access, CRM/analytics/CMS tools, external Skills/SOPs, private Git, shared workspaces, long-running harnesses, or ViralTrac. None is a prerequisite for the basic local/filesystem experience.
