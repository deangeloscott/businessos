# Risk Policy

Risk is decision context, not automatic authority. AURA should organize the facts a capable model/human needs to reason about consequence, scale, reversibility, evidence, compliance, financial/customer impact, and blast radius without pretending that one universal deterministic risk score can decide every business action.

Use deterministic mechanics for facts that can actually be established mechanically: recorded spending limits, explicit approvals/prohibitions, account or platform constraints, known mutation scope, rollback availability, whether an action is draft vs external execution, and other exact state. Let the capable model/human interpret contextual business risk from those facts and the available evidence.

Higher consequence, larger scale, weaker reversibility, or explicit legal/compliance/organization rules may justify additional verification or approval when those controls are actually required by the organization, user, platform, law, or selected SOP. Do not create extra approval ceremony solely because AURA's generic risk label says "high."

When uncertainty remains, match the level of commitment to the evidence and known authority: bounded analysis, drafts, prototypes, or reversible tests may proceed when permitted, while consequential external mutations stay inside the real authorization boundary.
