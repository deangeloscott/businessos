# AURA Work Evidence and Artifact Verification

AURA distinguishes **useful work exists** from **strong evidence supports what we say about that work**.

This policy provides reusable structural checks for artifacts, QA, research, measurement, detectors, and other outputs when deterministic verification adds value. It does **not** create an execution lifecycle around a playbook. Selecting an AURA playbook records useful operating knowledge/method provenance; it does not create a contract manifest, subcontract ledger, permission gate, or stronger class of Run.

A Run is an optional organizational work receipt. `scripts/complete_run.py` records material continuity for any method. Completing that receipt does not certify playbook conformance, QA, production readiness, deployment, authorization, or business outcomes.

## Rules

1. **The playbook defines useful operating knowledge; `writes` describe possible durable outputs, not quotas or permissions.** Persist an Opportunity, WorkRequest, Asset, ChangeEvent, measurement object, or other canonical object only when that meaning actually exists and will help future organizational work. Do not create state merely because a playbook lists the type.
2. **Use reusable structural evidence profiles only where they improve truthfulness.** `scripts/completion_evidence.py` may classify/check evidence shapes such as production, QA, detector, publishing, research, measurement, planning, canonical-state, and generic evidence. These are verification helpers, not an execution engine.
3. **Structural validation is a minimum, not a quality score.** A structurally valid file can still be strategically weak, inaccurate, unpersuasive, incomplete for the user, or commercially ineffective. The capable model, relevant operating knowledge, actual evidence, and substantive review remain responsible for quality.
4. **Production evidence must match the promised medium.** If the requested result is an article, image, presentation, video, audio piece, page, or other medium, verify the artifact that actually exists rather than trusting an extension or completion statement. A generic outline or description of future work is not the finished medium.
5. **Graceful degradation must remain truthful and useful.** When the active environment cannot render the requested medium, a complete production-ready specification/source package may still be useful if the task and playbook genuinely support that fallback. State clearly that final rendering did not occur. Do not invent an AURA Manual Action Packet/Package or claim unavailable media was rendered, recorded, exported, published, or scheduled.
6. **QA must show real inspection, not merely a verdict.** Where a structured QA record is useful, identify the exact artifact/version or target, what was checked, the actual method, concrete findings/evidence, issues/corrections/limitations, and per-check outcomes. Absent features are `not_applicable` with a reason rather than fictional passes.
7. **Artifact QA, readiness, deployment, authorization, and outcomes are separate facts.** A truthful draft may pass the checks applicable to its current version while still awaiting business facts, external permission, missing host capability, deployment, or later measurement. QA may recommend against publication because of a material defect; AURA does not become the publication authority.
8. **Intelligence must preserve enough evidence to support material conclusions.** Durable Observations, Insights, or Learning should remain traceable to the evidence that matters. For consequential synthesis, preserve source support, important limitations/uncertainty, contrary evidence where relevant, and unresolved gaps that could change the decision. Do not manufacture ceremonial analysis records solely to satisfy a framework.
9. **No-finding is a valid detector result when it is auditable.** A detector does not need to manufacture an Opportunity, Incident, or other downstream object. Preserve what was checked and the evidence supporting the no-finding conclusion when future work benefits from it.
10. **Integrated work does not require duplicate paperwork.** One genuine artifact or evidence package may demonstrate several pieces of work when it actually contains them. Do not create byte-different copies, duplicate canonical objects, separate internal jobs, or artificial files merely to prove each conceptual step occurred.
11. **Canonical-result expectations depend on real meaning, not bookkeeping.** Research, measurement, publishing, intelligence, detector, or canonical-state work often creates useful durable state because persistence is part of the business value. Planning or creative work may be complete without creating a canonical object if no durable organizational meaning needs preservation.
12. **Deterministic rejection should protect a real invariant.** Use mechanical validation for things such as schema shape, resolvable references, literal claim support, media integrity, exact-version QA targeting, or impossible contradictory state. Do not use deterministic helpers to decide semantic quality, strategy, evidence sufficiency, provider choice, permission, or whether a capable model is allowed to continue working.
13. **Independent qualification evaluates whether playbooks actually produce excellent work.** Qualification may use these evidence profiles and substantive rubrics to test playbooks, but qualification mechanics are not production runtime requirements. Do not make users or agents reproduce qualification paperwork during ordinary work.
14. **Optional receipts preserve continuity only.** If a Run is useful, record the method actually used plus material evidence, results, decisions, unresolved work, and a concise summary. The receipt may link a playbook by ID; it never creates or certifies an internal execution graph.

## Graceful-degradation examples

- animation: rendered motion, or a complete scene/keyframe/timing/transition specification when rendering is unavailable and that specification remains useful;
- presentation: rendered slides, or a complete slide-by-slide production specification appropriate to audience, setting, and desired duration;
- short/long video: rendered video, or a complete production specification with visual/audio/scene or beat execution detail appropriate to the requested duration;
- podcast: recorded audio, or a complete recording/edit specification whose script, timing, cues, and packaging are internally consistent.

A generic outline or unrelated Markdown document is not graceful degradation. Deterministic media checks may establish that an artifact is structurally usable; they do not establish that the creative work is professionally excellent.
