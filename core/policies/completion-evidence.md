# AURA Work Evidence and Artifact Verification

AURA distinguishes **useful work exists** from **what can be verified mechanically about that work**.

Deterministic evidence helpers are optional structural utilities. They do not judge business meaning, strategy, professional quality, persuasion, evidence sufficiency, or whether a capable model is allowed to continue working.

A Run remains an optional organizational work receipt. A receipt, structural check, or Workflow reference does not certify Playbook conformance, production readiness, deployment, authorization, or business outcomes.

## Rules

1. **Workflow knowledge guides work; it does not create evidence quotas.** `reads` and `writes` describe organizational knowledge a Workflow may use or preserve. Create durable state only when that meaning actually exists and will help later.
2. **Structural checks must be explicit.** `scripts/completion_evidence.py` does not infer a verification profile from Workflow ids, folders, names, historical roles, or domain labels. A Workflow may explicitly request a narrow structural check when it genuinely improves truthfulness.
3. **Mechanical validation protects mechanical facts.** Appropriate checks include: referenced files exist, media is structurally decodable, a QA record points to an existing exact Asset/version, declared evidence references resolve, or an explicitly-required durable object type is actually present.
4. **Mechanical validation does not understand the work.** Do not use keyword lists, token overlap, home-grown taxonomies, regex semantics, Workflow-name inference, or fixed content templates to decide whether research is insightful, a strategy is sound, a claim is supported in meaning, QA is professionally adequate, or a deliverable is excellent.
5. **Production evidence should truthfully match what exists.** If a rendered image, presentation, video, audio file, or other medium is claimed to exist, structural checks may verify the file really has that form rather than trusting its extension or a completion statement.
6. **Graceful degradation is a model/user judgment unless a Workflow explicitly permits a structural fallback.** A source package or production specification can be useful when rendering is unavailable, but deterministic AURA should not try to score whether that specification is creatively or professionally sufficient.
7. **QA evidence and QA quality are different.** A structural helper may verify that a QA record identifies the real Asset/version and contains structured checks. The capable model, user, relevant Workflow knowledge, and substantive qualification determine whether the inspection was actually adequate.
8. **Durable intelligence must remain traceable, but no ceremonial analysis record is required.** Preserve sources, observations, limitations, contrary evidence, uncertainty, and Learning when they matter. Do not force every research or intelligence task into a fixed `method/evidence_sample/findings/recommended_actions` record shape.
9. **No-finding is ordinary reasoning, not a special detector lifecycle.** If nothing material is found, preserve the evidence or conclusion only when future continuity benefits. Do not manufacture Opportunities, Incidents, or detector receipts.
10. **One real artifact can satisfy several conceptual pieces of work.** Do not create duplicate files, canonical objects, QA wrappers, or internal jobs simply to prove that each conceptual Workflow step occurred.
11. **Truth boundaries stay strict.** Artifact validity, production readiness, deployment, authorization, measured outcomes, and causal claims remain distinct facts. Structural verification of one does not imply another.
12. **Qualification is where substantive quality is tested.** Real-work qualification should evaluate whether a capable AI using AURA actually produces excellent, useful, grounded work. Qualification rubrics and evaluator reasoning must not become production runtime requirements.
13. **AURA never becomes the semantic authority.** The model/user determines relevance, interpretation, sufficiency, priority, strategy, composition, and next action. Deterministic AURA protects stored truth and structural integrity.

The governing test is simple:

> **Can AURA know this mechanically without trying to understand the business meaning of the work?**

If yes, a deterministic check may be useful. If not, leave the judgment to capable intelligence and substantive qualification.
