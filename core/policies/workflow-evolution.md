# Workflow Evolution and Organizational Learning

AURA may turn strong evidence-supported Learning into better reusable Workflow knowledge without converting one successful result into a universal rule.

## Use the narrowest useful memory

1. **Learning** — remember a conditional evidence-supported finding.
2. **Reusable guidance** — reuse that Learning directly when its conditions match.
3. **Workflow extension / local Workflow** — encode a repeatable organization-scoped procedure when a procedure adds real value beyond the Learning itself.
4. **Broader product candidate** — propose a reusable AURA improvement when evidence suggests the change generalizes beyond one organization.
5. **Canonical AURA change** — deliberate product-development work, validated before release.

This is an evidence/scope ladder, not an authority ladder. A capable model may still choose a different sound method for a particular job.

## Proposal requirements

A `WorkflowEvolutionProposal` should:
- cite the Learning and material evidence that motivated it;
- preserve contradictory evidence and limitations;
- state when the method applies and does not apply;
- keep durable domain/process guidance separate from incidental tool or provider details;
- define only the instructions and verification criteria that materially improve repeatability or quality;
- identify whether it augments an installed Workflow, creates a local Workflow, or suggests a canonical revision;
- stay at the narrowest scope justified by evidence.

Do not encode a host tool inventory or provider-neutral capability ontology. Describe the work in natural language and let the active model/harness choose the best available implementation.

## Adoption

A business-scoped proposal becomes active organization knowledge when an actor intentionally adopts it. AURA does not create another Approval object, autonomy gate, risk tier, provider preflight, or execution lifecycle around that choice.

Adoption makes the Workflow knowledge available; it does not force future models, harnesses, or users to use it. A canonical revision remains product-development work until AURA source is deliberately changed and validated.

## Re-evaluation

Revisit an extension when new outcomes contradict it, the mechanism materially changes, applicability changes, installed AURA knowledge supersedes it, or the organization retires it. Evidence can strengthen, narrow, deactivate, replace, or invalidate the method.
