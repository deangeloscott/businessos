# Approval Policy

Formal approvals are first-class records for actions requiring business, legal, regulatory, brand, financial, or risk authorization. Approval can be conditional or expire.

**Silence is not approval.** A clarification timeout, provider/tool timeout, user inactivity, or missing response must never be interpreted as authorization. When approval is required or the user's requested scope does not include implementation, preserve the pending decision/blocker and continue only with analysis/preparation that is independently authorized and does not make external/business-facing commitments.
