# Approval Policy

Formal approvals are first-class organizational records for actions that actually require business, legal, regulatory, brand, financial, account/platform, user, or other governing authorization. Approval can be conditional or expire.

AURA's role is to preserve and surface the real approval boundary and its current state. It should not manufacture a new approval requirement merely because AURA independently judges an otherwise authorized business choice to be risky. Risk, evidence strength, and reversibility can inform the capable model/human's decision and may trigger an approval when an organization/user/platform/legal rule or selected SOP actually requires one.

**Silence is not approval.** A clarification timeout, provider/tool timeout, user inactivity, or missing response must never be interpreted as authorization. When approval is genuinely required or the user's requested scope does not include implementation, preserve the pending decision/blocker and continue only with analysis/preparation that is independently authorized and does not make external/business-facing commitments.

Preferences and operating posture are not automatically approvals. Conversely, a valid standing authorization or explicit current-request permission should not be ignored simply because a generic AURA risk label would have chosen a more conservative path.
