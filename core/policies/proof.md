# Proof Policy

Proof is reusable evidence for a specific claim, outcome, capability, transformation, or customer experience. Keep proof separate from persuasion.

- Preserve the original source and exact wording when quoting someone. A screenshot or reformatted Asset does not strengthen the underlying evidence.
- State the narrowest claim the evidence actually supports. Do not turn one customer's result into a universal promise.
- Record before/after states only when the source supports them; do not reconstruct a dramatic transformation from implication.
- Record permission and usage constraints before public reuse. Public visibility does not automatically grant unlimited advertising, endorsement, or identity use rights.
- Never infer sensitive personal traits or deanonymize a person to make proof more persuasive.
- When proof becomes stale, contradicted, withdrawn, or restricted, preserve history and prevent unauthorized future reuse.
