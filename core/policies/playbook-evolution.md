# Playbook Evolution and Organizational Learning

AURA may turn strong evidence-supported Learning into better reusable operating knowledge without converting one successful result into a universal rule.

## Promotion ladder

Use the narrowest level justified by evidence:
1. **Learning** — remember a conditional finding.
2. **Reusable business guidance** — apply the Learning again when its conditions match.
3. **Process extension / local playbook** — encode a reusable organization-scoped method.
4. **Domain/system candidate** — propose broader AURA product guidance when evidence supports it.
5. **Canonical AURA change** — explicit product-development work, quality-tested before release.

This ladder is about evidence and scope, not authority over the executing intelligence.

## Proposal requirements

A PlaybookEvolutionProposal should:
- cite the Learning and material evidence that motivated it;
- preserve contradictory evidence/limitations;
- state `applies_when` and `does_not_apply_when`;
- distinguish evidence-supported method guidance from incidental implementation detail;
- declare provider-neutral capabilities only when useful;
- define the instructions and quality/verification checks that make the method valuable;
- identify whether it augments a playbook, creates a local playbook, or suggests a canonical revision;
- stay at the narrowest justified scope.

## Adoption

A business-scoped candidate becomes active only when an actor intentionally invokes the adoption path for that organization. AURA does not create a second `Approval` object, autonomy gate, risk tier, or provider/runtime preflight around that action.

Adoption makes the extension available as durable operational knowledge. It does not force future models/harnesses/users to use it. A `canonical_revision` is never auto-applied as ordinary business work; it remains a product-development candidate until AURA source itself is deliberately changed and validated.

## Re-evaluation

Revisit an extension when new outcomes contradict it, the underlying mechanism materially changes, applicability changes, a canonical playbook supersedes it, or the organization retires it. Success can strengthen the evidence; contradiction can narrow, deactivate, or supersede the method.
