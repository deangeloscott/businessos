# CAPABILITY-CAROUSEL-001 — Level 4 Carousel / Platform-Aware Media Execution

## Purpose
Test whether AURA can research the current platform context when it materially affects the work, then turn that evidence and the active business context into a real, professionally usable multi-image carousel rather than a generic slide outline.

This is a maintainer-side qualification case. The candidate must not see this file, the hidden target contract, evaluator criteria, checkpoints, receipts, prior runs, or scoring metadata.

## Layer
Level 4 — capability/media execution, with current platform-context research exercised as part of production.

## Hidden target
`content.production.carousel`

## Fixture
`atlasops-saas`

## Candidate-visible request
For AtlasOps, create a polished Instagram carousel for operations leaders on the topic “5 questions to ask before adding another field-service operations platform.” Make it educational first rather than a product brochure, while still fitting AtlasOps’s business, audience, and brand context. Use current Instagram platform guidance and a small credible sample of strong current B2B educational carousel examples where observable to inform the format, hook, sequence, dimensions, visual approach, and CTA. Distinguish directly supported platform behavior from uncertain algorithm advice or third-party opinion. Create the actual carousel as a post-ready series of image files, plus an editable/source or combined preview artifact if useful. Make the sequence genuinely worth swiping through and usable as customer-facing content. Do not invent AtlasOps product capabilities, integrations, customer outcomes, implementation promises, pricing, proof, or performance claims. Do not publish it or contact anyone.

## What Level 4 is testing
AURA should discover what the current host can actually do, refresh or create current platform context when needed, and choose the strongest truthful execution path.

The case is intentionally not given a fixed slide count, aspect ratio, hook formula, CTA pattern, or algorithm rule. The candidate should determine those from current evidence, business context, and the communication job rather than treating maintainer assumptions as answers.

Preferred execution order:
1. Inspect the active business/customer/brand context and determine the carousel’s audience job and desired action.
2. If current Instagram mechanics materially affect the work and no current PlatformProfile is available, refresh platform context from authoritative/current sources and direct observations where available.
3. Use a bounded sample of relevant current examples only as needed to understand useful creative mechanisms; do not infer profitability or universal algorithm advantage from visibility, engagement, or persistence alone.
4. Storyboard a sequential argument in which each frame has one primary job and earns the next swipe.
5. If the host can genuinely create/render the requested carousel, create the real multi-image artifact and inspect the actual exports.
6. If rendering is genuinely unavailable, produce the complete production-ready fallback required by the installed carousel playbook rather than pretending images were exported.
7. Preserve customer-facing claim governance and substantive pre-publish QA.

## Evaluation emphasis
The professional judge should distinguish actual platform-aware production from benchmark mimicry or paperwork.

- Platform research quality: Did the candidate use current, reconstructable evidence for consequential platform rules and distinguish authoritative behavior from speculation/folklore?
- Research proportionality: Was the sample sufficient to improve the work without turning production into endless research?
- Mechanism extraction: Did the candidate learn reusable creative/attention mechanisms rather than copy another creator’s expression?
- Sequential communication: Does the cover earn attention truthfully, does each frame have one clear job, and does the sequence build toward a useful synthesis/action?
- Native format fit: Are dimensions, density, typography, safe areas, frame count, CTA, and other format decisions appropriate to the current platform and audience based on the evidence actually found?
- Real media execution: Does a complete post-ready series of images exist when the host could create them, rather than a storyboard or one representative image masquerading as a carousel?
- Visual continuity: Are typography, palette, spacing, illustration/diagram language, numbering, and progression coherent across the full sequence without becoming monotonous?
- Mobile readability: Are the actual rendered frames readable at feed/mobile scale without clipped copy, tiny body text, or dense paragraph slides?
- Business and claim discipline: Is the work useful to AtlasOps’s audience without turning general operating guidance into unsupported AtlasOps product behavior or fabricated proof?
- QA and auditability: Were the actual frame exports inspected, claim-surface parity preserved for rendered media, and the exact completed Asset/version reviewed before Run completion?
- State integrity: Does the saved Asset/Run accurately represent the multi-file carousel and how it was produced?

## Pass interpretation
A strong pass demonstrates that AURA can combine current platform understanding, business context, visual-sequence judgment, real multi-file media production, factual governance, and final rendered QA in one ordinary user workflow.

This does not prove that any carousel will receive high reach or engagement after publication. Platform guidance, visible engagement, and current examples inform production decisions; real-world performance still requires publishing and measurement.

## Non-goals
- Do not require publication or an Instagram account connection.
- Do not require a proprietary AURA renderer or any specific image-generation vendor.
- Do not encode a universal slide count, aspect ratio, font size, or CTA formula into deterministic qualification gates.
- Do not treat third-party “algorithm hacks” as fact merely because they are widely repeated.
- Do not reward decorative complexity over clear sequential communication.
- Do not require copying current examples or creators.
