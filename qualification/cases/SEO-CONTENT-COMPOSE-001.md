# SEO-CONTENT-COMPOSE-001 — Evidence-Informed Organic Article

Status: planned

Qualification layer: 3 — composition quality

Benchmark business: AtlasOps

## Purpose

Test whether AURA helps a capable model carry current SEO/competitor/business evidence into an actually useful customer-facing article/page without forcing an internal domain-handoff lifecycle.

The useful composition under observation is:

1. establish enough current search/competitor/business evidence for the defined intent;
2. form or reuse the supported organic understanding needed for the task (an Opportunity is optional, not a prerequisite);
3. use `seo.planning.organic-content-requirements` or an equally sound method to produce decision-useful organic requirements without rediscovering the same research;
4. use Content Synthesis operating knowledge where helpful to produce the actual customer-facing article/page;
5. inspect the produced artifact against the material requirements, brand/business truth, and relevant content-quality checks;
6. preserve only useful durable evidence/results/brief state and a measurement or recheck intent where warranted, without claiming unobserved rankings, citations, traffic, leads, or revenue.

This is deliberately narrower than the full SEO/AEO domain mission. It answers one product question: **does AURA's shared organizational context and operating knowledge compound cleanly from evidence into requirements into a better finished asset?**

A WorkRequest, Run, particular playbook chain, or internal domain routing sequence is not required. If the candidate can do the work more directly while preserving the same evidence/quality/continuity benefits, that is a valid and potentially better result.

## Candidate-facing request

The ordinary request is maintained in `qualification/missions/missions.json` as `COMPOSE-SEO-CONTENT-001`. The candidate must not receive this case file, evaluator rubric, qualification directory, checkpoints, receipts, or scoring metadata.

## What the evaluator should inspect

### Composition integrity

- The search/competitor/business evidence used is real, current enough for the decision, and reconstructable.
- The organic requirements materially reflect that evidence and the AtlasOps business context rather than becoming generic SEO advice.
- The final article/page visibly satisfies the requirements that actually matter and does not silently discard useful differentiation, must-answer questions, evidence needs, business/brand constraints, CTA/business pathway, or material technical/AEO considerations.
- Current AURA state/evidence is reused rather than multiplying duplicate Insights, Opportunities, SourceRecords, briefs, or ownership artifacts.
- Relevant Content Synthesis quality/QA knowledge examines the actual produced artifact when it adds value; the evaluator must not require ceremonial subcontracts merely because they exist.
- The final user-facing result is an actual usable article/page artifact, not a requirements document or outline presented as production.
- Any persisted SEO requirements brief is a useful internal Asset or durable context. A WorkRequest is appropriate only if a real cross-session/person/model handoff actually needs to survive the runtime.

### Professional / competitive review

Independently inspect enough of the current search field to judge whether the final asset is genuinely informed by current competitive reality. Do not require the candidate to use the evaluator's exact sources if its evidence is equally credible.

Judge the composition as a system, not merely the prose quality of the final article:

- Did current evidence materially improve the requirements/strategy?
- Did those requirements materially improve the produced asset?
- Did the asset preserve AtlasOps's useful differentiation and factual/brand constraints?
- Would an SEO/content professional be able to use the result without rebuilding the strategy or article from scratch?
- Is the finished asset plausibly competitive for the intended search/AI-answer task while preserving uncertainty about authority, ranking, citation, and real-world outcomes?
- Did AURA add useful memory/operating knowledge rather than adding routing/coordination ceremony?

## Failure diagnosis

If the composition fails, classify the failure before changing AURA:

- model execution/randomness;
- missing or weak upstream evidence;
- organic-requirements/strategy weakness;
- Content Synthesis production/QA weakness;
- retrieval/state-reuse weakness;
- missing host capability;
- fixture/test design problem;
- evaluator/judge error.

Do not add benchmark-shaped product rules or a dedicated routing layer to make this case pass. Change AURA only if the run reveals a reusable real-world weakness in organizational context, evidence handling, operating knowledge, persistence, or continuity.

## Next step after this case

If this composition is professionally strong, move upward to broader capability/media execution or other realistic cross-domain missions rather than repeatedly hardening this exact scenario.
