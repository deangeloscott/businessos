# Expected outcome

Judge whether the result would give a real ecommerce team a credible, coherent launch package rather than a collection of generic marketing drafts.

A strong result should:

- ground positioning and offer choices in the supplied business/customer/product context and relevant current competitive evidence where useful;
- avoid unsupported product, performance, scarcity, customer, or comparative claims;
- produce a coherent launch concept that carries consistently across product/landing-page copy, email, and paid/social creative;
- make the core page persuasive, clear, specific, and usable rather than merely outlining sections;
- make the email sequence meaningfully sequenced rather than repeating the same pitch;
- make creative concepts/native drafts specific enough for a team to execute and appropriate to likely channels;
- preserve meaningful audience, offer, proof, objection, and messaging constraints across assets;
- include a practical measurement plan without pretending unobserved performance has occurred;
- create work a competent ecommerce marketer could actually use or refine without rebuilding the strategy from scratch;
- persist only durable organizational meaning that improves later work.

Equivalent or better execution is valid. Do not require a fixed number of emails/ads, a specific framework, an AURA Run, or exact Workflow composition.
