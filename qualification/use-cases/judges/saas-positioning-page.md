# Expected outcome

Judge whether the work would be genuinely useful to AtlasOps leadership deciding whether and how to move upmarket.

A strong result should:

- use existing AtlasOps/customer context rather than starting from generic SaaS assumptions;
- establish a credible current competitive set and inspect enough evidence to support meaningful positioning conclusions;
- distinguish observable competitor facts from inference and avoid unsupported claims about market leadership, conversion, profitability, or customer preference;
- identify a defensible target-audience/problem/value position rather than merely summarize competitor websites;
- explain the tradeoffs or evidence that make the proposed direction credible;
- turn the supported positioning into an actual usable homepage/landing-page draft rather than stop at strategy notes;
- preserve factual business constraints, meaningful proof, differentiation, and an appropriate CTA/path to value;
- produce work a competent B2B SaaS marketer or founder could review without rebuilding the core research or page;
- persist only durable organizational meaning that would materially help later work, without unnecessary bookkeeping.

Equivalent or better methods are valid. Do not require a particular Workflow order, Run, tool, number of competitors, or artifact structure.
