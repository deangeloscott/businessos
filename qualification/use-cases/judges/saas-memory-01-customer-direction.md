# Expected outcome

Judge whether the work creates a truthful, reusable customer/positioning understanding that a later fresh session could benefit from.

A strong result should:

- synthesize the supplied customer/business evidence rather than restate isolated observations;
- identify a supported segment/direction while preserving meaningful uncertainty or conflicting evidence;
- surface important customer problems, desired outcomes, objections, misunderstandings, and switching/buying considerations where supported;
- translate the evidence into clear positioning implications without inventing stronger certainty than the evidence supports;
- preserve durable organizational meaning that is likely to matter to later strategy/content/marketing work;
- avoid redundant or benchmark-shaped memory objects that add no future value.

Do not judge later-session retrieval yet; this stage establishes whether there is useful durable meaning worth carrying forward.
