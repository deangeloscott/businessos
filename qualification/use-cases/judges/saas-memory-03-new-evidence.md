# Expected outcome

Judge whether AURA/model handles changing organizational truth correctly when new evidence conflicts with or weakens earlier conclusions.

A strong result should:

- retrieve the prior customer/positioning understanding and compare it explicitly or substantively against the newly supplied evidence;
- distinguish what remains supported, what is weakened, what is contradicted, and what is still uncertain;
- update or supersede durable organizational meaning so later work is not dominated by stale conclusions;
- avoid both inertia (keeping the old direction because it already exists) and overreaction (discarding well-supported learning because of one weak signal);
- reprioritize the business direction based on the total evidence available now;
- revise the launch/growth work when the evidence materially warrants it;
- preserve provenance and uncertainty well enough that a later fresh session could understand why the organization changed its view.

Do not require a particular supersession object shape, Workflow order, or amount of rewritten material. Judge whether organizational understanding actually evolved correctly and usefully.
