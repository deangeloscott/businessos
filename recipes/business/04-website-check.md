# Check the website against memory

Use this when the organization's website, profile or listing may have drifted from what is now true.

## The idea

Prices change, suppliers change, and a page written last year keeps saying what was true then. The organization's saved facts, offers and approved claims are the current record. Reading the public page against that record finds the contradictions a visitor would trip over.

## Borrowable shape

**Read the live page → list each factual statement on it → match each to a saved fact, offer or claim → sort into matches, contradictions and statements with nothing saved behind them → report with the page text and the saved record side by side.**

Check the pages people act on first: prices, hours, the main offer, the frequently asked questions. Quote the page exactly and say when it was read. For each mismatch, say which side is likely stale and why, and what would confirm it.

## Tiny illustrative example

*The pricing page says the weekend loaf is $9.50. The saved offer says $9.00, confirmed by the owner three weeks ago. The FAQ says pre-orders close Thursday; the saved fact says Friday noon. The "about" page says all flour is local; the proof for that claim is past its review date.* This example is fictional.

## Adapt

A contradiction is a question for the owner, not a verdict: the page may be newer than the memory. Save a confirmed correction as the current fact, and raise the rest as items that need someone. Do not edit the live site as part of the check unless asked. To keep it from drifting again, ask the AI app to repeat the check on a schedule and report only real differences.
