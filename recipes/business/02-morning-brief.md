# A morning brief

Use this when someone wants to start the day knowing what changed, what needs them, and nothing else.

## The idea

A brief is useful when it is short and only reports what is real. Start from the organization's saved state, not from memory of yesterday's chat: `scripts/workspace_summary.py --business-id <id> --text` gives the recent changes, open items, deadlines, goals and watches in one step.

## Borrowable shape

**What changed since last time → what needs you, with the deadline → where the goals stand → one suggested next step.**

Lead with changes that came from a real check, and name the source and when it was checked. Put anything with a deadline before anything without one. Give goals as the latest recorded number against the target. End with one next step the person can approve in a word.

## Tiny illustrative example

*"Two things changed overnight. A rival cut its main product from $9 to $7.50 (their menu page, checked 05:40). A grant you are eligible for closes in 6 days; you meet 4 of 5 criteria and the fifth needs last year's revenue. Reviews: 87 of 100. Suggested next step: draft the grant application."* This example is fictional.

## Adapt

If nothing changed, say so in one line; do not fill the space. To repeat it daily, ask the AI app to schedule it with its own scheduler and to write each brief as a dated report. Delivery to a phone depends on the app: use one that can send messages if that matters. Never report a check that did not run, and never turn "no change found" into "no change" when a source could not be read.
