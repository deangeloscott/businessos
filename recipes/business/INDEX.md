# Showing and deciding

A small shelf for moments when a business wants to see where it stands, decide something, or show its work to someone else. Each recipe uses what the organization has already saved, and each keeps the difference between what is established, what is assumed and what is imagined.

## Choose a starting point

| Need | Open |
|---|---|
| Hear more than one view before a decision | [Second opinions from several models](01-second-opinions.md) |
| Know what changed and what needs someone | [A morning brief](02-morning-brief.md) |
| Publish the claims the business makes, with their proof | [A page of claims and proof](03-claims-and-proof-page.md) |
| Catch a website that no longer matches what is true | [Check the website against memory](04-website-check.md) |
| Show the business in one picture | [A portrait of the business](05-business-portrait.md) |
| Rehearse how customers might react | [An imagined customer panel](06-imagined-customer-panel.md) |
| Think a change through before making it | [A what-if before a decision](07-what-if.md) |

## Use with AURA

These are optional starting points, not required steps. Start from `scripts/workspace_summary.py --business-id <id> --text` when a short picture of the organization helps, then open the specific records a recipe needs. Anything a recipe imagines stays labelled as imagined: it is never saved as a finding, a customer view or a measured result.
