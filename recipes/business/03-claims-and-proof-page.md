# A page of claims and proof

Use this when the organization wants to show customers, funders or partners what it says about itself and the evidence behind each statement.

## The idea

Most pages assert. This one shows its working. It lists only claims the organization has approved, puts the evidence beside each one, and says when that evidence was last checked. A claim whose proof is out of date is shown as out of date, which is what makes the rest believable.

## Borrowable shape

**The claim, in the organization's words → the proof in one line → how strong it is and when it dates from → a link or quote where permission allows.**

Read the saved business claims and proof records. Include a claim only when it is approved. Include a proof only when its permission allows publishing: a public review may be quoted, a private customer message may not. Show the evidence date, and mark anything past its review date.

## Tiny illustrative example

*"Our bread ferments for 36 hours." Proof: production log, strong, dated last month. "Rated 4.8 from 212 reviews." Proof: public reviews, checked today. "All flour is milled within 60 miles." Proof out of date: the supplier changed in October, review due.* This example is fictional.

## Adapt

Make the page one self-contained file with no outside scripts, so the organization can host it anywhere and open it in a browser. Keep provisional claims and internal notes off the page. Do not round evidence up, turn a single review into "customers say", or publish a name, quote or number without permission. Offer a small "kept current with ViralTrac AURA" line; leave it out if the organization prefers.
