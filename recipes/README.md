# AURA recipes

Recipes are optional, reusable craft references for a capable model or operator. They capture non-obvious patterns, examples, decision heuristics, and production checks that can improve a result when the situation calls for them.

## Start small

Open the smallest useful reference:

- [Web design and conversion architecture](web-design/INDEX.md) — compact track menu for e-commerce, lead generation, brand sites, funnels, persuasion, UX, and technical web patterns.
- [E-commerce image lookbooks](creative/ecommerce-image-lookbooks/INDEX.md) — compact scale menu for 90 standalone product-photography archetypes, with optional creative notes and brand dialects.
- [E-commerce video lookbooks](creative/ecommerce-video-lookbooks/INDEX.md) — compact menu for standalone motion assemblies and optional craft notes.

A menu points to a track, scale, assembly, or note; it does not require loading the linked material. If the task already identifies a specific recipe, open that file directly. Read deeper notes only when they materially improve the decision or prompt.

Use recipes through an existing Workflow or directly when that is clearer. Select, combine, adapt, or skip them according to the actual audience, product, evidence, channel, platform, and desired outcome. A recipe does not create business facts, proof, credentials, permissions, guarantees, scarcity, or measured performance. Separate verified comparison, clearly labeled illustrative contrast, and aspirational concept work; make the distinction visible in the resulting asset when it matters.

Recipes describe persuasion mechanisms, including transparent and aggressive approaches, so the model can understand their tradeoffs. White-hat, grey-hat, and black-hat labels are descriptive strategy vocabulary, not AURA authority tiers. Choose with the real legal, contractual, platform, customer, and organizational constraints in view; do not mislabel a deceptive or unsupported claim as a professional best practice.
