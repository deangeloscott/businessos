# Scale 05 — Archetype 12: The Multi-Human Seating Capacity & Shoulder Clearance Blueprint

* **Visual job:** The Specimen
* **Illustrative examples:** 4-person outdoor barrel sauna, 6-seat modular sectional sofa, 8-seater solid timber dining table, dual-lounge cold plunge.

## The idea

Make capacity and personal space visible with a measured, representative occupancy scene. Show shoulders, knees, entry, and usable circulation rather than simply counting bodies; an elevation, cutaway, or clean interior view may be the clearest format. Use actual dimensions and capacity specifications for callouts, and let the people and product scale match the evidence.

## Borrowable execution

- **What to make felt:** Spacious ease: open relaxed shoulders, uncrowded posture, generous legroom, and clearly stated measured capacity when available.
- **One visual treatment:** Lens: 35mm Architectural Prime. Aperture: f/8 for uniform focus across all seats. Lighting: Bright, clean architectural interior illumination showing every bench and corner clearly.
- **Check:** Models must reflect the actual people and product scale. Add capacity and shoulder-clearance callouts only from measured dimensions.
