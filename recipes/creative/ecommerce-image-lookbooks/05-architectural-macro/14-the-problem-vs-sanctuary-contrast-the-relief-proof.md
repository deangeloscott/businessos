# Scale 05 — Archetype 14: The Problem vs. Sanctuary Contrast (The Relief Proof)

* **Visual job:** The Proof
* **Illustrative examples:** Freestanding outdoor sauna, cold plunge tub, deep modular ergonomic sectional, restorative sleep system.

## The idea

Make a problem-to-relief contrast immediate: show the cramped, stressful, or undesirable state beside the calmer experience the product is meant to enable. Choose a visual difference the product can actually address and keep the comparison legible across the intended crop. This can dramatize an emotional benefit without claiming a medical or guaranteed outcome.

## Borrowable execution

- **What to make felt:** A clear shift from stress or crowding into the calmer experience the product is meant to enable.
- **One visual treatment:** Lens: 50mm Prime. Aperture: f/5.6. Lighting: Controlled, directional lighting creating sharp contrast between the stressed state and the glowing sanctuary state.
- **Check:** Recommended grammar: left represents Problem/Stress and right represents the Serene Recovery Sanctuary. Keep the contrast legible, but adapt direction and panel ratio to the platform and narrative.
