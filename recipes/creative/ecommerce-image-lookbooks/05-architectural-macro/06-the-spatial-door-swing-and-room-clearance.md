# Scale 05 — Archetype 06: The Spatial Door-Swing & Room Clearance

* **Visual job:** The Specimen
* **Illustrative examples:** Frameless glass sauna door, insulated cold plunge lid, sectional storage chaise, folding gym power rack.

## The idea

Show the full operating footprint of a door, lid, drawer, panel, or other moving part in a real room. Make the access path and nearby obstacles easy to inspect, using measured geometry for the swing radius and required clearance. The elevated architectural view is one useful execution; a plan view, overlay, or in-use angle may communicate the same job better.

## Borrowable execution

- **What to make felt:** Practical spatial mastery: *"I can see exactly how the door swings; it will clear my window and wall easily."*
- **One visual treatment:** Lens: 35mm Tilt-Shift. Aperture: f/8. Lighting: Bright, even architectural room daylight clearly showing the floor boundary.
- **Check:** Door-swing radius and clearance zones must be geometrically accurate to CAD specs.
