# Scale 05 — Archetype 06: The Spatial Door-Swing & Room Clearance

> Standalone starting point from the [Scale 05 menu](../05-architectural-macro.md). Adapt the bracketed inputs to the actual product, evidence, platform, and tool; inspect generated geometry, text, and any implied performance claim.
>
> Optional context: [image library index](../INDEX.md) · [brand dialects](../DIALECTS.md)

* **Universal Class:** The Specimen
* **Example Products (Illustrative Guidance, Not an Allowlist):** Frameless glass sauna door, insulated cold plunge lid, sectional storage chaise, folding gym power rack.
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   PDP Slot 4 & Delivery De-risking. Prevents devastating delivery day returns by showing the full functional footprint required to operate the product in a real room.
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   A wide 45° elevated architectural perspective showing the product with its heavy tempered glass door swung completely open (or the cold plunge lid fully propped).
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 35mm Tilt-Shift.  
   * *Aperture:* f/8.  
   * *Lighting:* Bright, even architectural room daylight clearly showing the floor boundary.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   Practical spatial mastery: *"I can see exactly how the door swings; it will clear my window and wall easily."*
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   Door-swing radius and clearance zones must be geometrically accurate to CAD specs.
* **Director's Rig Prompt Blueprint:**
```text
[BLOCK 1: HERO SPATIAL CLEARANCE]: Commercial spatial clearance photograph of [HERO PRODUCT: Sauna, plunge, or sectional], occupying 75% of frame weight with its main access door swung completely open at 90 degrees.
[BLOCK 2: CLEARANCE DETAIL]: Clearly demonstrates the full physical swing radius and entry opening, showing ample clearance along the floor boundary.
[BLOCK 3: OPTICS]: 35mm architectural lens, 20-degree elevated perspective, f/8 aperture keeping the open door and main body in sharp focus.
[BLOCK 4: STAGE & LIGHT]: Clean 5200K architectural daylight illuminating the floor plane and boundary lines, soft ground shadows.
[BLOCK 5: EXCLUSIONS]: Negative: blocked entryways, warped floor lines, fish-eye distortion, messy garden hoses, clutter.
```
