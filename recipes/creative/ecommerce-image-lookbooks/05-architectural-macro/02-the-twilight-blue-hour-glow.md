# Scale 05 — Archetype 02: The Twilight / Blue Hour Glow

> A quick, adaptable visual starting point. Borrow the idea, change the product or treatment freely, combine it with another approach, or skip it when you already have a better direction.
>
> Optional context: [image library index](../INDEX.md) · [brand dialects](../DIALECTS.md)

* **Visual job:** The Habitat
* **Illustrative examples:** Freestanding cedar sauna, illuminated cold plunge tub, outdoor fire pit lounge, architectural pergola suite.

## The idea

Use the transition from cool ambient light to a warm product glow to make an evening ritual feel inviting. Preserve enough detail to understand the product and its materials while letting the light carry the mood. Blue-hour outdoor scenes, wellness interiors, and quiet living spaces can all express the same dusk-to-warmth idea.

## Borrowable execution

- **What to make felt:** Calm evening rest: warm amber light cutting through cool air, rising steam catching backlight, and a clear transition into the product’s ritual.
- **One visual treatment:** Lens: 45mm Tilt-Shift Prime. Aperture: f/4. Lighting: Ambient cool exterior twilight (6500K) balanced against radiant 2700K warm amber LED illumination glowing from beneath the sauna benches, inside the plunge water, or around the sofa perimeter.
- **Check:** Interior lighting must not blow out wood grain details; amber glow must roll off naturally across cedar and glass.
