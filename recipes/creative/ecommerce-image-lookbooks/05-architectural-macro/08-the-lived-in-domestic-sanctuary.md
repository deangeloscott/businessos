1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   Editorial Catalog & Homepage Hero. Proves the product integrates into daily family life rather than feeling like a sterile showroom exhibit.
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   An authentic high-end home: sectional sofa with a soft cashmere throw casually draped over an armrest, open art book on the coffee table, soft morning sunlight casting window shadows on the rug.
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 50mm Prime.  
   * *Aperture:* f/2.8 for gentle background falloff.  
   * *Lighting:* Motivated natural daylight (4500K) with warm ambient bounce.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   The deep feeling of "home": tactile upholstery softness, quiet morning coffee moments, timeless domestic relaxation.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   Candid lived-in props must be curated with luxury restraint; zero messy clutter.
