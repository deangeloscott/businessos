# Scale 05 — Archetype 08: The Lived-In Domestic Sanctuary

> A quick, adaptable visual starting point. Borrow the idea, change the product or treatment freely, combine it with another approach, or skip it when you already have a better direction.
>
> Optional context: [image library index](../INDEX.md) · [brand dialects](../DIALECTS.md)

* **Visual job:** The Habitat
* **Illustrative examples:** Modular linen sectional sofa, solid white oak dining table, freestanding soaking tub, architectural daybed.

## The idea

Show the product as part of believable daily life rather than as an untouched showroom object. Use a few purposeful traces of the routine—light, a book, a cup, a throw, or an activity—while keeping the product’s form and role obvious. The feeling should be lived-in and considered; the exact home, time of day, and props can follow the brand and buyer.

## Borrowable execution

- **What to make felt:** The deep feeling of "home": tactile upholstery softness, quiet morning coffee moments, timeless domestic relaxation.
- **One visual treatment:** Lens: 50mm Prime. Aperture: f/2.8 for gentle background falloff. Lighting: Motivated natural daylight (4500K) with warm ambient bounce.
- **Check:** Candid lived-in props must be curated with luxury restraint; zero messy clutter.
