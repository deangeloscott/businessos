# Scale 05 — Archetype 10: The Dimensional Orthographic Architecture

> A quick, adaptable visual starting point. Borrow the idea, change the product or treatment freely, combine it with another approach, or skip it when you already have a better direction.

> Optional context: [image library index](../INDEX.md) · [brand dialects](../DIALECTS.md)

* **Visual job:** The Specimen
* **Illustrative examples:** Modular sectional sofa footprint, outdoor sauna external dimensions, cold plunge floor layout, dining table seating plan.

## The idea

Give detail-oriented buyers a clean way to understand the product’s footprint and key dimensions before they order or plan a space. Use an orthographic elevation, plan, isometric, or simple measured overlay according to what needs to be decided. Keep labels and units tied to supplied dimensions; a neutral grid is only one visual treatment.

## Borrowable execution

- **What to make felt:** Total engineering clarity; professional CAD-level precision; zero guesswork before ordering freight delivery.
- **One visual treatment:** Perspective: True Orthographic (Zero perspective vanishing points; parallel projection). Lighting: Clean, uniform studio ambient.
- **Check:** Labels and units should be calculated from the supplied dimensions and formatted for the intended audience; use one consistent projection and readable callouts.
