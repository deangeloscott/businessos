# Scale 05 — Archetype 03: The Ergonomic Headroom & Seated Clearance

> A quick, adaptable visual starting point. Borrow the idea, change the product or treatment freely, combine it with another approach, or skip it when you already have a better direction.
>
> Optional context: [image library index](../INDEX.md) · [brand dialects](../DIALECTS.md)

* **Visual job:** The Specimen
* **Illustrative examples:** 2-person barrel sauna, freestanding soaking tub, modular sectional sofa, compact squat rack.

## The idea

Make physical comfort and clearance easy to judge with a real or representative person in the product. Show headroom, knee room, posture, and the space needed to enter or move, using supplied measurements when the image supports a fit claim. Choose the angle and callouts that answer the buyer’s actual concern rather than forcing a generic wide shot.

## Borrowable execution

- **What to make felt:** Complete bodily comfort and spaciousness: relaxed open shoulders, un-cramped posture, breathing room.
- **One visual treatment:** Lens: 28mm or 35mm Wide-Angle with zero wide-angle distortion (level camera angle). Aperture: f/5.6. Lighting: Warm diffused interior light that makes the measured headroom and natural legroom legible.
- **Check:** Model dimensions must reflect the actual model and product measurements. Add a clearance callout only after calculating it from those measurements.
