# Scale 05 — Archetype 04: The Structural Joinery & Timber Macro

> A quick, adaptable visual starting point. Borrow the idea, change the product or treatment freely, combine it with another approach, or skip it when you already have a better direction.
>
> Optional context: [image library index](../INDEX.md) · [brand dialects](../DIALECTS.md)

* **Visual job:** The Proof
* **Illustrative examples:** Red cedar sauna stave ball-and-socket joinery, cold plunge commercial refrigeration fittings, modular sofa solid oak frame, gym rack 11-gauge steel welds.

## The idea

Zoom in on the joint, edge, seal, or connection where build quality can be seen rather than merely asserted. Raking light and a close crop can make grain, welds, fasteners, or gasket contact feel tangible; choose the detail that matters for the actual product. A generic lower-quality reference can clarify the contrast when the supplied materials and construction are known.

## Borrowable execution

- **What to make felt:** The material character and solidity of the supplied wood, steel, glass, or seal; use a close detail and light that make the physical connection tangible.
- **One visual treatment:** Lens: 90mm or 105mm Macro. Aperture: f/5.6. Lighting: Raking cross-light grazing across natural timber grain rings, steel chamfers, and gasket seals to highlight physical density.
- **Check:** Grain patterns, wood species, and hardware materials should follow the physical product specs; when unknown, use a neutral close-up rather than a false species or grade.
