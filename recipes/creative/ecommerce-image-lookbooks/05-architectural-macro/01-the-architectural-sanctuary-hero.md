# Scale 05 — Archetype 01: The Architectural Sanctuary Hero

> A quick, adaptable visual starting point. Borrow the idea, change the product or treatment freely, combine it with another approach, or skip it when you already have a better direction.
>
> Optional context: [image library index](../INDEX.md) · [brand dialects](../DIALECTS.md)

* **Visual job:** The Habitat
* **Illustrative examples:** Red cedar outdoor barrel sauna, stainless steel cold plunge, modular velvet sectional, solid walnut dining table, commercial half-rack gym.

## The idea

Show the product at home in an aspirational interior or landscape without losing its scale, materials, or use. Let the architecture explain where it belongs and what kind of life it supports, while keeping the product the first thing a viewer can inspect. A cedar-and-stone outdoor setting or an open loft is a useful execution, not a requirement.

## Borrowable execution

- **What to make felt:** Quiet luxury and spatial sanctuary; the rich scent of cedar in cool air; the grounding weight of solid timber and stone.
- **One visual treatment:** Lens: 35mm Architectural Prime with **simulated tilt-shift vertical alignment** (all wall posts, window mullions, and door frames are visually straight with zero perspective keystoning). Aperture: f/8 for deep, expansive architectural sharpness. Lighting: Soft ambient daylight balanced with interior architectural cove lights.
- **Check:** Keep the product primary and its scale, materials, and relationship to the space easy to inspect; let the exact frame share follow the setting and placement.
