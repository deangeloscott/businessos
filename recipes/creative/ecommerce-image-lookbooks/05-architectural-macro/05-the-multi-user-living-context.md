> Optional context: [image library index](../INDEX.md) · [brand dialects](../DIALECTS.md)

* **Universal Class:** The Habitat
* **Example Products (Illustrative Guidance, Not an Allowlist):** 4-person outdoor sauna, extra-deep modular sectional sofa, 8-seater dining table, dual-seat cold plunge.
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   PDP Slot 3 & Social Lifestyle. Shows real social connection: couples or families enjoying the product together without overcrowding.
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   A 2-to-4 person wellness structure or living space with adults comfortably seated in relaxed conversation, or a modular sectional with ample lounging space.
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 50mm Prime.  
   * *Aperture:* f/4.  
   * *Lighting:* Natural, flattering room ambient light capturing authentic, candid human connection.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   Shared intimacy, emotional well-being, lifestyle upgrade; the joy of investing in a product that brings family together.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   Models must look genuinely relaxed and uncrowded. Seating space must reflect real product capacity ratings.
* **Director's Rig Prompt Blueprint:**
```text
[BLOCK 1: HERO HABITAT & SOCIAL]: Commercial lifestyle photograph of [HERO PRODUCT: e.g., 4-person cedar barrel sauna / sectional sofa], occupying 70% of frame weight in tack-sharp focus.
[BLOCK 2: SOCIAL CONTEXT]: Two adults are seated inside in comfortable, relaxed conversation with ample space between them, uncrowded and restored.
[BLOCK 3: OPTICS]: 45mm prime lens, eye-level perspective, f/4 aperture keeping the architecture and models in crisp focus.
[BLOCK 4: STAGE & LIGHT]: Warm 2800K ambient interior lighting balanced with diffused daylight through the tempered glass wall; serene natural surroundings.
[BLOCK 5: EXCLUSIONS]: Negative: cramped crowded poses, unnatural stiff smiles, cheap bathrobes, distorted limbs, cluttered patio background.
```
