# Scale 05 — Archetype 05: The Multi-User Living Context

> A quick, adaptable visual starting point. Borrow the idea, change the product or treatment freely, combine it with another approach, or skip it when you already have a better direction.
>
> Optional context: [image library index](../INDEX.md) · [brand dialects](../DIALECTS.md)

* **Visual job:** The Habitat
* **Illustrative examples:** 4-person outdoor sauna, extra-deep modular sectional sofa, 8-seater dining table, dual-seat cold plunge.

## The idea

Show how the product supports the number of people and the kind of interaction it promises. Give the people natural room to sit, move, and relate to one another so the scene communicates comfort rather than crowding. Use the supplied capacity and dimensions when the image is making a fit claim; otherwise keep the scene an illustrative lifestyle cue.

## Borrowable execution

- **What to make felt:** Shared intimacy, emotional well-being, lifestyle upgrade; the joy of investing in a product that brings family together.
- **One visual treatment:** Lens: 50mm Prime. Aperture: f/4. Lighting: Natural, flattering room ambient light capturing authentic, candid human connection.
- **Check:** Models must look genuinely relaxed and uncrowded. Seating space must reflect real product capacity ratings.
