# Scale 05 — Archetype 13: The Biophilic Alpine / Coastal Dawn Sanctuary

> Optional context: [image library index](../INDEX.md) · [brand dialects](../DIALECTS.md)

* **Universal Class:** The Habitat
* **Example Products (Illustrative Guidance, Not an Allowlist):** Red cedar barrel outdoor sauna, cold plunge tub, outdoor fire table, cantilevered teak daybed, architectural hot tub.
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   Hero Header / Brand Equity (Aspirational Wellness Sanctuaries). Commands the highest tier of emotional desirability, positioning the product as a restorative retreat embedded in wild nature.
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   A breathtaking natural landscape: misty Pacific Northwest pine forest, dramatic coastal cliff overlooking the ocean, or alpine meadow at sunrise. The product occupies 60%–65% of the frame, firmly commanding visual primacy.
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 45mm Tilt-Shift lens.  
   * *Aperture:* f/5.6.  
   * *Lighting:* Dawn golden-hour sun cutting through morning mist, warm amber interior illumination glowing through floor-to-ceiling tempered glass wall.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   Profound sensory calm: breathing cold mountain air while enveloped in soothing cedar warmth; total restorative escape.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   The structure must be 3x more prominent than the landscape; do not let mountains or trees drown out the hero product.
* **Director's Rig Prompt Blueprint:**
