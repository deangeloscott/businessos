* **Illustrative examples:** Red cedar barrel outdoor sauna, cold plunge tub, outdoor fire table, cantilevered teak daybed, architectural hot tub.

## The idea

Use a memorable natural setting to sell the feeling and place associated with the product. Keep the product large and recognizable enough to inspect, then let the landscape supply the emotional contrast—mist, coast, forest, or alpine light. The best setting is the one that belongs to the buyer and the brand, not a universal luxury backdrop.

## Borrowable execution

- **What to make felt:** A restorative sense of place: cool air, natural surroundings, and the product’s own warmth or material character working together.
- **One visual treatment:** Lens: 45mm Tilt-Shift lens. Aperture: f/5.6. Lighting: Dawn golden-hour sun cutting through morning mist, warm amber interior illumination glowing through floor-to-ceiling tempered glass wall.
- **Check:** Keep the product recognizable and primary enough to inspect while allowing the setting to carry the intended mood.
