# Scale 05 — Archetype 09: The Material Torture Test (Weather & Wear)

* **Visual job:** The Proof
* **Illustrative examples:** Outdoor barrel sauna, commercial cold plunge, marine-grade aluminum fire pit, stain-resistant performance fabric sectional.

## The idea

Let weather, handling, or everyday wear meet the surface so the material’s behavior becomes easy to imagine. Water beading, snow, abrasion, or a used contact area can make a durability story visual; use the real material and test data for any resistance claim. If the record is absent, present the scene as an illustrative stress moment rather than a passed test.

## Borrowable execution

- **What to make felt:** Long-term investment security: show the supplied weathering or care story without promising a lifespan that has not been established.
- **One visual treatment:** Lens: 70mm Prime. Aperture: f/5.6. Lighting: Natural outdoor overcast or crisp studio directional light highlighting water-beading surface tension.
- **Check:** Weathering resistance claims must be backed by real engineering data.
