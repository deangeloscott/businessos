# E-Commerce Image Lookbooks

A compact menu for 90 standalone visual starting points across six physical product scales. Choose the scale that matches the product, then open one archetype directly. The archetype file contains the usable prompt and local adaptation guidance; the optional notes add shared craft reasoning without loading the library all at once.

## Choose a scale

| Scale | Open when | Menu |
|---|---|---|
| Micro & intimate | Products under 6 inches: skincare, cosmetics, jewelry, fragrance, supplements, watches. | [Scale 01 menu](01-micro-intimate.md) |
| Handheld & tabletop | Products 6–18 inches: CPG, beverages, food, coffee, everyday carry, small tech. | [Scale 02 menu](02-handheld-tabletop.md) |
| Body-worn & apparel | Products worn on the body: apparel, footwear, eyewear, bags, activewear, outerwear. | [Scale 03 menu](03-body-worn-apparel.md) |
| Countertop appliances & heavy gear | Products 1.5–3 feet: espresso machines, ovens, power stations, blenders, tools. | [Scale 04 menu](04-countertop-appliances.md) |
| Architectural macro & high-ticket | Products 3–10+ feet: saunas, furniture, cold plunges, gyms, fire pits. | [Scale 05 menu](05-architectural-macro.md) |
| Mobility, vehicles & adventure transport | Products 4–12+ feet: e-bikes, scooters, cargo rigs, trailers, overland systems. | [Scale 06 menu](06-mobility-transport.md) |

## Choose by viewer job

| Viewer needs to… | Start with |
|---|---|
| See the product's form, size, contents, or fit | **The Specimen** — choose the matching scale menu, then open a packshot, scale anchor, fit, or dimensional archetype. |
| Understand a meaningful difference or answer an objection | **The Proof** — choose a documented demonstration, mechanism, or clearly illustrative generic contrast. |
| Imagine the product in a desirable environment | **The Habitat** — choose a scale menu's in-situ or architectural scene. |
| Feel the product's use, texture, or human contact | **The Ritual** — choose a tactile, pour, application, or motion-led scene. |

## Use with AURA

Recipes are optional craft references. Use the fewest files that materially help, adapt them to the actual product, evidence, platform, source material, and tool, and inspect the finished output. Existing AURA workflows own organizational truth and workflow-level QA; this library supplies reusable image direction.
