# Scale 03 — Archetype 13: The Hydrostatic Deluge & DWR Water-Beading Stress Test

* **Visual job:** The Proof
* **Good for:** making rain, water beading, wetness, and weather protection visually immediate.
* **Fits:** mountain shells, rain jackets, ponchos, all-weather pants, and alpine gear.

## The idea

Put the garment in a real-looking rain or spray situation and show the water behavior the viewer needs to understand. The storm creates pain and relief; the supplied test conditions determine whether it can be called proof.

## Borrow this treatment

Use a stable three-quarter wearer, clean technical silhouette, and water droplets that catch the light. A misty trail, alpine ridge, or coastal storm can carry the mood, but keep the jacket, hood, seams, and zippers visible. Avoid dramatic fabric extensions and impossible water paths.

## Make it yours

Use actual hydrostatic, DWR, seam, and wear evidence for performance language. Change the weather, activity, model, and composition for the audience. If it is an illustration, say so in the brief and inspect anatomy, fabric, and water behavior before use.
