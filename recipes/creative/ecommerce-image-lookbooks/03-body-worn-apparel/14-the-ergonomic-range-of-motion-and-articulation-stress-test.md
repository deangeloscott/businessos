# Scale 03 — Archetype 14: The Ergonomic Range-of-Motion & Articulation Stress Test

* **Visual job:** The Proof
* **Good for:** showing how a garment's seams, gussets, and hem behave during a meaningful movement.
* **Fits:** climbing jackets, stretch trousers, running shells, technical fleeces, and work pants.

## The idea

Capture one representative reach, step, climb, or bend that tests the garment's intended articulation. The viewer can see where the construction supports movement; a single pose cannot promise unrestricted motion for every body.

## Borrow this treatment

Choose a movement that exposes the relevant underarm gusset, articulated knee, elbow dart, waistband, or hem. Use a simple climbing wall, boulder, studio, or work setting and enough light to keep the construction visible. Freeze the action or allow a deliberate blur according to the story.

## Make it yours

Use the supplied pattern, fit evidence, and intended activity. Change the movement, environment, camera, and speed for the product. Inspect joints, fabric joins, and contact; describe observed behavior rather than inferring a universal comfort guarantee.

## Optional prompt seed

```text
Create an action image of the supplied [GARMENT] during a representative [REACH, STEP, CLIMB, OR BEND]. Make [GUSSET, ARTICULATED SEAM, WAISTBAND, OR HEM] visible as the body moves, with a clean pose and enough light to inspect the construction. Tie any mobility statement to supplied evidence and keep anatomy and fabric physically plausible.
```
