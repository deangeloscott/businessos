# Scale 03 — Archetype 01: The Ghost Mannequin & Architectural Studio Drape

* **Visual job:** The Specimen
* **Good for:** showing a garment's silhouette, construction, interior, and drape without a model's styling or pose changing the read.
* **Fits:** heavyweight tees, technical shells, merino sweaters, blazers, and denim jackets.

## The idea

Let the garment explain itself. An invisible or lightly suggested form can show the collar, sleeves, hem, lining, and hollow structure as a clean product object.

## Borrow this treatment

Use a neutral studio field and soft wraparound light so seams and textile weight read across the whole piece. Open the neck or one closure enough to reveal construction, but keep the silhouette calm and the garment grounded rather than floating.

## Make it yours

Use the supplied pattern, material, color, and construction. Choose a real mannequin, ghost effect, hanger, or flat lay when that is more faithful to the garment. Inspect proportions and fabric behavior; do not infer exact color or performance from a render.
