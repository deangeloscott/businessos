# Scale 03 — Archetype 01: The Ghost Mannequin & Architectural Studio Drape

> Optional context: [image library index](../INDEX.md) · [brand dialects](../DIALECTS.md)

* **Universal Class:** The Specimen
* **Example Products (Illustrative Guidance, Not an Allowlist):** Heavyweight cotton t-shirt, 3-layer technical shell, merino wool sweater, tailored wool blazer, denim jacket.
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   Shopify & Amazon Catalog Main (Slot 1). Provides complete, distraction-free view of garment silhouette, collar structure, sleeve drape, and hemlines without human model bias.
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   Pure seamless light-grey or neutral white cyclorama (RGB 248, 248, 248). The garment is styled on an invisible 3D ghost mannequin, showing interior neck label, lining, and authentic 3D hollow depth.
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 85mm Prime.  
   * *Aperture:* f/8 for uniform front-to-back fabric sharpness.  
   * *Lighting:* Soft wrap-around studio lighting (large overhead diffused bank + two side bounce panels) showing true fabric drape and seam construction without harsh hot spots.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   Visual weight and structural stiffness/softness of the textile (e.g. heavy 400 GSM French terry vs lightweight breezy linen).
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   Zero warping of garment proportions. Calibrated 5200K daylight balance ensuring fabric dye exactly matches physical Pantone under real sunlight.
* **Director's Rig Prompt Blueprint:**
```text
[BLOCK 1: HERO]: Commercial ghost mannequin packshot of [HERO PRODUCT: Garment type, color, textile material, GSM weight, collar/cuff construction]. Centered in frame, occupying 82% of frame height, showcasing natural 3D anatomical volume with hollow interior collar and neck label visible.
[BLOCK 2: OPTICAL RIG]: 85mm prime portrait lens, eye-level perspective, f/8 aperture for front-to-back edge sharpness from collar to hem.
[BLOCK 3: LIGHTING RIG]: Clean 5200K wrap-around studio softbox lighting with soft ambient fill, highlighting authentic fabric drape and micro-seam stitching with a soft contact floor shadow.
[BLOCK 4: SUBORDINATE STAGE]: Pure seamless neutral light-gray cyclorama background (calibrated RGB 248, 248, 248). Zero props, zero distractions.
[BLOCK 5: EXCLUSIONS]: Negative: human face, visible mannequin neck, plastic shine, distorted sleeves, uneven hemlines, wrinkled background.
```
