# Scale 03 — Archetype 07: Multi-Body Fit Diversity (Sizing Reassurance)

> Standalone starting point from the [Scale 03 menu](../03-body-worn-apparel.md). Adapt the bracketed inputs to the actual product, evidence, platform, and tool; inspect generated geometry, text, and any implied performance claim.
>
> Optional context: [image library index](../INDEX.md) · [brand dialects](../DIALECTS.md)

* **Universal Class:** The Proof
* **Example Products (Illustrative Guidance, Not an Allowlist):** Unisex fleece hoodie, tailored chinos, everyday button-down shirt, activewear leggings, denim jacket.
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   PDP Sizing Guide & Slot 4. Dramatically reduces return rates by showing how the same garment fits across different body types and heights.
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   Clean, neutral architectural studio with soft daylight. Two or three diverse models standing side-by-side wearing the garment with natural posture.
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 50mm Prime at chest height to eliminate perspective distortion.  
   * *Aperture:* f/8 (all models in equal sharpness).  
   * *Lighting:* Broad, flat, flattering studio light.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   Instant customer confidence: *"She looks like my body type—I know exactly which size to order now."*
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   Clear text safe zones at the bottom of the frame for model height and size callouts. Zero unnatural body warping.
* **Director's Rig Prompt Blueprint:**
```text
[BLOCK 1: HERO & SIZING REASSURANCE]: Commercial e-commerce fit guide photograph of diverse models standing side-by-side wearing [HERO PRODUCT: Garment type, color, fabric] in their respective sizes, occupying 80% of total frame width.
[BLOCK 2: OPTICAL RIG]: 50mm prime portrait lens at chest height, eye-level perspective, f/8 aperture keeping all models and garments in uniform sharpness.
[BLOCK 3: LIGHTING RIG]: Clean 5400K commercial catalog studio lighting with soft diffused shadows and crisp ground contact shadows.
[BLOCK 4: SUBORDINATE STAGE]: Seamless neutral light-gray studio cyclorama with zero props. Authentic models with natural relaxed postures and unretouched skin.
[BLOCK 5: EXCLUSIONS]: Negative: wax mannequins, uncanny valley, stiff identical poses, wooden barn, distorted hands, blurry garment hems.
```
