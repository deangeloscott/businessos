# Scale 03 — Archetype 07: Multi-Body Fit Diversity (Sizing Reassurance)

* **Visual job:** The Proof
* **Good for:** helping shoppers compare drape and proportion across representative bodies before opening the size guide.
* **Fits:** fleece hoodies, chinos, button-downs, leggings, and denim jackets.

## The idea

Show the same garment on a small, useful range of people with their actual measurements or sizes available alongside the asset. The image can reduce uncertainty without pretending that two or three bodies predict every fit.

## Borrow this treatment

Use a neutral studio or simple shared setting, natural postures, and consistent framing. Let shoulder seams, sleeve length, hem, ease, and fabric drape remain visible. Keep the models distinct and human; a size callout is helpful when it can be rendered or added reliably afterward.

## Make it yours

Use representative customer data, real size-chart values, and appropriate model consent. Change the number of people, pose, and environment for the garment. Inspect anatomy and body proportions, and use the image as sizing context rather than a universal fit guarantee.

## Optional prompt seed

```text
Show the supplied [GARMENT] on [NUMBER] representative models with their supplied [HEIGHT, MEASUREMENTS, AND SIZE] available for accurate labels. Use consistent neutral framing and natural posture so shoulder, sleeve, hem, and ease differences are easy to compare. Preserve body diversity and anatomy; direct shoppers to the size guide for their own fit.
```
