# Scale 03 — Archetype 08: The Curated Outfit Knolling (Wardrobe Pairing)

> Standalone starting point from the [Scale 03 menu](../03-body-worn-apparel.md). Adapt the bracketed inputs to the actual product, evidence, platform, and tool; inspect generated geometry, text, and any implied performance claim.
>
> Optional context: [image library index](../INDEX.md) · [brand dialects](../DIALECTS.md)

* **Universal Class:** The Specimen
* **Example Products (Illustrative Guidance, Not an Allowlist):** Chore jacket, tailored linen shirt, selvedge denim jeans, leather boots, technical daypack.
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   Cross-Sell & Complete the Look. Sells the entire lifestyle wardrobe by pairing the hero item with complementary pieces.
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   Top-down 90° flat-lay on a raw concrete, warm oak, or neutral linen floor. The hero jacket/shirt is laid out naturally alongside matching trousers, curated sneakers, sunglasses, and a watch.
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 35mm Prime mounted overhead.  
   * *Aperture:* f/8 for uniform edge-to-edge sharpness.  
   * *Lighting:* Large overhead diffused softbox casting soft, subtle directional shadows.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   Styling clarity: how different fabric textures (cotton, wool, leather, acetate) harmonize together.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   The hero product must be the visual anchor (positioned centrally with 50% larger visual presence than accessories).
* **Director's Rig Prompt Blueprint:**
```text
[BLOCK 1: HERO FLAT-LAY]: Overhead 90-degree knolling flat-lay photograph. In the center, [HERO PRODUCT: Garment, color, fabric] is laid out naturally, commanding 55% of the frame in tack-sharp focus.
[BLOCK 2: WARDROBE COMPLEMENTS]: Neatly arranged around the hero are 2-3 curated accessories [COMPLEMENTS: e.g., raw selvedge denim, leather boots, acetate sunglasses] creating a complete outfit.
[BLOCK 3: OPTICS]: 35mm prime lens overhead perpendicular to surface, f/8 aperture for edge-to-edge focal sharpness.
[BLOCK 4: STAGE & LIGHT]: Honed light-gray concrete or natural linen floor. Soft diffused overhead softbox casting delicate micro-shadows under every fold.
[BLOCK 5: EXCLUSIONS]: Negative: messy scattered clothes, wrinkly paper, accessories covering the hero product, harsh overhead glare.
```
