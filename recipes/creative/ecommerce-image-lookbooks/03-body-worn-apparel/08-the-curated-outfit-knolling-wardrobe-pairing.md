# Scale 03 — Archetype 08: The Curated Outfit Knolling (Wardrobe Pairing)

* **Visual job:** The Specimen
* **Good for:** showing how one hero item could work with complementary pieces without prescribing a single wardrobe.
* **Fits:** chore jackets, linen shirts, selvedge denim, leather boots, and daypacks.

## The idea

Build a visual outfit around the supplied garment. Fabric, color, silhouette, and accessories can show the range of a piece faster than a paragraph of styling advice.

## Borrow this treatment

Use a top-down layout, a simple floor or linen, and two or three complements with distinct roles. Keep the hero item central or otherwise clearly dominant; arrange shoes, bag, eyewear, or jewelry so they suggest a look without covering the garment.

## Make it yours

Choose complements that match the audience, season, and brand. Change the camera, styling, and degree of order freely; a model, rack, or split lookbook may communicate better. Keep every visible item intentional and avoid implying that the bundle is required.
