# Scale 03 — Archetype 06: The Gorpcore Weather & Element Torture Test

* **Visual job:** The Proof
* **Good for:** dramatizing weather exposure and the relief a technical garment is meant to provide.
* **Fits:** technical shells, hiking pants, trail runners, waterproof duffels, and ponchos.

## The idea

Put the supplied garment in the weather it is designed for: rain, mist, wind, cold, mud, or snow. Beading, wetness, hood shape, and the wearer's posture make the problem visible; a test record supplies the performance claim.

## Borrow this treatment

Use one believable trail, alpine, or coastal setting and a stable three-quarter view. Let droplets catch a rim light and keep the garment's technical silhouette clean. Make the rain feel physical without turning the fabric into a cape or the scene into an uncheckable fantasy.

## Make it yours

Use actual membrane, DWR, seam, and test information for ratings and outcomes. Change the weather, activity, model, and camera for the product. If the behavior is only aspirational, describe the frame as a concept and inspect fabric, anatomy, and water interaction carefully.

## Optional prompt seed

```text
Create an outdoor performance image of the supplied [GARMENT] in [WEATHER AND ACTIVITY]. Show the intended [BEADING, WETTING, HOOD, OR SEAM] behavior clearly on an anatomically natural wearer. Use supplied test conditions for factual claims; otherwise make the desired relief an explicit concept and keep the technical silhouette clean.
```
