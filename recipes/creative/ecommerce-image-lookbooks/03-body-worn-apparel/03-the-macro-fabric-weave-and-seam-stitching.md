# Scale 03 — Archetype 03: The Macro Fabric Weave & Seam Stitching

* **Visual job:** The Proof
* **Good for:** making textile quality, reinforcement, and construction details visible to a buyer.
* **Fits:** raw denim, merino knits, Cordura bags, taped zippers, and cashmere.

## The idea

Move close to the one construction detail that matters: a ribbed collar, bar tack, selvedge edge, weave, seam, or taped zipper. The viewer can inspect the material instead of taking “premium” on faith.

## Borrow this treatment

Use raking side light to reveal yarns, grain, stitch relief, or a matte technical surface. Keep the surrounding garment just present enough to explain where the detail sits. A quiet crop and sharp focal plane give one physical cue more authority than a pile of adjectives.

## Make it yours

Use the supplied tech pack, sample, and material references for thread count, stitch density, or construction language. Change the detail, magnification, and light for the garment. Show texture when measurements are unknown rather than inventing them.
