# Scale 03 — Archetype 09: Elasticity, Stretch & Tension Macro

* **Visual job:** The Proof
* **Good for:** making stretch, recovery, weave, and opacity easier to inspect.
* **Fits:** performance leggings, stretch boardshorts, compression layers, and comfort-stretch denim.

## The idea

Show hands or a wearer creating one representative tension in the actual fabric. The viewer can see the weave, surface, and amount of movement; a supplied specification or test is still needed for “maximum stretch,” “squat-proof,” or recovery claims.

## Borrow this treatment

Use a waistband, seam, panel, or leg opening that matters to the use case. Raking side light reveals the fibers and surface while a neutral background keeps the action legible. Hold the fabric at the relevant tension instead of stretching it theatrically beyond normal use.

## Make it yours

Use the actual fabric, stretch direction, opacity condition, and tested result. Change the hand action or wearer pose for the product. Inspect weave, fingers, and structural recovery; show texture rather than inventing a specification.

## Optional prompt seed

```text
Create a tactile close-up of the supplied [GARMENT] under one representative [STRETCH OR TENSION ACTION]. Make the actual weave, surface, and supplied [RECOVERY OR OPACITY OBSERVATION] easy to inspect with controlled side light. Keep the claim tied to the source and avoid absolute performance language when the condition is unknown.
```
