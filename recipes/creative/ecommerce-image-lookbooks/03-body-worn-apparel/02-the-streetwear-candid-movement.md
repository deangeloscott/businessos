# Scale 03 — Archetype 02: The Streetwear Candid Movement

* **Visual job:** The Ritual
* **Good for:** showing how clothing moves, folds, and feels in a real urban day.
* **Fits:** hoodies, cargo pants, commuter coats, sneakers, and cross-body bags.

## The idea

Catch the garment in an ordinary movement—a walk, turn, lean, or pause—so the viewer sees drape and attitude rather than a frozen mannequin pose. The city gives the piece a cultural and practical context.

## Borrow this treatment

Use one architectural street, stair, sidewalk, or facade with enough texture to establish place. Let a hem swing, trouser fold shift, or jacket open during the movement. Open-shade light and a calm camera keep the garment's color and shape trustworthy.

## Make it yours

Choose the movement, location, model, and styling that belong to the audience. Change the pace, camera height, or setting when a quieter treatment communicates better. Inspect hands, limbs, garment joins, and reflected color in the final frame.

## Optional prompt seed

```text
Create a candid urban image of the supplied [GARMENT] during a natural [WALK, TURN, LEAN, OR PAUSE].
```
