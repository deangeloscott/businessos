# Scale 03 — Archetype 11: The 5-Year Break-In & Patina Evolution

* **Visual job:** The Proof
* **Good for:** showing how leather, denim, canvas, or another material may mature through use.
* **Fits:** raw denim, leather boots, waxed canvas, wool knitwear, and leather belts.

## The idea

Pair a new piece with a documented worn sample or a clearly labeled conceptual later state. The contrast makes long-term character and ownership pride tangible without fabricating a five-year record.

## Borrow this treatment

Use a workbench, oak floor, or other surface that supports the material story. Raking light can reveal softened grain, creases, fading, and seam wear. Keep the mature state specific and dignified; “used” should not mean broken or ruined.

## Make it yours

Use the actual wear study, maintenance, material, and time period. Change the arrangement for the product and audience. If the later state is imagined, label it as an illustration and avoid promising durability from the image alone.
