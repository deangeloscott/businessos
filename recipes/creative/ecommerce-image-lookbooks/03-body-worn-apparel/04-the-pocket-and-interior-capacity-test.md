# Scale 03 — Archetype 04: The Pocket & Interior Capacity Test

* **Visual job:** The Proof
* **Good for:** answering what a bag, pocket, or compartment will actually hold and how it is organized.
* **Fits:** commuter backpacks, shell pockets, weekender bags, cargo pants, and totes.

## The idea

Open the relevant compartment and show the everyday object it is meant to carry. A laptop, phone, bottle, passport, or tool supplies both scale and function more quickly than an empty silhouette.

## Borrow this treatment

Show one item partly entering the pocket or a small, orderly set inside it. Light the interior far enough to reveal dividers, lining, padding, and closure. Keep the opening and object relationship obvious; the product should remain the hero.

## Make it yours

Use the real compartment dimensions and correctly sized reference objects. Adapt the objects to the audience and use case, and choose a cutaway, hand-in-action, or flat lay if that is clearer. Do not imply capacity, padding, or water resistance beyond the supplied construction.

## Optional prompt seed

```text
Show the supplied [BAG, POCKET, OR COMPARTMENT] open with the correctly sized [EVERYDAY OBJECTS] entering or organized inside. Make the available space, divider, lining, and closure easy to inspect with clean interior light. Use actual dimensions and keep the product geometry and objects proportionate.
```
