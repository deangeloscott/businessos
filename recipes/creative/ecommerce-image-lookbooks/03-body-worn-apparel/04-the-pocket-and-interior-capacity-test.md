# Scale 03 — Archetype 04: The Pocket & Interior Capacity Test

> Standalone starting point from the [Scale 03 menu](../03-body-worn-apparel.md). Adapt the bracketed inputs to the actual product, evidence, platform, and tool; inspect generated geometry, text, and any implied performance claim.
>
> Optional context: [image library index](../INDEX.md) · [brand dialects](../DIALECTS.md)

* **Universal Class:** The Proof
* **Example Products (Illustrative Guidance, Not an Allowlist):** Commuter backpack, technical shell jacket, weekender duffel bag, EDC tactical pants, leather tote bag.
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   PDP Slot 4 (Utility De-risking for Bags, Jackets & Pants). Answers the critical functional question: *"Will this actually hold my phone, laptop, water bottle, or passport?"*
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   The bag, backpack, or outerwear piece with its main compartment or pocket held open. Authentic everyday gear (15" laptop, phone, passport, water bottle) is shown partially sliding in or neatly organized inside.
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 35mm Prime looking down into the open pocket.  
   * *Aperture:* f/5.6.  
   * *Lighting:* Clean interior fill lighting illuminating deep into dark pocket linings so internal dividers are clearly visible.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   The immense psychological relief of organized utility: smooth-gliding metal zippers, padded fleece laptop sleeves, water-resistant interior coatings.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   Real-world items used for scale must match universal dimensions (e.g. an actual 15-inch laptop, not an oversized or undersized prop).
* **Director's Rig Prompt Blueprint:**
```text
[BLOCK 1: HERO & CAPACITY]: Commercial utility demonstration photograph of [HERO PRODUCT: Backpack, tote, or jacket interior], with main pocket unzipped to reveal interior storage capacity, occupying 80% of frame weight.
[BLOCK 2: REAL-WORLD SCALE]: A recognized daily object [SCALE OBJECT: e.g., sleek aluminum 15-inch laptop or water bottle] is shown smoothly sliding into the dedicated padded fleece compartment, proving ample dimensional clearance.
[BLOCK 3: OPTICS]: 40mm prime lens looking into the open compartment, f/5.6 aperture keeping interior dividers and zippers in sharp focus.
[BLOCK 4: STAGE & LIGHT]: Bright 5500K studio daylight with targeted interior fill light illuminating the dark lining fabric; grounded on a clean concrete or wood workbench.
[BLOCK 5: EXCLUSIONS]: Negative: dark unreadable pocket interior, cluttered messy junk, warped laptop dimensions, distorted zippers, messy background.
```
