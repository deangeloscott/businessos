# Scale 03 — Archetype 12: The Dual-Model Real-World Fit & Layering Comparison

* **Visual job:** The Proof
* **Good for:** showing how one garment sits alone and over a layer, or how fit changes across two representative sizes.
* **Fits:** technical shells, overcoats, denim jackets, structured blazers, and parkas.

## The idea

Place comparable wear states side by side so sleeve length, shoulder position, chest room, and layering ease are easy to inspect. The image supports a size guide; it does not stand in for every body or movement.

## Borrow this treatment

Use two people or two clearly separated states in a neutral studio. Keep postures relaxed and repeat the camera position so the garment—not pose or perspective—carries the comparison. Show base layer, over layer, size, or measurements where the supplied information makes that useful.

## Make it yours

Use the actual size chart, model measurements, and layering conditions. Change the number of models or use a before/after arrangement for the product. Inspect seams, sleeves, hands, and body proportions; avoid universal “no restriction” language.
