# Scale 03 — Archetype 10: The Luxury Unboxing & Hangtag Presentation

* **Visual job:** The Specimen
* **Good for:** making a genuine garment delivery or gifting experience feel tactile and considered.
* **Fits:** cashmere, handmade shoes, bespoke jackets, sunglasses cases, and silk robes.

## The idea

Show the garment at the moment it is received: folded, wrapped, tagged, or lifted from its actual presentation box. Paper, tissue, fold lines, and card stock can communicate care before the garment is worn.

## Borrow this treatment

Use one open box or mailer and reveal the packaging detail that carries the brand—glassine, embossing, ribbon, hangtag, or a careful fold. Warm low-contrast light and a quiet surface let paper and fabric texture do the work.

## Make it yours

Use the real fulfillment configuration, materials, tags, and product state. Change the setting for gifting, subscription, or direct delivery. If the package is aspirational, label it as a concept rather than presenting an unbuilt experience as established fact.

## Optional prompt seed

```text
Create a tactile unboxing image of the supplied [GARMENT] in its actual [PACKAGING CONFIGURATION]. Reveal [TISSUE, BOX, HANGTAG, OR OTHER DETAIL] with warm controlled light, clear fabric folds, and readable branding. Preserve the real delivery experience and do not invent packaging materials or fulfillment promises.
```
