# Scale 01 — Archetype 05: The Fingertip Touch & Application

> Standalone starting point from the [Scale 01 menu](../01-micro-intimate.md). Adapt the bracketed inputs to the actual product, evidence, platform, and tool; inspect generated geometry, text, and any implied performance claim.
>
> Optional context: [image library index](../INDEX.md) · [brand dialects](../DIALECTS.md)

* **Universal Class:** The Ritual
* **Example Products (Illustrative Guidance, Not an Allowlist):** Eye cream, facial balm, solid perfume, tinted lip treatment, cuticle oil.
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   PDP Slot 2 or Paid Social Hook. Demonstrates human scale, skin absorption, and effortless everyday application.
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   A cropped macro composition focused on a model's natural hand or index fingertip. The product bottle is held naturally or rests softly in the background.
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 85mm Macro.  
   * *Aperture:* f/4.  
   * *Lighting:* Diffused beauty dish or massive octabox directly above and in front, casting flattering, shadowless light across skin while preserving micro-texture.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   Natural, un-airbrushed skin texture (visible pores, authentic hydration); glistening droplet of serum resting on the fingertip showing zero grease.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   Anti-artifact guardrail: inspect fingers, wrist angle, formula contact, and skin texture. Model styling should match the brand and audience; a negative prompt cannot guarantee anatomical correctness.
* **Director's Rig Prompt Blueprint:**
```text
[BLOCK 1: HERO & RITUAL]: Macro application shot of [HERO PRODUCT: Container and formula]. On the pad of a model's natural index fingertip in the foreground, a single perfect pearl of [FORMULA: Texture, color, opacity] catches a dewy specular highlight.
[BLOCK 2: OPTICAL RIG]: 90mm macro lens, f/4 aperture, tack-sharp focus on the formula bead and natural skin pores of the finger.
[BLOCK 3: LIGHTING RIG]: High-CRI 5200K diffused beauty lighting creating soft, even illumination that reveals authentic skin texture and formula radiance.
[BLOCK 4: SUBORDINATE STAGE]: In the soft-focus background (f/2.8 blur, 2 stops darker), the opened [HERO PRODUCT PACKAGING] rests on a neutral stone vanity.
[BLOCK 5: EXCLUSIONS]: Negative: distorted hands, extra fingers, airbrushed plastic blur, fake manicure, greasy skin, cluttered background, dirty nails.
```
