# Scale 01 — Archetype 05: The Fingertip Touch & Application

* **Visual job:** The Ritual
* **Good for:** showing human scale, touch, and a product's use in one intimate frame.
* **Fits:** eye cream, facial balm, solid perfume, tinted lip treatment, and cuticle oil.

## The idea

Let a natural hand perform one small, legible action: pick up, dispense, dab, smooth, or apply the product. The viewer should understand the relationship between person, package, and formula without needing a diagram.

## Borrow this treatment

Use a cropped macro view with authentic skin texture and a single fingertip or hand as the carrier. A small bead of formula, a gentle swipe, or a product held just behind the action gives the frame a tactile focal point. Soft beauty light can keep the skin readable while avoiding a plastic retouched look.

## Make it yours

Choose the action that fits the product and audience: a balm can be scooped, a roll-on can touch skin, and a solid can be held. Match formula appearance and hand styling to supplied references. Inspect anatomy and contact in the final image; the image should not promise absorption, residue, or efficacy without evidence.
