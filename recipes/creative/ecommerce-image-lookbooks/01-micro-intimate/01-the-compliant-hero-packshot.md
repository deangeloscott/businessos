# Scale 01 — Archetype 01: The Catalog Hero Packshot

* **Visual job:** The Specimen
* **Good for:** product pages, catalogs, shopping feeds, and any first-glance view where the package, form, and label must be unmistakable.
* **Fits:** dropper bottles, moisturizer jars, perfume flacons, jewelry, and supplement containers.

## The idea

Make the supplied product the one clear subject. A clean, quiet field and a grounded base let the viewer recognize its shape, finish, volume, and label before anything else competes for attention.

## Borrow this treatment

Use a seamless neutral background, direct or gently elevated framing, and enough depth of field to keep the product's important surfaces readable. A restrained highlight can reveal glass or polished metal; a soft contact shadow keeps the object from floating. Leave useful negative space when the placement needs copy.

## Make it yours

Use the actual product reference, label, dimensions, crop, and platform requirement. Change the background, angle, lighting, or scale when they improve recognition; preserve the product identity and any supplied color or text.
