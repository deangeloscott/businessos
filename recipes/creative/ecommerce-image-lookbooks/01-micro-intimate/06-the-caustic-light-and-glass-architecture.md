# Scale 01 — Archetype 06: The Caustic Light & Glass Architecture

* **Visual job:** The Specimen
* **Good for:** making glass, liquid, translucency, and bottle construction feel luminous and substantial.
* **Fits:** perfume bottles, botanical oils, facial essences, crystal pendants, and watches.

## The idea

Turn the product's transparency into the composition. Light passing through glass or liquid can draw an architectural pattern across stone, water, or another quiet surface, giving the package visual weight without adding clutter.

## Borrow this treatment

Backlight a translucent bottle or object with a controlled hard beam and let its refractions become a secondary subject. Keep the label readable, the base grounded, and the caustic pattern subordinate to the product. A dark polished surface supports amber or clear glass; a pale surface can make the effect airy.

## Make it yours

Use the supplied bottle material, liquid color, and label. Change the surface, beam direction, and contrast to fit the product and placement. Do not let a dramatic reflection invent liquid properties or obscure the information the viewer needs.
