# Scale 01 — Archetype 06: The Caustic Light & Glass Architecture

> Standalone starting point from the [Scale 01 menu](../01-micro-intimate.md). Adapt the bracketed inputs to the actual product, evidence, platform, and tool; inspect generated geometry, text, and any implied performance claim.
>
> Optional context: [image library index](../INDEX.md) · [brand dialects](../DIALECTS.md)

* **Universal Class:** The Specimen
* **Example Products (Illustrative Guidance, Not an Allowlist):** Heavy-bottom perfume bottle, botanical hair oil, facial essence, crystal pendant, chronometer watch.
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   Top-of-funnel luxury brand hero image (Fragrance, Serums, Fine Spirits). Celebrates bottle industrial design, glass thickness, and liquid purity.
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   Polished stone, glass slab, or shallow pool of pristine clear water. The translucent or amber glass bottle is lit from behind to cast intricate, dancing light refractions (caustics) across the surface.
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 105mm Macro.  
   * *Aperture:* f/5.6.  
   * *Lighting:* Intense, narrow directional beam (snooted hard key light) hitting the back of the bottle, projecting golden or amber liquid caustics onto the foreground plane.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   Visual weight and temperature of cold glass; the pure, luminous golden refraction of oil or perfume liquid; clean optical brilliance.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   Backlighting must not blow out or obscure label typography. Label printing must remain crisp and readable against the illuminated liquid background.
* **Director's Rig Prompt Blueprint:**
```text
[BLOCK 1: HERO]: Architectural packshot of [HERO PRODUCT: Bottle material, glass thickness, liquid color, label details], occupying 75% of vertical frame height in tack-sharp focus.
[BLOCK 2: OPTICAL RIG]: 100mm prime lens, eye-level perspective, f/5.6 aperture for crisp front-to-back glass wall definition.
[BLOCK 3: LIGHTING RIG]: Hard directional backlight angled at 45 degrees through the rear of the bottle, projecting intricate, glowing golden-amber caustics and liquid refractions across the foreground surface. Crisp contact base shadow.
[BLOCK 4: SUBORDINATE STAGE]: Monolithic polished dark stone or shallow sheet of pristine still water. Background is a neutral, deep charcoal tone 3 stops darker than the glowing bottle.
[BLOCK 5: EXCLUSIONS]: Negative: blown-out unreadable label, murky water, dirty glass fingerprints, floating shadows, domestic clutter, bright distracting walls.
```
