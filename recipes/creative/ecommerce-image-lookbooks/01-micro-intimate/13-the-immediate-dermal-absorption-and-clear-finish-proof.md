# Scale 01 — Archetype 13: The Application & Clear-Finish Comparison

* **Visual job:** The Proof
* **Good for:** making a colored or rich formula's application and resulting finish easier to evaluate.
* **Fits:** face oils, botanical salves, concentrated serums, peptide essences, and dry body oils.

## The idea

Use a two-state visual: the formula as dispensed and the same formula spread on a suitable surface. The pain-to-relief contrast can be persuasive when it reflects an observed finish; without evidence, it should remain an explicit illustration of the intended experience.

## Borrow this treatment

Place one controlled droplet, dab, or stripe beside the blended finish on a natural forearm or neutral swatch surface. Keep both states large enough to read and use clean high-CRI light so color is not hidden by the treatment. A bottle in soft focus supplies context without adding another claim.

## Make it yours

Use the actual formula color, amount, surface, and observation. Adapt the two states for oil, balm, serum, or tint. Do not infer absorption time, residue, potency, or stain resistance from a convincing render.

## Optional prompt seed

```text
Create a clear two-state application image of the supplied [PRODUCT]: one controlled [DROPLET, DAB, OR STRIPE] beside the same formula spread to its supplied or explicitly conceptual [FINISH] on [SKIN OR SWATCH SURFACE]. Keep color and texture legible, use clean directional light, and label any illustrative result instead of promising absorption or zero residue.
```
