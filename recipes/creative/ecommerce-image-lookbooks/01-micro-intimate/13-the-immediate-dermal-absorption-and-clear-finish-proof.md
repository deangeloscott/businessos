# Scale 01 — Archetype 13: The Immediate Dermal Absorption & Clear Finish Proof

> Standalone starting point from the [Scale 01 menu](../01-micro-intimate.md). Adapt the bracketed inputs to the actual product, evidence, platform, and tool; inspect generated geometry, text, and any implied performance claim.
>
> Optional context: [image library index](../INDEX.md) · [brand dialects](../DIALECTS.md)

* **Universal Class:** The Proof
* **Example Products (Illustrative Guidance, Not an Allowlist):** Blue tansy face oil, botanical barrier salve, concentrated night serum, peptide essence, dry body oil.
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   PDP Slot 2 or Paid Social Hook (Overcomes Staining & Greasiness Anxiety). Makes a common customer objection to intensely colored or rich oils easier to evaluate: *"Will this stain my skin blue/yellow, or leave me looking greasy?"*
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   A woman's natural forearm resting on a clean, neutral white linen or marble studio surface, occupying 70% of frame weight. 
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 100mm True Macro.  
   * *Aperture:* f/8 for tack-sharp detail across natural skin pores.  
   * *Lighting:* 5200K high-CRI soft directional daylight.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   Visible demonstration or concept of a colored formula transitioning to a lighter finish. “High-potency,” absorption time, and zero residue require product evidence.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   Use left-to-right pain-to-relief grammar when helpful: raw formula on the left and the observed or intended finish on the right. If the finish is unverified, label the comparison as an illustration and do not promise instant absorption.
* **Director's Rig Prompt Blueprint:**
```text
[BLOCK 1: HERO DEMONSTRATION]: A crisp e-commerce skincare absorption demonstration on a clean, seamless neutral studio surface. The subject is a natural forearm positioned across the lower half of the frame, occupying 70% of visual weight with authentic skin pores visible.
[BLOCK 2: COMPARISON GRAMMAR]: A two-stage side-by-side demonstration directly on the skin. ON THE LEFT: A single concentrated micro-droplet of [HERO FORMULA: color and texture from source] resting on the skin. ON THE RIGHT: The identical formula rubbed smoothly into the skin, showing the supplied or observed finish. If unverified, make it a concept illustration rather than promising instant absorption, zero discoloration, or zero grease.
[BLOCK 3: OPTICS]: 100mm True Macro lens, f/8 aperture, tack-sharp focus on skin absorption.
[BLOCK 4: STAGE & LIGHT]: Clean 5200K high-CRI daylight. In the soft-focus background (f/2.8 blur, 2 stops darker), the [HERO BOTTLE] rests out of focus.
[BLOCK 5: EXCLUSIONS]: Negative: multiple skin patches, makeup swatches, floating blue blobs, brown droplets, messy droppers, cluttered bathroom props, airbrushed plastic skin.
```
