# Scale 01 — Archetype 15: The Precision Micro-Tolerance & Mechanism Teardown

* **Visual job:** The Specimen
* **Good for:** making small construction, sealing, machining, or actuation details inspectable.
* **Fits:** watches, jewelry clasps, airless pumps, glass droppers, and safety razors.

## The idea

Bring the mechanism close enough to understand. Show the intact interface or a clean, documented cutaway so the viewer can see what is made, how it connects, or why the object feels deliberate.

## Borrow this treatment

Use a dark industrial mat or neutral technical surface, isolate the relevant component, and light chamfers, threads, gaskets, springs, or other supplied details with precise highlights. A separated part can sit beside the assembled product to explain the relationship. Keep the object large and the geometry calm.

## Make it yours

Use only documented components, materials, tolerances, and construction. Change the magnification, surface, and lighting to fit the product. Inspect for warped threads, impossible joins, dust, and invented internals; a macro image can illustrate a mechanism but cannot establish an unsupported longevity or performance claim.

## Optional prompt seed

```text

Do not invent tolerances or internals.
