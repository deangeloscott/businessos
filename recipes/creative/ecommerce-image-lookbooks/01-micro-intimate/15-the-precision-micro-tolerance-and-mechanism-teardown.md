# Scale 01 — Archetype 15: The Precision Micro-Tolerance & Mechanism Teardown

> Standalone starting point from the [Scale 01 menu](../01-micro-intimate.md). Adapt the bracketed inputs to the actual product, evidence, platform, and tool; inspect generated geometry, text, and any implied performance claim.
>
> Optional context: [image library index](../INDEX.md) · [brand dialects](../DIALECTS.md)

* **Universal Class:** The Specimen
* **Example Products (Illustrative Guidance, Not an Allowlist):** Mechanical luxury watch, fine jewelry clasp, magnetic airless cosmetic pump, precision glass pipette dropper, safety razor.
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   PDP Slot 6 (Industrial Craftsmanship). Justifies a premium price by making supplied machining, seal, and build details legible; “aerospace-grade,” tolerance, and longevity claims require product documentation.
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   Dark anodized aluminum plate or anti-static watchmaker's mat. The hero mechanism is displayed either intact or cleanly unthreaded to reveal its internal silicone O-ring, micro-springs, or jewel bearings.
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 105mm True Macro at 1:1 reproduction.  
   * *Aperture:* f/11 for front-to-back microscopic sharpness across metal threads.  
   * *Lighting:* Cross-polarized linear LED strips casting surgical specular highlights along chamfered edges and threads.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   Deep mechanical confidence: visible haptic interfaces, supplied seal construction, stable contact, and polished micro-surfaces. Inspect for grease, dust, warped threads, and impossible geometry; the component should be large enough for the intended display without inventing tolerances.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   Keep the component large enough for the intended display and inspect for grease, dust, warped threads, and impossible geometry.

```text
[BLOCK 1: HERO]: Microscopic engineering macro photograph of [HERO PRODUCT / MECHANISM: e.g., unthreaded brass dropper collar with silicone hermetic seal, watch movement, or jewelry clasp]. Occupying 80% of frame weight in extreme tack-sharp focus.
[BLOCK 2: OPTICAL RIG]: 105mm True Macro lens at 1:1 magnification, f/11 aperture for edge-to-edge optical sharpness across micro-threads.
[BLOCK 3: LIGHTING RIG]: Cross-polarized 5500K linear LED studio lighting carving surgical specular highlights along knurled metallic ridges and O-ring gaskets, with deep black contact shadow.
[BLOCK 4: SUBORDINATE STAGE]: Dark matte anti-static industrial surface. Background is clean and uniform, 3 stops darker than the gleaming metal.
[BLOCK 5: EXCLUSIONS]: Negative: dust specks, fingerprints, scratches, blurry threads, cheap plastic look, domestic clutter, yellow color cast.
```
