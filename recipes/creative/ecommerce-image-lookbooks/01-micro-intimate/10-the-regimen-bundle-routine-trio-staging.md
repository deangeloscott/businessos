# Scale 01 — Archetype 10: The Regimen Bundle (Routine Trio Staging)

> Standalone starting point from the [Scale 01 menu](../01-micro-intimate.md). Adapt the bracketed inputs to the actual product, evidence, platform, and tool; inspect generated geometry, text, and any implied performance claim.
>
> Optional context: [image library index](../INDEX.md) · [brand dialects](../DIALECTS.md)

* **Universal Class:** The Specimen
* **Example Products (Illustrative Guidance, Not an Allowlist):** 3-step acne clearing system, anti-aging serum bundle, shampoo/conditioner/oil hair kit, perfume discovery set.
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   Cart-upsell & Bundle PDPs. Sells the complete 3-step system (Cleanse, Treat, Moisturize) as a unified daily habit rather than isolated single purchases.
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   A graduated tiered stone pedestal (honed travertine or fluted concrete). The three products are sequenced logically from left to right in order of usage (Step 1, Step 2, Step 3).
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 70mm Prime.  
   * *Aperture:* f/5.6 to maintain focus across all three staggered bottles.  
   * *Lighting:* Cohesive directional key light ensuring matching shadow angles across all three packaging silhouettes.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   The psychological satisfaction of completeness, structural order, and synergistic daily transformation.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   Relative bottle heights must be strictly true to scale. Fixed camera pitch (15°) preserves horizon harmony.
* **Director's Rig Prompt Blueprint:**
```text
[BLOCK 1: HERO BUNDLE]: Commercial trio packshot of [HERO BUNDLE: Product 1 Step-One Cleanser, Product 2 Step-Two Treatment Serum, Product 3 Step-Three Barrier Cream], arranged in clean left-to-right usage hierarchy, occupying 80% of frame width.
[BLOCK 2: OPTICAL RIG]: 70mm prime lens, 15-degree elevated angle, f/7.1 aperture keeping all three containers in crisp focal alignment.
[BLOCK 3: LIGHTING RIG]: Clean 5200K studio softbox from camera-left casting consistent, uniform directional shadows across all three products, with crisp contact base shadows.
[BLOCK 4: SUBORDINATE STAGE]: Monolithic stepped travertine pedestal against a seamless neutral light-gray background. Zero clutter.
[BLOCK 5: EXCLUSIONS]: Negative: mismatched shadow angles, incorrect relative bottle heights, unreadable labels, floating products, dark gloomy background.
```
