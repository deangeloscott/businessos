# Scale 01 — Archetype 10: The Regimen Bundle (Routine Trio Staging)

* **Visual job:** The Specimen
* **Good for:** making a set feel like a simple routine or complete purchase rather than disconnected products.
* **Fits:** cleanser-treatment-moisturizer sets, hair kits, discovery sets, and refill systems.

## The idea

Arrange the supplied products as a sequence the buyer can understand at a glance. Order, repetition, and shared light turn a collection into a usable ritual while keeping each package identifiable.

## Borrow this treatment

Use a tiered stone or stepped surface, or another arrangement that gives every item a clear place. Show the order of use left-to-right, top-to-bottom, or through discreet step cues. Keep shadows and scale coherent so the system feels intentional rather than assembled from unrelated packshots.

## Make it yours

Use the actual number of products, order, sizes, and usage instructions. A bundle can be a trio, discovery set, refill pair, or tool-and-consumable kit. Do not imply that products work synergistically unless that relationship is supported; let the visual organization do the work.

## Optional prompt seed

```text
Arrange the supplied [BUNDLE] as a clear [ROUTINE OR USE ORDER]. Give each product its own readable position on a simple stepped or tiered stage, with consistent light and believable relative scale. Make the sequence easy to follow and keep labels, quantities, and any step language faithful to the supplied references.
```
