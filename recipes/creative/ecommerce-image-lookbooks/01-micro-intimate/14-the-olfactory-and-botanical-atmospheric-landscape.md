# Scale 01 — Archetype 14: The Olfactory & Botanical Atmospheric Landscape

* **Visual job:** The Habitat
* **Good for:** giving an invisible scent, botanical note, or place story a memorable visual atmosphere.
* **Fits:** eau de parfum, botanical hair elixir, restorative night oil, and facial mist.

## The idea

Translate the product's supplied scent or source cues into a small world rather than trying to photograph an invisible smell. The product stays recognizable while one surface, light quality, or botanical reference suggests its mood and origin.

## Borrow this treatment

Try a dark veined stone, a restrained apothecary surface, warm candle glow, or a single delicate botanical element. Keep the atmosphere quiet and the label readable; one convincing cue is stronger than a pile of literal ingredients.

## Make it yours

Use the actual notes, source, and brand world. Replace the marble, botanical, candle, or color with cues that fit the product, and remove any element that suggests an ingredient or claim the source does not support. The treatment can be bright, domestic, or abstract.
