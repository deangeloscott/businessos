# Scale 01 — Archetype 14: The Olfactory & Botanical Atmospheric Landscape

> Optional context: [image library index](../INDEX.md) · [brand dialects](../DIALECTS.md)

* **Universal Class:** The Habitat
* **Example Products (Illustrative Guidance, Not an Allowlist):** Artisanal eau de parfum, botanical hair elixir, restorative night oil, organic facial mist.
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   Hero Header / Brand Equity (Sensory Storytelling). Conveys the invisible scent notes, terroir, and luxury prestige of a fragrance or botanical formula without words.
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   A dark, veined Calacatta or Nero Marquina marble slab in a moody apothecary setting. Centered bottle occupying 75% vertical frame height in tack-sharp focus, flanked by a single subtle botanical element (e.g. dried tansy sprig, single jasmine petal).
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 90mm Macro.  
   * *Aperture:* f/2.8 for creamy chiaroscuro depth.  
   * *Lighting:* Warm 2800K atmospheric candlelight in the deep background providing subtle rim lighting, while a soft 4500K frontal key light sculpts the bottle label.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   Intimate, tactile luxury: cold polished marble, warm flickering candle amber, delicate botanical fragility.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   The bottle is the undisputed hero (75% frame height). Background candle and botanicals are strictly subordinate in soft bokeh.
* **Director's Rig Prompt Blueprint:**
```text
[BLOCK 1: HERO]: Luxury commercial still-life photograph of [HERO PRODUCT: Fluted glass bottle, collar material, liquid color, label details]. The bottle is centered, occupying 75% of vertical frame height in tack-sharp focus with label facing directly toward camera.
[BLOCK 2: OPTICAL RIG]: 90mm prime macro lens, eye-level perspective, f/2.8 aperture for shallow depth of field.
[BLOCK 3: LIGHTING RIG]: Moody chiaroscuro lighting. Soft 4500K frontal key light illuminating the label, with a warm 2800K candlelight glow in the far background providing soft golden rim light; crisp contact occlusion shadow at base.
[BLOCK 4: SUBORDINATE STAGE]: Grounded on a dark veined marble slab inside an apothecary setting. In the soft-focus lower corner rests a single [SUBORDINATE BOTANICAL: e.g. dried botanical bloom]; background is 3 stops darker in creamy bokeh.
[BLOCK 5: EXCLUSIONS]: Negative: competing foreground props, multiple candles, fruit slices, clutter, distracting background, small bottle, off-center.
```
