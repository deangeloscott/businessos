# Scale 01 — Archetype 12: The Multi-Tone Universal Compatibility Matrix

> Standalone starting point from the [Scale 01 menu](../01-micro-intimate.md). Adapt the bracketed inputs to the actual product, evidence, platform, and tool; inspect generated geometry, text, and any implied performance claim.
>
> Optional context: [image library index](../INDEX.md) · [brand dialects](../DIALECTS.md)

* **Universal Class:** The Proof
* **Example Products (Illustrative Guidance, Not an Allowlist):** Tinted mineral sunscreen, universal lip oil, skin barrier balm, glow drops, sheer CC serum.
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   PDP Slot 2 (Universal Suitability & Inclusivity). Addresses the hesitation for tinted and topical products: *"Will this leave an ashy white cast on dark skin or look orange on fair skin?"* Use documented swatches or present the frame as a concept rather than a universal result.
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   A clean, modern studio setting featuring three natural, unretouched forearms side-by-side spanning the Fitz-Patrick skin spectrum (Fair Type I/II, Olive Type III/IV, Deep Melanin Type V/VI). The product is rubbed in smoothly on each arm.
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 85mm Prime Portrait lens.  
   * *Aperture:* f/5.6 for uniform sharpness across all three skin tones.  
   * *Lighting:* High-CRI 5500K color-calibrated daylight with large diffused octabox overhead, rendering true undertones without color cast.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   Visual relief: show the supplied or observed finish across the chosen skin tones. Avoid universal “zero cast” language unless the product has been tested across the represented conditions.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   Skin textures must remain natural and un-airbrushed with authentic pores. Arms positioned horizontally or diagonally with the hero bottle resting softly in the background.
* **Director's Rig Prompt Blueprint:**
```text
[BLOCK 1: HERO & INCLUSIVITY PROOF]: A commercial e-commerce skin compatibility demonstration. Three natural, diverse human arms (Deep Melanin, Olive, and Fair tones) positioned neatly side-by-side across the lower 70% of the frame.
[BLOCK 2: PROOF CLAIM]: On each arm, [HERO PRODUCT: Sunscreen, barrier balm, or tint] has been blended in, showing the supplied or observed finish across the represented tones. If the swatches are not documented, present the result as a concept and avoid universal claims about absorption, white cast, chalkiness, or residue.
[BLOCK 3: OPTICS]: 85mm prime lens, f/5.6 aperture, tack-sharp focus on natural skin pores and micro-texture.
[BLOCK 4: STAGE & LIGHT]: High-CRI 5500K clinical studio daylight. In the soft-focus background (f/2.8 blur, 2 stops darker), the [HERO PACKAGING] rests upright.
[BLOCK 5: EXCLUSIONS]: For an idealized conceptual treatment, avoid inventing white chalky residue or an ashy film; if supplied swatches document either result, preserve and label the observed finish. In all cases avoid airbrushed plastic skin, makeup foundation patches, floating blobs, dirty smudges, and cluttered props.
```
