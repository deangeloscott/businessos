# Scale 01 — Archetype 12: The Multi-Tone Finish Comparison

* **Visual job:** The Proof
* **Good for:** helping a specific audience see how a tint, sunscreen, balm, or finish reads across represented skin tones.
* **Fits:** tinted sunscreen, lip oil, glow drops, sheer CC serum, and barrier balm.

## The idea

Show the supplied product on a deliberately chosen range of natural skin tones, with enough consistency for comparison. The frame answers “how might this read on people like me?” without pretending that a few swatches represent every person or condition.

## Borrow this treatment

Align arms or hands simply, blend the same product in the same way, and use color-consistent light. Keep pores and undertones natural; let the product finish be the point. A quiet package in the background can connect the swatches to the hero without taking over.

## Make it yours

Use documented swatches, audience needs, and the intended wear condition. Change the number of tones and arrangement for the crop. Avoid universal “no cast,” “invisible,” or compatibility language unless the represented conditions and evidence support it.

## Optional prompt seed

```text
Create a color-consistent finish comparison for the supplied [PRODUCT] across [REPRESENTED SKIN TONES]. Apply the same amount and treatment to natural, unretouched skin so the supplied finish is easy to inspect. Keep the product visible but subordinate, and label the range accurately rather than implying universal performance.
```
