# Scale 01 — Archetype 04: The Domestic Vanity Altar (In-Situ Sanctuary)

> Optional context: [image library index](../INDEX.md) · [brand dialects](../DIALECTS.md)

* **Universal Class:** The Habitat
* **Example Products (Illustrative Guidance, Not an Allowlist):** Fragrance flacon, facial essence, restorative night oil, gold vermeil watch, daily peptide serum.
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   PDP Slot 3 (Lifestyle & Habit Projection). Allows the customer to visualize the product permanently living on their luxury bathroom vanity or bedroom dresser.
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   Honed Carrara marble vanity or unlacquered brass tray. Background features soft out-of-focus elements: textured waffle linen towel, minimalist ceramic vase, subtle subway tile reflection.
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 50mm Prime.  
   * *Aperture:* f/2.8 for cinematic shallow depth of field.  
   * *Lighting:* Warm natural morning sunlight filtering through frosted glass, creating soft rectangular light patches.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   Warm, clean, steamy bathroom atmosphere; pristine reflective glass; comforting physical weight of a morning self-care ritual.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   Upper quadrant preserved as clean negative space. The product must remain in tack-sharp focus (occupying 65% visual weight) while vanity props melt into soft bokeh.
* **Director's Rig Prompt Blueprint:**
```text
[BLOCK 1: HERO]: Commercial in-situ vanity photograph of [HERO PRODUCT: Packaging, finish, fill level], resting upright and commanding 70% of frame weight with label in tack-sharp focus.
[BLOCK 2: OPTICAL RIG]: 60mm prime lens, eye-level angle, f/2.8 aperture for creamy subject separation.
[BLOCK 3: LIGHTING RIG]: Warm 3200K morning sunlight filtering through a sheer window curtain from camera-left, creating soft rectangular light patches with gentle chiaroscuro and crisp contact base shadow.
[BLOCK 4: SUBORDINATE STAGE]: Honed neutral marble countertop. In the distant, heavily blurred background (f/2.8 bokeh, 2.5 stops darker), a folded waffle-linen hand towel and subtle ceramic vase rest out of focus.

```
