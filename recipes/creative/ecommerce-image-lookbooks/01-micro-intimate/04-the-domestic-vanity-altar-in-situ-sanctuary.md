# Scale 01 — Archetype 04: The Domestic Vanity Altar (In-Situ Sanctuary)

* **Visual job:** The Habitat
* **Good for:** helping a viewer picture a small product living naturally inside a daily ritual.
* **Fits:** fragrance, facial essence, night oil, watches, and daily skincare.

## The idea

Put the supplied product in a believable place where it would be reached for and enjoyed: a vanity, bedside table, bath shelf, or dressing area. The setting should suggest the ritual without hiding the product inside décor.

## Borrow this treatment

Try a stone vanity or brass tray, one or two quiet domestic cues such as linen or ceramic, and warm window light. Keep the product sharp and the habitat soft. Reserve a clean area for copy when the asset needs it; the atmosphere can be intimate without becoming cluttered.

## Make it yours

Use the customer's actual context, brand materials, and intended crop. Swap the ritual, surface, palette, and time of day freely. Keep secondary props subordinate and avoid implying an ingredient or result the supplied product information does not support.
