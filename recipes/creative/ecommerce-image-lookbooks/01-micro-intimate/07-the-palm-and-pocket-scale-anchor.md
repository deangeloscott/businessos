# Scale 01 — Archetype 07: The Palm & Pocket Scale Anchor

* **Visual job:** The Specimen
* **Good for:** answering “how big is it?” and making portability or everyday handling immediately understandable.
* **Fits:** mini serums, lip balms, pill cases, compact fragrances, and signet rings.

## The idea

Place the product beside a familiar human scale reference: an open palm, a pocket, a pouch, or a hand performing a normal carry action. The reference should clarify proportion, not exaggerate it.

## Borrow this treatment

Use a relaxed palm holding the product upright or flat, or show it entering the pocket or pouch it is meant to fit. Keep the relationship natural and the background quiet. Neutral daylight and an undistorted perspective make the size comparison feel trustworthy.

## Make it yours

Use actual dimensions and the intended use case. Choose a reference that matches the audience and culture, and change the hand, pocket, crop, or setting when that communicates better. Inspect product proportions, anatomy, and contact; do not turn portability into a claim the dimensions cannot support.

## Optional prompt seed

```text
Show the supplied [PRODUCT AND DIMENSIONS] in a natural [PALM, POCKET, OR POUCH] context so its real scale is easy to judge. Keep the hand or container proportionate, the product recognizable, and the perspective calm. Use clean neutral light and a quiet background; preserve the supplied dimensions and avoid deceptive distortion.
```
