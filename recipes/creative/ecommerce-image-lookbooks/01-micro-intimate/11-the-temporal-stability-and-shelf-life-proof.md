# Scale 01 — Archetype 11: The Temporal Stability & Shelf-Life Proof

* **Visual job:** The Proof
* **Good for:** showing a real documented change over time, or clearly illustrating the question a stability claim must answer.
* **Fits:** vitamin C serums, probiotic creams, botanical oils, and peptide lip treatments.

## The idea

Use a before-and-later comparison to make freshness, separation, color, or packaging protection legible. A documented later sample can support the story; a generated “Day 1 / Later” frame without a record is only a labeled concept.

## Borrow this treatment

Keep matching bottles or samples on a clinical stone or neutral bench so the viewer compares the material, not the décor. Use backlight or a clear surface to reveal the supplied difference, and place the time label beside the sample rather than relying on visual implication.

## Make it yours

Use the actual record, storage condition, interval, and observed result. If those are unavailable, show the question without inventing elapsed time, oxidation, clarity, or stability. The light can make a difference legible; it cannot prove it.

## Optional prompt seed

```text
Create a clearly labeled comparison of the supplied [PRODUCT] at [DOCUMENTED START STATE] and [DOCUMENTED LATER STATE], using matching framing and a quiet clinical surface. Make the observed difference easy to inspect and include the supplied interval and conditions. If no record exists, label the later state as conceptual and do not present the image as a stability test.
```
