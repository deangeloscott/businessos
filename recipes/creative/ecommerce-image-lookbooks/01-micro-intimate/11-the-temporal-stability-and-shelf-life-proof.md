# Scale 01 — Archetype 11: The Temporal Stability & Shelf-Life Proof

> Standalone starting point from the [Scale 01 menu](../01-micro-intimate.md). Adapt the bracketed inputs to the actual product, evidence, platform, and tool; inspect generated geometry, text, and any implied performance claim.
>
> Optional context: [image library index](../INDEX.md) · [brand dialects](../DIALECTS.md)

* **Universal Class:** The Proof
* **Example Products (Illustrative Guidance, Not an Allowlist):** Active L-ascorbic acid serum, probiotic eye cream, organic botanical face oil, peptide lip treatment.
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   PDP Slot 5 & Customer Reassurance. Addresses the silent fear: *"Will this vitamin C turn brown, separate, or oxidize before I can finish the bottle?"* Use an observed stability record or make the comparison explicitly conceptual.
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   Clean clinical or pharmaceutical marble bench. A side-by-side: freshly unsealed bottle beside a documented later sample, or a labeled conceptual “Day 1 / Later” contrast when no stability record is available. Do not invent the elapsed time or result.
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 105mm Macro.  
   * *Aperture:* f/8.  
   * *Lighting:* Calibrated 5500K clinical backlighting through the amber or opaque glass to make clarity differences legible; the light does not prove stability.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   Deep biochemical trust: the reassuring amber glass blocking UV rays; airless pump mechanism preventing oxidation; pristine, unblemished freshness.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   Recommended comparison grammar: Day 1 or generic problem on the left, later/hero state on the right. Label the elapsed time and result only from the supplied record.
* **Director's Rig Prompt Blueprint:**
```text
[BLOCK 1: HERO COMPARISON]: A side-by-side e-commerce formulation stability demonstration of two identical [HERO PRODUCT: Amber dropper bottles / airless pumps] on a clinical white marble surface, occupying 75% of frame width.
[BLOCK 2: COMPARISON GRAMMAR]: ON THE LEFT: A freshly unsealed Day 1 bottle with a droplet on a watch glass. ON THE RIGHT: [DOCUMENTED LATER SAMPLE OR CLEARLY LABELED CONCEPTUAL LATER STATE]. Show the supplied observation; do not invent six months, identical clarity, oxidation, or phase-separation results.
[BLOCK 3: OPTICS]: 90mm macro lens, eye-level perspective, f/8 aperture for crisp sharpness across both bottles.
[BLOCK 4: STAGE & LIGHT]: 5500K clinical back-illumination shining through the bottles to prove internal liquid clarity, with soft diffused fill.
[BLOCK 5: EXCLUSIONS]: For a clean conceptual comparison, avoid inventing brown oxidation or murky sediment; if the supplied stability record documents either result, preserve it and label the observed outcome. In all cases avoid unreadable labels, cluttered bathroom props, and dramatic gloomy shadows.
```
