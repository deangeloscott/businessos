# Scale 01 — Archetype 09: The Formulation Purity Split

* **Visual job:** The Proof
* **Good for:** a forceful generic poor-versus-premium contrast when a material or formulation difference is central to the offer.
* **Fits:** marula oil, peptide ampoules, mineral sunscreen, raw honey balm, and concentrated treatments.

## The idea

Put a generic weak or unsatisfying reference beside the hero formula so the buyer can see the pain and relief at once. The contrast can be deliberately stark and persuasive, but the alternative stays generic and illustrative unless its facts are supplied; it is never a disguised named competitor.

## Borrow this treatment

Use a clean side-by-side or split surface. On one side, show the chosen compromise—thin, dull, cluttered, or poorly presented. On the other, let the supplied hero formula carry density, color, cohesion, or finish through a controlled macro treatment. Give each state a clear visual role and enough room for a short label if the placement needs it.

## Make it yours

Choose one meaningful difference the audience already cares about. Use actual composition, swatch, and packaging references for the hero; label a conceptual or generic comparison honestly. Change the reading direction, crop, and visual intensity for the placement.

## Optional prompt seed

```text
Create a direct side-by-side comparison between the supplied [HERO FORMULA] and a clearly generic illustrative [LOWER-QUALITY REFERENCE]. Make [MEANINGFUL DIFFERENCE] immediately visible through texture, finish, or presentation, with the hero communicating [DESIRED BENEFIT] and the generic state communicating [COMPROMISE]. Keep the comparison generic, label it when useful, and preserve only supplied product facts.
```
