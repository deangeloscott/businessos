# Scale 01 — Archetype 09: The Formulation Purity Split

> Standalone starting point from the [Scale 01 menu](../01-micro-intimate.md). Adapt the bracketed inputs to the actual product, evidence, platform, and tool; inspect generated geometry, text, and any implied performance claim.
>
> Optional context: [image library index](../INDEX.md) · [brand dialects](../DIALECTS.md)

* **Universal Class:** The Proof
* **Example Products (Illustrative Guidance, Not an Allowlist):** Pure unrefined marula oil, concentrated peptide ampoule, mineral zinc sunscreen, raw honey lip nectar.
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   PDP Slot 6 (Differentiation). Uses a controlled generic lower-quality reference beside the hero formula to make the buyer's pain and the premium relief immediately legible. Use only supplied composition facts for the hero; do not imply that the generic reference is a named competitor or laboratory result.
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   A clean side-by-side on neutral acrylic. On the left: a deliberately generic weak or unsatisfying reference state. On the right: the hero formula rendered from supplied color/texture references or an explicitly conceptual ideal. The contrast may be strong; the label should identify the left state as generic or illustrative.
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 100mm Macro.  
   * *Aperture:* f/8 for equal sharpness on both comparison samples.  
   * *Lighting:* Neutral clinical lab lighting (5500K) over pure white surgical acrylic.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   Visceral perception of value: visible density, natural color saturation, and unbroken surface tension can communicate a premium sensory difference. They do not prove potency without a source or test.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   Recommended comparison grammar: generic/diluted reference on the left and the hero formula on the right, with clear text zones. Adjust the direction if the platform or reading context calls for it.
* **Director's Rig Prompt Blueprint:**
```text
[BLOCK 1: HERO COMPARISON]: A side-by-side macro formulation comparison photograph on a clean white surgical acrylic stage.
```
