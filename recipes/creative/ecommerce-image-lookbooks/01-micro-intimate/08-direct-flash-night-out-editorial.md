# Scale 01 — Archetype 08: Direct-Flash Night-Out Editorial

> Standalone starting point from the [Scale 01 menu](../01-micro-intimate.md). Adapt the bracketed inputs to the actual product, evidence, platform, and tool; inspect generated geometry, text, and any implied performance claim.
>
> Optional context: [image library index](../INDEX.md) · [brand dialects](../DIALECTS.md)

* **Universal Class:** The Ritual
* **Example Products (Illustrative Guidance, Not an Allowlist):** Matte lipstick bullet, compact mirror, luxury pocket fragrance, gold statement earring, recovery drops.
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   Paid Social (TikTok / Meta Feed pattern-interrupt). Cuts through stale beige wellness feeds with vibrant, high-fashion, youth-culture energy.
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   A night-out setting: retro diner booth, vintage car dashboard, or black lacquer nightclub vanity. Staged as if captured mid-celebration or mid-touch-up.
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 35mm Prime.  
   * *Aperture:* f/8.  
   * *Lighting:* Hard, direct on-camera speedlight flash. Sharp black cast shadows directly behind the product, 0% ambient fill. Film emulation tone with subtle grain.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   Auditory and sensory memory of social nightlife: high energy, glossy lipstick sheen, the click of a compact mirror, the crisp pop of a flash.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   Flash exposure must not wash out packaging text. The product must remain unmistakably the sharpest object in the frame despite the candid environment.
* **Director's Rig Prompt Blueprint:**
```text
[BLOCK 1: HERO]: Editorial night-out still-life of [HERO PRODUCT: Packaging, materials, finish], placed boldly in the foreground occupying 75% of frame weight in tack-sharp focus.
[BLOCK 2: OPTICAL RIG]: 35mm prime lens, eye-level perspective, f/8 aperture with authentic 35mm analog film grain.
[BLOCK 3: LIGHTING RIG]: Hard, direct on-camera paparazzi speedlight flash with zero ambient fill light, creating a crisp, graphic pitch-black cast shadow directly behind the product.
[BLOCK 4: SUBORDINATE STAGE]: Dark lacquered vintage vanity or car dashboard surface. Ambient background is intentionally underexposed (-2 EV) with subtle out-of-focus city neon bokeh.
[BLOCK 5: EXCLUSIONS]: Negative: soft pastel wellness lighting, washed-out label text, blurry product, boring white studio, digital plastic over-smoothing.
```
