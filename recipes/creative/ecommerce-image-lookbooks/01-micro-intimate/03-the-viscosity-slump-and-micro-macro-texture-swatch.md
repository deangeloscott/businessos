# Scale 01 — Archetype 03: The Viscosity, Slump & Micro-Macro Texture Swatch

* **Visual job:** The Ritual
* **Good for:** helping a viewer imagine a formula's touch, flow, finish, or material character.
* **Fits:** balms, gels, clay masks, liquid highlighters, and rich night creams.

## The idea

Show the product and one generous, readable swatch together. A smear, bead, slump, or controlled drip gives the eye something physical to inspect and answers the feeling behind “what will this be like?”

## Borrow this treatment

Set the open product beside the swatch on matte ceramic, acrylic, stone, or another surface that supports the material. Raking side light can sculpt ridges and peaks; a focused highlight can reveal gloss, cohesion, or surface tension. Keep the background quiet so one texture carries the frame.

## Make it yours

Match color, opacity, thickness, and behavior to a supplied sample or reference. Choose a different interaction for a gel, balm, powder, or liquid. If the texture is imagined, describe it as a concept instead of presenting it as a formula or performance result.
