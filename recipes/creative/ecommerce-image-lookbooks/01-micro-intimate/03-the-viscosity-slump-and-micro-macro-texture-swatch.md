# Scale 01 — Archetype 03: The Viscosity, Slump & Micro-Macro Texture Swatch

> Standalone starting point from the [Scale 01 menu](../01-micro-intimate.md). Adapt the bracketed inputs to the actual product, evidence, platform, and tool; inspect generated geometry, text, and any implied performance claim.
>
> Optional context: [image library index](../INDEX.md) · [brand dialects](../DIALECTS.md)

* **Universal Class:** The Ritual
* **Example Products (Illustrative Guidance, Not an Allowlist):** Ceramide barrier balm, hyaluronic acid gel, clay purifying mask, liquid highlighter, night renewal cream.
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   PDP Slot 2 or 5. Directly addresses the "I can't feel it" objection through tactile simulation. It can show observed viscosity or a deliberately idealized texture concept; only a documented sample supports a factual formula claim.
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   Frosted white acrylic plate, matte ceramic tile, or unsealed honed limestone slab. A single generous, tactile smear or droplet of the product is placed directly adjacent to the open jar or dropper tip.
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 105mm Macro at 1:1 reproduction ratio.  
   * *Aperture:* f/4 (ultra-sharp focus on the apex of the cream smear or liquid bead, with soft, creamy background falloff on the packaging).  
   * *Lighting:* Raking low-angle side lighting at 20° from the left to sculpt microscopic ridges and peaks of the formula swatch.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   Glossy, dewy specular highlights on the formula apex; visible richness and cohesion of the emulsion; a bead of liquid hanging with unbroken surface tension.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   Formula color and opacity should follow a supplied sample or reference. If the composition is unknown, use neutral descriptive texture language and avoid labeling the image as a chemical or performance result.
* **Director's Rig Prompt Blueprint:**
```text
[BLOCK 1: HERO]: Macro formulation swatch and packshot of [HERO PRODUCT: Open jar, bottle with dropper, or tube]. Centered, occupying 75% of frame area in tack-sharp focus.
[BLOCK 2: FORMULA SWATCH]: Directly beside the packaging on the stage surface, a generous, sculptural smear of [FORMULA: Texture, color, opacity, finish, sourced from the real sample when available] displays microscopic peaks, ridges, and a luminous specular highlight that communicates the intended sensory texture. Do not label this as a hydration result unless supported.
[BLOCK 3: OPTICAL RIG]: 105mm True Macro lens, f/4 aperture, low 25-degree perspective locking sharp focus on the formula swatch apex.
[BLOCK 4: STAGE & LIGHT]: Honed matte neutral ceramic tile surface. Raking directional 5200K side light from camera-left casting dimensional shadows across the swatch, background falling off into soft creamy bokeh.
[BLOCK 5: EXCLUSIONS]: Negative: runny watery mess, dirty fingerprints, multiple conflicting swatch colors, plastic skin, cluttered props, floating shadows.
```
