# Scale 06: Micro-Mobility, Vehicles & Adventure Transport Lookbook
### Master Archetypes for Micro-Mobility, Transport & Overland Systems
*Categories: E-Bikes, Electric Scooters, Rooftop Overland Tents, Cargo Trailers, Adventure Rigs, Skateboards, OneWheels*

---

## Overview & Scale Dynamics
Micro-mobility and adventure transport represent an explosive, high-ticket e-commerce sector ($1,000 to $8,000+). These products are mechanical, electrical, and kinetic—purchased to navigate city streets, conquer mountain trails, or transform a vehicle into an outdoor campsite.

Shoppers face high-stakes functional doubts:
1. *Battery & Range Anxiety:* "How do I charge it? Is the battery removable?"
2. *Spatial / Storage Dread:* "Can I fit this in my apartment, car trunk, or elevator?"
3. *Kinetic Stability & Power:* "Will this climb steep hills? Does it feel stable at 20 mph?"
4. *Weather & Durability Dread:* "Can I ride this in the rain, or will the electronics short out?"

Every archetype here is engineered with **kinetic velocity physics, modular utility proof, real-world scale**, and strict adherence to the **5-Block Director's Rig Protocol**.

### Archetype 01: The Compliant Studio Side-Profile Hero
* **Universal Class:** The Specimen
* **Example Products (Illustrative Guidance, Not an Allowlist):** Commuter e-bike, electric stand-up scooter, single-wheel electric board, overland cargo trailer, carbon gravel bike.
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   Shopify & Catalog Main Hero (Slot 1). Flawless, distortion-free view of frame geometry, wheelbase proportions, tire tread, and drive-train components.
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   Pure seamless neutral white cyclorama (RGB 255, 255, 255). The e-bike, scooter, or trailer is positioned in true parallel side-profile (drive-side facing the camera), resting solidly on its tires.
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 85mm Prime positioned 15 feet back at axle height to eliminate perspective keystoning (both wheels appear identical in size).  
   * *Aperture:* f/8 to f/11 for corner-to-corner mechanical sharpness.  
   * *Lighting:* Commercial automotive studio softbox array casting long, smooth highlight ribbons along the top tube, down tube, and wheel rims.  
   * *Shadow:* Crisp, razor-thin black contact shadow directly under both tire contact patches.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   Machined mechanical precision: the tautness of the drive chain/belt, cold density of hydroformed aluminum tubing, rugged rubber tire tread.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   Vehicle occupies 82% to 85% of total frame width. Frame decals, motor brand badges, and battery housing must be 100% faithful to CAD specs.
* **Director's Rig Prompt Blueprint:**
```text
[BLOCK 1: HERO]: Commercial hero side-profile photograph of [HERO PRODUCT: Brand, vehicle type, frame geometry, matte/gloss finish, tire size]. Positioned in true parallel profile occupying 82% of frame width in tack-sharp focus.

[BLOCK 4: SUBORDINATE STAGE]: Pure seamless non-reflective studio cyclorama background (calibrated pure white RGB 255, 255, 255). Zero props, zero distractions.
[BLOCK 5: EXCLUSIONS]: Negative: floating shadows, colored backgrounds, kickstand clutter, distorted wheel sizes, street clutter, blurry frame text.
```
