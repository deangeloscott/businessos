# Scale 04 — Archetype 11: The 10-Year Workhorse & Heavy-Cycle Resilience

* **Visual job:** The Proof
* **Illustrative examples:** Dual-boiler espresso machine, commercial burr grinder, cast-iron waffle maker, heavy power tool, commercial blender.

## The idea

Make long-term value visible through a product that looks used, cared for, and still ready to work. A documented service history, cycle test, warranty, or aging sample can support a longevity story; a generated patina is only an illustrative mature state. Show the marks of use that matter to the buyer while keeping the machine clean, safe, and structurally credible.

## Borrowable execution

- **What to make felt:** Unshakeable acoustic and mechanical solidity: the weighty snap of commercial-grade toggle switches, dense cast-metal vibration dampening.
- **One visual treatment:** Lens: 60mm Prime. Aperture: f/5.6 for sharp structural depth across mechanical joints and dials. Lighting: Rich, warm culinary lighting (4200K) with glancing specular highlights accentuating cast-metal chassis integrity and machined bevels.
- **Check:** Distinguish cleanly between durable operational patina and dirt: equipment must be spotless and hygienic, showing material maturation rather than poor maintenance.
