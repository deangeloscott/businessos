## The idea

Show one natural operating gesture clearly enough that a viewer understands both the touchpoint and the effort. Locking, turning, pressing, lifting, or sliding can all work; choose the action the actual product asks of the user and let the hand, mechanism, and result read together. The image can suggest ease and competence without claiming a measured force or speed.

## Borrowable execution

- **What to make felt:** Ease and control: let the gesture make the machine feel like a capable professional tool without asserting a measured force or skill level.
- **One visual treatment:** Lens: 50mm Prime. Aperture: f/4. Lighting: Clean natural kitchen daylight highlighting the interface.
- **Check:** Hands must show authentic ergonomic leverage (zero awkward AI joint bending).
