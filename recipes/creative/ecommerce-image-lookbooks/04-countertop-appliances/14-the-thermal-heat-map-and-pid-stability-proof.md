## The idea

Make the relationship between heat source, control, and exterior surface visible. A split view, thermal overlay, or carefully staged test scene can explain stability and isolation more quickly than a paragraph. Use actual test conditions for temperatures, gradients, or safe-touch language; without them, show the mechanism or concept without implying a measured result.
