# Scale 04 — Archetype 01: The Master Countertop Hero

> A quick, adaptable visual starting point. Borrow the idea, change the product or treatment freely, combine it with another approach, or skip it when you already have a better direction.
>
> Optional context: [image library index](../INDEX.md) · [brand dialects](../DIALECTS.md)

* **Visual job:** The Specimen
* **Illustrative examples:** Dual-boiler espresso machine, countertop pizza oven, precision coffee grinder, high-torque vortex blender, smart indoor grill.

## The idea

Make a substantial appliance feel like the center of the work. Give it clear hierarchy, enough environment to explain where it belongs, and restrained styling that lets its materials, controls, and silhouette carry the premium impression. A centered stone-countertop studio treatment is one useful execution; the idea also suits workshop, patio, and studio equipment.

## Borrowable execution

- **What to make felt:** Industrial heft and mechanical permanence; tactile sensation of cold brushed steel and heavy cast-metal levers.
- **One visual treatment:** Lens: 50mm Prime at counter-level. Keep wide-angle distortion low and inspect vertical appliance lines. Aperture: f/8 for broad front-to-back mechanical focus. Lighting: Commercial studio softboxes casting long, smooth gradient highlights down brushed stainless steel or powder-coated panels. Subtle rim light separating top contours from the backsplash.
- **Check:** Keep the product large enough to inspect in the target crop; dial markings, digital screens, and brand nameplates should remain faithful to the supplied references.
