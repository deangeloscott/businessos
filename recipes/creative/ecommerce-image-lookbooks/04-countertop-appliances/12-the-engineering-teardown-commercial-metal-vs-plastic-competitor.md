# Scale 04 — Archetype 12: The Engineering Contrast: Metal vs. Generic Reference

> A quick, adaptable visual starting point. Borrow the idea, change the product or treatment freely, combine it with another approach, or skip it when you already have a better direction.
>
> Optional context: [image library index](../INDEX.md) · [brand dialects](../DIALECTS.md)

* **Visual job:** The Proof
* **Illustrative examples:** Dual-boiler espresso machine, commercial high-torque blender, precision burr grinder, portable power station, heavy table saw.

## The idea

Make a premium difference visible by placing the hero beside a generic poor-quality reference on a dimension the buyer can understand immediately. Materials, construction, access, and finish can create a forceful pain-to-relief contrast without naming a competitor or inventing a failure. Keep the hero’s internals tied to supplied references and let the framing, ratio, and comparison dimension change with the product.

## Borrowable execution

- **What to make felt:** Tactile contrast: supplied forged brass versus a generic lightweight reference; supplied copper tubing versus a generic hose; a clear impression of construction without attributing an unverified failure.
- **One visual treatment:** Lens: 60mm Prime lens. Aperture: f/8 tack-sharp depth of field across both machines. Lighting: High-contrast commercial catalog lighting (5500K) with crisp contact floor shadows and zero reflections on the back wall.
- **Check:** Make the compared dimension obvious, label the generic reference and supplied hero attributes clearly, and keep the hero details faithful. Choose the layout and ratio that make this comparison easiest to understand.
