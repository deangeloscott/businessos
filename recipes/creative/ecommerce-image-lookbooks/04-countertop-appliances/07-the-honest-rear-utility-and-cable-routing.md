* **Visual job:** The Specimen
* **Illustrative examples:** Heavy grounded power cable, water intake line, rear ventilation exhaust, power station AC ports.

## The idea

Turn hidden installation logistics into useful product information. Show the rear or side view with the real cords, ports, hoses, vents, clearances, and routing paths that determine whether the product will work in the buyer’s space. Keep the treatment clean enough to inspect while preserving the practical complexity someone needs to plan around.

## Borrowable execution

- **What to make felt:** Engineering trust: thoughtful hidden cable grooves, heavy-duty industrial gauge wiring, clean flush wall-hugging profile.
- **One visual treatment:** Lens: 60mm Prime. Aperture: f/8. Lighting: Even studio fill illuminating dark recessed ports and cord channels clearly.
- **Check:** Electrical plug type, voltage rating stamps, and cord lengths must reflect actual production hardware.
