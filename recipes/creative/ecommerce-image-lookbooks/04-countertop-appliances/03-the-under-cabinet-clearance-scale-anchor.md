# Scale 04 — Archetype 03: The Under-Cabinet Clearance Scale Anchor

> A quick, adaptable visual starting point. Borrow the idea, change the product or treatment freely, combine it with another approach, or skip it when you already have a better direction.
>
> Optional context: [image library index](../INDEX.md) · [brand dialects](../DIALECTS.md)

* **Visual job:** The Specimen
* **Illustrative examples:** Heavy countertop mixer, bean-to-cup espresso machine, high-capacity toaster oven, commercial blender.

## The idea

Show the product in its intended space so a buyer can judge fit, operating clearance, and access at a glance. Build the scene from the supplied product and room measurements, including lids, handles, hoppers, or the space needed to use the product. If the product does not fit, make that useful limitation clear instead of inventing room. The same move works for a chair under a desk, luggage in a storage bay, or shelving in an alcove.

## Borrowable execution

- **What to make felt:** Let the measured gap and working space answer the fit question immediately. The example gap may be positive, zero, or negative; do not force a fit the dimensions do not support.
- **One visual treatment:** Lens: 35mm Architectural Perspective with vertical shift correction. Aperture: f/8. Lighting: Natural, realistic under-cabinet task lighting (3000K warm LED) combined with soft room daylight.
- **Check:** Cabinet and appliance proportions must reflect the supplied measurements; annotate the gap when the viewer needs a precise decision.
