* **Visual job:** The Proof
* **Illustrative examples:** Solid brass steam lever, diamond-knurled grind size collar, analog dual-pressure gauge, billet aluminum power dial.

## The idea

Zoom in on the control or touchpoint that makes the product’s quality tangible. Choose a meaningful detail—machining, texture, markings, resistance, or the way an interface meets a hand—and light it so the viewer can imagine using it. A generic reference can sharpen the contrast when it helps, but the hero’s materials and markings must come from the supplied product.

## Borrowable execution

- **What to make felt:** Mechanical delight: the apparent resistance of a metal dial and the crisp "thunk" of a solid switch can make the interface feel tangible.
- **One visual treatment:** Lens: 105mm Macro. Aperture: f/4. Lighting: Raking cross-light grazing across the metallic knurling, creating brilliant microscopic specular highlights on each geometric diamond facet.
- **Check:** Gauge typography, pressure PSI/bar units, and numerical markings should match the supplied machine references.
