# Scale 04 — Archetype 09: The Sensory Output Proof

> Standalone starting point from the [Scale 04 menu](../04-countertop-appliances.md). Adapt the bracketed inputs to the actual product, evidence, platform, and tool; inspect generated geometry, text, and any implied performance claim.
>
> Optional context: [image library index](../INDEX.md) · [brand dialects](../DIALECTS.md)

* **Universal Class:** The Proof
* **Example Products (Illustrative Guidance, Not an Allowlist):** Clear glass espresso cup with thick tiger crema, wood-fired pizza with leopard-spotted crust, silky vortex smoothie.
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   PDP Slot 2 & Social Ad Demonstration. Shows a plausible real-world output and makes the mechanism legible. Any result or “every time” language needs the actual recipe, settings, and evidence.
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   The appliance positioned directly alongside its freshly created masterpiece: clear glass cup with dense hazelnut crema, blistered Neapolitan pizza on a wooden peel, or live battery readout powering a full workstation.
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 70mm Prime.  
   * *Aperture:* f/4.  
   * *Lighting:* Rich, appetizing directional key light illuminating food gloss, steam, and crisp textures.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   Irresistible culinary craving or technical peace of mind: proving the machine turns a novice into a master.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   The output shown must be realistically achievable by a home consumer using the machine.
* **Director's Rig Prompt Blueprint:**
```text
[BLOCK 1: HERO & OUTPUT PROOF]: Commercial culinary performance photograph featuring [HERO PRODUCT: Appliance] alongside its fresh output: [CULINARY OUTPUT: e.g., clear glass cup catching a thick hazelnut espresso extraction with tiger crema], occupying 80% of frame width.
[BLOCK 2: OPTICAL RIG]: 70mm prime lens, eye-level perspective, f/4 aperture for crisp focus on the freshly extracted output and the machine group head.
[BLOCK 3: LIGHTING RIG]: Warm 4500K directional key light illuminating the golden glossy surface of the food/liquid, with soft fill on the machine chassis.
[BLOCK 4: SUBORDINATE STAGE]: Grounded on a dark soapstone countertop with a clean, softly blurred background.
[BLOCK 5: EXCLUSIONS]: Negative: thin watery output, burned food, messy spills, cluttered counter, blurry machine branding.
```
