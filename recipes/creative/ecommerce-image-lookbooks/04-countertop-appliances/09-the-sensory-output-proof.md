* **Visual job:** The Proof
* **Illustrative examples:** Clear glass espresso cup with thick tiger crema, wood-fired pizza with leopard-spotted crust, silky vortex smoothie.

## The idea

Connect the machine to the satisfying result the buyer wants. Show enough of the product, process, and output that the relationship feels credible—fresh food, a finished drink, a powered setup, or another visible result. Use the supplied recipe, settings, or test record for factual repeatability; without them, keep the result illustrative rather than promising “every time” performance.

## Borrowable execution

- **What to make felt:** A satisfying, believable result or technical peace of mind: show how the supplied product and process lead to the output.
- **One visual treatment:** Lens: 70mm Prime. Aperture: f/4. Lighting: Rich, appetizing directional key light illuminating food gloss, steam, and crisp textures.
- **Check:** The output shown must be realistically achievable by a home consumer using the machine.
