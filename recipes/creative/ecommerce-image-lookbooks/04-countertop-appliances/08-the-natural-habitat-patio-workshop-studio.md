# Scale 04 — Archetype 08: The Natural Habitat (Patio / Workshop / Studio)

> Standalone starting point from the [Scale 04 menu](../04-countertop-appliances.md). Adapt the bracketed inputs to the actual product, evidence, platform, and tool; inspect generated geometry, text, and any implied performance claim.
>
> Optional context: [image library index](../INDEX.md) · [brand dialects](../DIALECTS.md)

* **Universal Class:** The Habitat
* **Example Products (Illustrative Guidance, Not an Allowlist):** Outdoor pizza oven, portable battery power station, smart outdoor smoker, heavy-duty jobsite table saw.
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   Brand Campaign & Category Lifestyle. Places outdoor ovens, grills, or power stations in their native environment of peak social connection or workshop productivity.
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   An outdoor cedar pergola patio at twilight or an overland camper tailgate at sunrise. The appliance commands 70% of frame weight in tack-sharp focus.
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 50mm Prime.  
   * *Aperture:* f/2.8.  
   * *Lighting:* Blue hour twilight ambient balanced with the warm golden interior glow of the oven fire or the battery status screen.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   Aspirational hospitality: outdoor gatherings, the warmth of fire on a cool evening, rugged self-reliance.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   The appliance must remain the crisp focal hero; social background figures must be softly blurred in the background.
* **Director's Rig Prompt Blueprint:**
```text
[BLOCK 1: HERO HABITAT]: Commercial outdoor lifestyle photograph of [HERO PRODUCT: Outdoor oven, grill, or power station] in its natural habitat, centered and commanding 70% of frame weight in tack-sharp focus.
[BLOCK 2: OPTICAL RIG]: 50mm prime lens, eye-level perspective, f/2.8 aperture for shallow cinematic depth of field.
[BLOCK 3: LIGHTING RIG]: Blue hour twilight ambient light balanced against the warm 2700K golden glow radiating from the interior burner/screen, with soft rim highlights.
[BLOCK 4: SUBORDINATE STAGE]: Weathered stone patio or cedar table. In the soft-focus distant background (f/2.8 bokeh, 2.5 stops darker), friends gathered on a patio rest softly blurred.
[BLOCK 5: EXCLUSIONS]: Negative: messy dirty patio, cheap plastic lawn chairs, blurry appliance, suburban clutter, harsh daylight glare.
```
