# Scale 04 — Archetype 08: The Natural Habitat (Patio / Workshop / Studio)

* **Visual job:** The Habitat
* **Illustrative examples:** Outdoor pizza oven, portable battery power station, smart outdoor smoker, heavy-duty jobsite table saw.

## The idea

Place the product where its value actually happens: hosting, cooking, making, recording, charging, or working outdoors. Let the setting communicate the use case and desired lifestyle while the product remains the visual hero. A twilight patio, workshop, studio, or camp setup is a worked direction; choose the habitat that fits the real buyer and product.

## Borrowable execution

- **What to make felt:** Aspirational hospitality: outdoor gatherings, the warmth of fire on a cool evening, rugged self-reliance.
- **One visual treatment:** Lens: 50mm Prime. Aperture: f/2.8. Lighting: Blue hour twilight ambient balanced with the warm golden interior glow of the oven fire or the battery status screen.
- **Check:** The appliance must remain the crisp focal hero; social background figures must be softly blurred in the background.
