# Scale 04 — Archetype 02: The Active Performance State

> A quick, adaptable visual starting point. Borrow the idea, change the product or treatment freely, combine it with another approach, or skip it when you already have a better direction.
>
> Optional context: [image library index](../INDEX.md) · [brand dialects](../DIALECTS.md)

* **Visual job:** The Ritual
* **Illustrative examples:** Commercial espresso machine, countertop wood-fired pizza oven, high-wattage power station, professional air fryer.

## The idea

Show the product in the moment it performs its signature job. Make the useful output—steam, heat, motion, light, or a visible operating state—easy to read while keeping the machine and the action connected. Choose a dramatic active scene when it clarifies the benefit; use documented conditions when the image is making a performance claim.

## Borrowable execution

- **What to make felt:** Auditory and thermal memory: hearing the hiss of steam, the roar of a 900°F burner, the satisfying hum of a high-torque motor.
- **One visual treatment:** Lens: 85mm Prime. Shutter Speed: Fast shutter (1/1000s) capturing backlit steam droplets, flame licks, or bubbling liquids crisply. Aperture: f/4. Lighting: Controlled, slightly moody ambient lighting so self-illuminated features (flames, indicator LEDs, steam) pop with radiant glow.
- **Check:** Steam or flame must look physically authentic, not like flat CGI clip-art.
