# Scale 04 — Archetype 02: The Active Performance State

> Standalone starting point from the [Scale 04 menu](../04-countertop-appliances.md). Adapt the bracketed inputs to the actual product, evidence, platform, and tool; inspect generated geometry, text, and any implied performance claim.
>
> Optional context: [image library index](../INDEX.md) · [brand dialects](../DIALECTS.md)

* **Universal Class:** The Ritual
* **Example Products (Illustrative Guidance, Not an Allowlist):** Commercial espresso machine, countertop wood-fired pizza oven, high-wattage power station, professional air fryer.
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   PDP Slot 2 & Social Video/Ad Hook. Demonstrates the machine in full operational power, proving its thermodynamic or mechanical capability.
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   Kitchen counter or outdoor prep station. The appliance is running: visible steam billowing from a commercial wand, live rolling flame across an oven dome, or illuminated OLED interface displaying live output.
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 85mm Prime.  
   * *Shutter Speed:* Fast shutter (1/1000s) capturing backlit steam droplets, flame licks, or bubbling liquids crisply.  
   * *Aperture:* f/4.  
   * *Lighting:* Controlled, slightly moody ambient lighting so self-illuminated features (flames, indicator LEDs, steam) pop with radiant glow.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   Auditory and thermal memory: hearing the hiss of steam, the roar of a 900°F burner, the satisfying hum of a high-torque motor.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   Steam or flame must look physically authentic, not like flat CGI clip-art.
* **Director's Rig Prompt Blueprint:**
```text
[BLOCK 1: HERO & PERFORMANCE]: High-performance action photograph of [HERO PRODUCT: Appliance, material, finish] running at full operational power, occupying 75% of frame weight.
[BLOCK 2: ACTIVE PROOF]: [PERFORMANCE OUTPUT: e.g., high-pressure microfoam steam billowing from commercial steel wand, or live flame rolling across pizza stone], frozen at 1/1000s shutter speed with illuminated digital interface glowing crisply.
[BLOCK 3: OPTICS]: 85mm prime lens, eye-level perspective, f/4 aperture locking tack-sharp focus on the active output mechanism.
[BLOCK 4: STAGE & LIGHT]: Controlled 4800K ambient lighting allowing the steam and indicator dials to catch glowing back-rim highlights; dark slate counter.
[BLOCK 5: EXCLUSIONS]: Negative: artificial flat CGI fire, blurry dials, messy kitchen counter, water splatters on lens, dark underexposed shadows.
```
