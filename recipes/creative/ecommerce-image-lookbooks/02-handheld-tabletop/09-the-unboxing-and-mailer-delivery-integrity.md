# Scale 02 — Archetype 09: The Unboxing & Mailer Delivery Integrity

* **Visual job:** The Specimen
* **Good for:** setting expectations around a real delivery, protective packaging, gifting, and the first opening moment.
* **Fits:** pantry kits, wine shipments, coffee subscriptions, ceramics, and gear cases.

## The idea

Show the package opened far enough to understand how the product arrives. The insert, tissue, box, and product position make the delivery experience tangible and can relieve uncertainty about care and presentation.

## Borrow this treatment

Use a freshly opened mailer on a console, table, or kitchen island. Reveal the actual protective structure and one satisfying opening detail—tear strip, tissue, molded pulp, or hangtag—while keeping the product and any important label visible.

## Make it yours

Use the real fulfillment configuration, materials, and product count. Change the setting and light to match gifting, subscription, or everyday delivery. If packaging is still a concept, show it as a concept rather than implying shipping performance or an experience the business does not provide.

## Optional prompt seed

```text
Create an inviting unboxing image of the supplied [PRODUCT] arriving in its actual [MAILER OR BOX CONFIGURATION]. Show [PROTECTIVE OR BRANDED DETAIL] being opened on [SURFACE], with the product nested clearly and labels readable. Preserve the real fulfillment materials and avoid implying protection or packaging details that have not been supplied.
```
