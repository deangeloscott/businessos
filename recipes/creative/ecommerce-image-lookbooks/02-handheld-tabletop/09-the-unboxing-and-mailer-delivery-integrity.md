# Scale 02 — Archetype 09: The Unboxing & Mailer Delivery Integrity

> Optional context: [image library index](../INDEX.md) · [brand dialects](../DIALECTS.md)

* **Universal Class:** The Specimen
* **Example Products (Illustrative Guidance, Not an Allowlist):** Glass-bottled pantry kit, wine shipment box, artisanal coffee subscription box, ceramic tableware set, EDC gear case.
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   PDP Slot 7 & Post-Purchase Reassurance. Helps set expectations around protective packaging and gifting appeal.
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   A freshly opened custom corrugated shipping box resting on an entryway console table or kitchen island. The protective pulp insert or custom branded tissue is peeled back, revealing the products nested securely in pristine condition.
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 45mm Prime at a 45° angle looking into the box.  
   * *Aperture:* f/4.  
   * *Lighting:* Warm natural daylight streaming from an open doorway or window.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   The visceral thrill of delivery day: the crisp pull of cardboard tear-strips, pristine product labels arriving unblemished, thoughtful sustainable packing materials.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   Packaging structure shown must reflect the exact mailer used in fulfillment.
* **Director's Rig Prompt Blueprint:**
```text
[BLOCK 1: HERO UNBOXING]: High-end unboxing delivery photograph of [HERO PRODUCT: e.g., 2 glass bottles of olive oil] nestled securely inside an open custom branded kraft corrugated shipping box with protective molded pulp inserts.
[BLOCK 2: OPTICAL RIG]: 45mm prime lens, 45-degree overhead perspective looking into the box, f/4 aperture for crisp packaging detail.
[BLOCK 3: LIGHTING RIG]: Clean 5200K natural window daylight illuminating the interior of the box, showing pristine unblemished labels and zero shipping damage.
[BLOCK 4: SUBORDINATE STAGE]: Grounded on a clean white oak kitchen island. Background is soft and clean, 2 stops darker.
[BLOCK 5: EXCLUSIONS]: Negative: crushed cardboard, ripped torn paper, broken glass, packing peanut mess, plastic bubble wrap clutter, dark underexposed shadows.
```
