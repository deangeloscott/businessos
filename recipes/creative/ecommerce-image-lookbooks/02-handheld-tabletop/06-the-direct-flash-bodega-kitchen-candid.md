# Scale 02 — Archetype 06: The Direct-Flash Bodega / Kitchen Candid

* **Visual job:** The Ritual
* **Good for:** creating an unvarnished pattern interrupt for a food or beverage product in a social feed.
* **Fits:** canned lattes, hot honey, botanical water, crackers, and chili oil.

## The idea

Make the image feel like a great candid from a diner, bodega, kitchen, stoop, or late-night counter. Direct flash, ordinary surroundings, and a little imperfection can make a product feel immediate and culturally specific.

## Borrow this treatment

Use one believable place and one clear use moment. Hard flash can create crisp shadows and glossy packaging highlights; grain, slight crop tension, or an imperfect angle can add authenticity. Keep the product the sharpest readable object even when the scene feels spontaneous.

## Make it yours

Choose the setting, people, props, and cultural cues with the brand and audience in mind. Redirect the light if it blows out typography or color. A natural-light candid, disposable-camera treatment, or another lived-in approach may be a better fit.

## Optional prompt seed

```text
Create a candid, direct-flash image of the supplied [PRODUCT] in a specific [BODEGA, DINER, KITCHEN, OR STOOP] setting during [USE MOMENT]. Keep the product sharp and the label readable, with crisp shadows, believable everyday details, and restrained grain. Make the setting feel lived-in without adding unrelated clutter.
```
