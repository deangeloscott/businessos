# Scale 02 — Archetype 06: The Direct-Flash Bodega / Kitchen Candid

> Standalone starting point from the [Scale 02 menu](../02-handheld-tabletop.md). Adapt the bracketed inputs to the actual product, evidence, platform, and tool; inspect generated geometry, text, and any implied performance claim.
>
> Optional context: [image library index](../INDEX.md) · [brand dialects](../DIALECTS.md)

* **Universal Class:** The Ritual
* **Example Products (Illustrative Guidance, Not an Allowlist):** Canned specialty iced latte, hot honey squeeze bottle, sparkling botanical water, sourdough crackers, chili oil jar.
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   Paid Social (TikTok / Meta Pattern-Interrupt). Drives high click-through rates by feeling like an authentic friend's unvarnished photo rather than a polished commercial advertisement.
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   Casual, retro, or urban food setting: diner booth, linoleum kitchen counter, or grocery bag on a stoop.
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 28mm or 35mm wide-angle prime.  
   * *Aperture:* f/8.  
   * *Lighting:* Hard, point-source direct flash mounted on-camera. Deep, razor-sharp black shadow cast immediately behind the product. High contrast, film-like grain.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   Nostalgia, raw authenticity, unpretentious fun, mouthwatering late-night craving.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   Flash must not wash out typography on the packaging. The product remains the undisputed focal point.
* **Director's Rig Prompt Blueprint:**
```text
[BLOCK 1: HERO]: Candid direct-flash food photograph of [HERO PRODUCT: Can, jar, or bottle], resting boldly in the foreground and commanding 75% of frame weight in tack-sharp focus.
[BLOCK 2: OPTICAL RIG]: 35mm prime lens, eye-level angle, f/8 aperture with subtle 35mm film grain.
[BLOCK 3: LIGHTING RIG]: Hard, point-source direct on-camera flash casting a crisp, graphic pitch-black shadow directly behind the container, 0% ambient fill.
[BLOCK 4: SUBORDINATE STAGE]: Retro linoleum table or diner counter. Saturated, punchy colors, background underexposed (-2 EV) to keep full focus on the hero.
[BLOCK 5: EXCLUSIONS]: Negative: washed-out unreadable label text, blurry can, corporate minimalist staging, soft pastel wellness aesthetic.
```
