# Scale 02 — Archetype 03: The Kinetic Pour & High-Speed Viscosity

* **Visual job:** The Ritual
* **Good for:** making taste, flow, temperature, or texture feel present through one satisfying pour.
* **Fits:** olive oil, syrups, cold brew, honey, hot sauce, and cocktail mixers.

## The idea

Capture the product doing the thing people buy it to do. A stream into a glass, bowl, pan, or cup gives the viewer a physical cue for thickness, sparkle, volume, or use without requiring a long explanation.

## Borrow this treatment

Freeze or clearly shape the pour so the ribbon, droplets, bubbles, or condensation read as one controlled action. Backlight can make a translucent liquid glow; a simple stone or kitchen surface keeps the action central. Let the vessel support the product rather than become a second hero.

## Make it yours

Use the actual package and a plausible destination for the liquid. Choose the speed, camera distance, and light for the material; change them when a slower or more atmospheric action communicates better. Match color, viscosity, and contents to supplied references, and treat an imagined pour as a sensory concept.

## Optional prompt seed

```text

Keep the package recognizable, the fluid physically plausible, and any unverified result conceptual rather than a test claim.
