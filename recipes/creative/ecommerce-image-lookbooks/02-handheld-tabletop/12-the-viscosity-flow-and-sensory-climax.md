# Scale 02 — Archetype 12: The Viscosity Flow & Sensory Appetite Cue

* **Visual job:** The Ritual
* **Good for:** making a food or beverage's pour, sheen, density, or serving moment feel desirable.
* **Fits:** olive oil, maple syrup, honey, balsamic vinegar, and cold brew dispensers.

## The idea

Let one physical flow carry the sensory story: a ribbon into a dish, a drizzle over food, a glossy pour into a glass, or a controlled release from the package. The product remains the source of the action, not just a label beside an unrelated plate.

## Borrow this treatment

Use a simple warm surface such as stone, wood, or terracotta and keep the receiving vessel subordinate. Shape the flow with directional light so beads, sheen, and continuity read clearly. A close crop can feel indulgent; a wider context can show the use.

## Make it yours

Match the liquid, vessel, speed, and serving context to the supplied product. Use sensory language as an invitation, not as a hidden claim about dilution, purity, or quality. Try a still pool, splash, or hand action when a long ribbon is not the right expression.

## Optional prompt seed

```text
Create a sensory hero image of the supplied [PRODUCT] during one satisfying [POUR OR SERVING ACTION]. Make the supplied [COLOR, SHEEN, FLOW, OR TEXTURE] easy to see against a simple [SURFACE], keeping the package identifiable and the vessel subordinate. Preserve the actual contents and avoid turning appearance into an unsupported purity claim.
```
