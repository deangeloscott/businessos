> Optional context: [image library index](../INDEX.md) · [brand dialects](../DIALECTS.md)

* **Universal Class:** The Specimen
* **Example Products (Illustrative Guidance, Not an Allowlist):** 16oz insulated travel mug, 750ml glass wine bottle, EDC titanium flashlight, ground coffee can, hot sauce bottle.
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   PDP Slot 4 (Scale & Ergonomics). Helps reduce uncertainty around package size, volume, and ease of handling.
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   A casual, authentic human hand naturally holding the product upright, unscrewing the cap, or pouring from it. Shot in a bright, modern domestic or outdoor setting.
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 50mm Prime.  
   * *Aperture:* f/4.  
   * *Lighting:* Clean, directional daylight balancing skin tones with packaging color.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   Tactile ergonomic reassurance: showing how fingers wrap around the bottle/can, the satisfying mechanical scale, proof that the product is neither tiny nor unwieldy.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   Hand must be realistically proportioned to standard human anatomy. Zero AI knuckle distortion or rubbery skin artifacts. Nails and skin styled cleanly and neutrally.
* **Director's Rig Prompt Blueprint:**
```text
[BLOCK 1: HERO & HUMAN SCALE]: Commercial ergonomic scale photograph of [HERO PRODUCT: Container, material, finish, volume] held comfortably in an authentic adult hand. The product occupies 70% of vertical frame height in tack-sharp focus.
[BLOCK 2: OPTICAL RIG]: 50mm prime lens, eye-level perspective, f/4 aperture providing realistic depth of field.
[BLOCK 3: LIGHTING RIG]: Clean 5200K diffused daylight balancing natural skin tones with accurate packaging colors, soft contact shadow where fingers meet container.
[BLOCK 4: SUBORDINATE STAGE]: Clean, minimalist neutral background (f/2.8 blur, 2 stops darker). Hand has natural un-airbrushed skin texture and neutral neat manicure.
[BLOCK 5: EXCLUSIONS]: Negative: extra fingers, distorted knuckles, rubbery cartoon hand, plastic skin, warped packaging dimensions, cluttered room.
```
