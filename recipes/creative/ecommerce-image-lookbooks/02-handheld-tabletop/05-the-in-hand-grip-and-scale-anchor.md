# Scale 02 — Archetype 05: The In-Hand Grip & Scale Anchor

* **Visual job:** The Specimen
* **Good for:** making package size, weight, grip, and everyday handling understandable.
* **Fits:** travel mugs, glass bottles, flashlights, coffee cans, and pantry containers.

## The idea

Show a real hand performing a normal product action: holding, opening, carrying, or pouring. The relationship between fingers and package gives a faster scale cue than a dimension list alone.

## Borrow this treatment

Use a relaxed grip, clean daylight, and a simple domestic or outdoor context. Let the hand wrap naturally around the product, with enough of the label and action visible to understand the use. Keep perspective calm so the object does not look artificially large or small.

## Make it yours

Use actual dimensions and the intended action. Match skin, styling, and setting to the audience, and change the camera or crop when it improves the relationship. Inspect fingers, knuckles, contact, and packaging proportions in the result.

## Optional prompt seed

```text
Show the supplied [PRODUCT AND DIMENSIONS] in a natural hand performing [ACTION]. Make grip, scale, and use immediately clear with realistic anatomy, clean directional light, and a quiet background. Keep the package and label faithful and avoid perspective that exaggerates its size.
```
