# Scale 02 — Archetype 14: The Multi-Item Merchandising Routine & Bundle Matrix

> Standalone starting point from the [Scale 02 menu](../02-handheld-tabletop.md). Adapt the bracketed inputs to the actual product, evidence, platform, and tool; inspect generated geometry, text, and any implied performance claim.
>
> Optional context: [image library index](../INDEX.md) · [brand dialects](../DIALECTS.md)

* **Universal Class:** The Specimen
* **Example Products (Illustrative Guidance, Not an Allowlist):** Coffee bean bag + ceramic dripper + grinder, olive oil + balsamic pair, artisanal pantry trio, EDC knife + pen + wallet kit.
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   Bundle PDPs & Cart AOV Escalation. Sells the complete daily ritual as an inseparable system, driving higher basket size.
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   Monolithic architectural pedestal (honed travertine, blackened steel, or solid white oak). 2–3 complementary products arranged with strict mathematical spacing and uniform shadow geometry.
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 70mm Prime.  
   * *Aperture:* f/8 for uniform front-to-back sharpness across all products.  
   * *Lighting:* Single cohesive directional softbox at 45° ensuring identical shadow angles across all items.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   The psychological satisfaction of order, complete preparation, and elevated daily habit.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   Relative product scales must be 100% faithful to reality. Products occupy 80% of total frame width.
* **Director's Rig Prompt Blueprint:**
```text
[BLOCK 1: HERO BUNDLE]: Commercial multi-item routine photograph of [HERO BUNDLE: Product A, Product B, and Product C], arranged in clean, balanced mathematical spacing, occupying 80% of total frame width.
[BLOCK 2: OPTICAL RIG]: 70mm prime lens, 20-degree elevated perspective, f/8 aperture for uniform edge-to-edge sharpness across all packaging.
[BLOCK 3: LIGHTING RIG]: Cohesive 5200K directional softbox from camera-left casting matching soft shadows, with crisp contact base shadows grounding each product.
[BLOCK 4: SUBORDINATE STAGE]: Monolithic honed travertine or white oak pedestal against a clean neutral light-gray background. Zero clutter.
[BLOCK 5: EXCLUSIONS]: Negative: mismatched shadow angles, incorrect relative scales, unreadable branding, domestic clutter, dirty fingerprints.
```
