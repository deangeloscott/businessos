# Scale 02 — Archetype 01: The Catalog Packshot

* **Visual job:** The Specimen
* **Good for:** making a handheld product instantly identifiable in a shopping feed, catalog, or product page.
* **Fits:** olive oil bottles, coffee bags, hot sauce, travel mugs, and pocket organizers.

## The idea

Let the package carry the frame. A quiet background, clear label, believable base, and controlled reflection make shape, material, and finish easy to understand before the viewer explores anything else.

## Borrow this treatment

Use a seamless neutral field and direct or slightly elevated framing. Give the curved package a restrained highlight, keep the label evenly lit, and add a small contact shadow so it feels present on the surface. Keep props and graphics out when recognition is the job.

## Make it yours

Use the actual package, label, dimensions, crop, and platform requirements. Change light, angle, or background when they improve legibility. Preserve supplied typography, color, closures, and geometry; a prompt cannot repair a missing reference after the fact.

## Optional prompt seed

```text
Create a clean catalog hero of the supplied [PRODUCT]. Make its package shape, material, finish, and label immediately legible on a quiet seamless background. Use even product light, a restrained edge highlight, and a believable contact shadow; preserve the supplied typography and compose for [PLACEMENT].
```
