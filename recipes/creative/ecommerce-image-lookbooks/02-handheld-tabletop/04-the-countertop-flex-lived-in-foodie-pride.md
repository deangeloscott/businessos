# Scale 02 — Archetype 04: The Countertop Flex (Lived-In Foodie Pride)

* **Visual job:** The Habitat
* **Good for:** making a useful food or kitchen product feel proud, lived-in, and worth displaying.
* **Fits:** hot sauce, chili crisp, finishing salt, cold brew, and cocktail bitters.

## The idea

Treat the product as part of an active kitchen rather than an isolated package. A little evidence of preparation—salt, crumbs, a spoon, a cut citrus, or an open ingredient—can communicate everyday pleasure and capability.

## Borrow this treatment

Use a butcher block, stainless counter, diner tile, or another surface with a point of view. Keep the props authentic and sparse, give the product the strongest hierarchy, and use crisp daylight or flash to make the scene feel captured rather than over-styled.

## Make it yours

Choose the kitchen culture, ingredients, and hand action that belong to the brand. Remove any prop that competes with the label or implies a recipe, flavor, or claim not supported by the product. The “lived-in” feeling can be clean, minimal, playful, or messy in a controlled way.

## Optional prompt seed

```text

Match every visible ingredient and use state to the supplied brand and product.
