# Scale 02 — Archetype 04: The Countertop Flex (Lived-In Foodie Pride)

> Standalone starting point from the [Scale 02 menu](../02-handheld-tabletop.md). Adapt the bracketed inputs to the actual product, evidence, platform, and tool; inspect generated geometry, text, and any implied performance claim.
>
> Optional context: [image library index](../INDEX.md) · [brand dialects](../DIALECTS.md)

* **Universal Class:** The Habitat
* **Example Products (Illustrative Guidance, Not an Allowlist):** Artisanal hot sauce, chili crisp jar, gourmet finishing salt, cold brew carton, cocktail bitters.
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   Social Ads & Community Proof. Positions the product not just as food, but as a design-forward lifestyle accessory that consumers proudly display on their kitchen counters.
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   An active home chef's prep area: stainless steel counter or butcher block, flanked by authentic culinary prep elements (flaky sea salt in a ceramic cellar, a wooden spoon resting on a saucer, a freshly sliced lemon, micro-crumbs).
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 35mm or 50mm Prime.  
   * *Aperture:* f/4.  
   * *Lighting:* Bright, crisp midday natural kitchen light with punchy, high-contrast character.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   Freshness, culinary mastery, effortless everyday luxury; tactile matte packaging standing out proudly against utilitarian kitchen equipment.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   The hero product must dominate 65% of the visual hierarchy; kitchen props must never obscure the label or compete in saturation.
* **Director's Rig Prompt Blueprint:**
```text
[BLOCK 1: HERO]: Commercial food lifestyle photograph of [HERO PRODUCT: Jar, squeeze bottle, or tin], standing proudly in the foreground commanding 65% of visual weight in tack-sharp focus.
[BLOCK 2: OPTICAL RIG]: 50mm prime lens, eye-level perspective, f/4 aperture for natural depth separation.
[BLOCK 3: LIGHTING RIG]: Bright 5400K natural window light with crisp, graphic cast shadows and clean specular glints on the packaging.
[BLOCK 4: SUBORDINATE STAGE]: Stainless steel prep counter or wooden butcher block. In the soft-focus background (f/2.8 blur, 2 stops darker), a ceramic pinch bowl of flaky salt and fresh culinary herbs rest out of focus.
[BLOCK 5: EXCLUSIONS]: Negative: filthy stove, greasy pans, messy trash, unreadable label, distorted bottle text, dark gloomy atmosphere.
```
