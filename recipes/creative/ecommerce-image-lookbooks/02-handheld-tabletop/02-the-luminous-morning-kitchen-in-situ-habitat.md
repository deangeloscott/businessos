# Scale 02 — Archetype 02: The Luminous Morning Kitchen (In-Situ Habitat)

* **Visual job:** The Habitat
* **Good for:** connecting a food, beverage, or kitchen object to a warm daily ritual.
* **Fits:** coffee, tea, olive oil, bread knives, and pour-over tools.

## The idea

Show the product where it will be used: on a morning counter, beside a cup, during prep, or at the first meal of the day. A believable habitat helps the viewer imagine the product in their life while the package remains easy to find.

## Borrow this treatment

Pair the supplied product with one or two supporting cues—a ceramic mug, linen towel, steam, wood, or quiet cabinetry. Warm directional window light and a soft background can suggest aroma and comfort without turning the image into a prop catalogue.

## Make it yours

Choose the ritual, surface, time of day, and supporting objects that fit the product and audience. Use actual contents or preparation details where they matter, and keep the product and label readable at the intended crop.
