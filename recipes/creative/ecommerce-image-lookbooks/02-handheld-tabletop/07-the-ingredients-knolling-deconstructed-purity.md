# Scale 02 — Archetype 07: The Ingredients Knolling (Deconstructed Purity)

* **Visual job:** The Proof
* **Good for:** making supplied formulation, sourcing, or preparation information visible at a glance.
* **Fits:** coffee, granola, loose-leaf tea, snack bars, and sauces.

## The idea

Lay the package beside the ingredients or materials that explain it. A measured overhead arrangement can turn a list into a clear visual story, as long as the visible components actually belong to the supplied product.

## Borrow this treatment

Use a simple top-down field—stone, butcher paper, linen, or matte board—with the package as the anchor. Arrange a few raw materials in deliberate groups, leaving breathing room around the label. Soft even light keeps the set legible; a controlled shadow can separate pieces without making it busy.

## Make it yours

Use actual formulation or sourcing references for factual disclosure. When contents are unknown, keep the props generic and label the scene as illustrative. Choose a grid, loose arrangement, or partial crop that fits the product instead of treating knolling as a requirement.

## Optional prompt seed

```text
Create an overhead still life of the supplied [PRODUCT] with [DOCUMENTED INGREDIENTS OR SOURCE MATERIALS] arranged around it. Keep the package centered and readable, use a restrained [SURFACE], and let spacing and soft even light make the relationship clear. If the contents are illustrative, say so and do not present them as a literal ingredient list.
```
