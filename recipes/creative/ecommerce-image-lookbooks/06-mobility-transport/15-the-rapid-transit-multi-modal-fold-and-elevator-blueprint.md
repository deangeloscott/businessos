## The idea

Show a folded product moving through the everyday route that makes multi-modal travel valuable. Make the folded footprint, rolling or carrying interface, doorway, elevator, or storage point easy to compare, using measured dimensions when the image promises fit. If the route is unverified, a planning diagram or labeled concept is more useful than effortless-looking passage.

## Borrowable execution

- **What to make felt:** Multi-modal relief: show the supplied fold sequence and rolling interface. State fold time or cleanliness benefits only when measured or documented.
- **One visual treatment:** Lens: 35mm Prime. Aperture: f/5.6. Lighting: Clean, diffused modern architectural elevator lighting with soft contact floor shadows.
- **Check:** Elevator and transit dimensions must reflect the actual route and product footprint. The folded footprint should be clearly visible on the floor plane.
