# Scale 06 — Archetype 10: The Documented Long-Use Commuter Demonstration

> A quick, adaptable visual starting point. Borrow the idea, change the product or treatment freely, combine it with another approach, or skip it when you already have a better direction.
>
> Optional context: [image library index](../INDEX.md) · [brand dialects](../DIALECTS.md)

* **Visual job:** The Proof
* **Illustrative examples:** Gates carbon belt drive e-bike, heavy-duty commercial cargo bike, daily urban electric scooter.

## The idea

Use a documented mileage, service history, battery condition, or observed wear state to make long-term value visible. A cared-for vehicle with honest road patina can communicate pride and durability; a generated patina is only a concept until a record supports it. Show the components and condition that matter without inventing an odometer, savings result, or replacement claim.

## Borrowable execution

- **What to make felt:** Pride of ownership and long-use value: connect mileage, service history, battery condition, cost savings, or current performance only to the supplied record; a patina scene can remain a conceptual long-use illustration.
- **One visual treatment:** Lens: 50mm Prime. Aperture: f/5.6. Lighting: Honest, crisp morning workshop daylight.
- **Check:** Patina must look like authentic, dignified road use; frame welds and structural components must remain visibly sound and crack-free.
