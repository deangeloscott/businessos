* **Visual job:** The Proof
* **Illustrative examples:** City commuter e-bike, smart LED electric scooter, night-riding cargo trike.

## The idea

Make night visibility and the lighting system’s shape legible in a responsible context. Show the supplied headlight, rear light, reflectors, beam cutoff, and surrounding conditions clearly enough to inspect, using a road or test setup that matches the intended use. Avoid turning a dramatic night image into a universal safety or distance promise.

## Borrowable execution

- **What to make felt:** Safety-oriented confidence: show the documented field of view, beam cutoff, and visibility conditions without promising a universal distance or 360-degree result.
- **One visual treatment:** Lens: 50mm Prime at f/2.8. Lighting: Long exposure night shot capturing the luminous beam of the headlight cutting through darkness, with city streetlights reflecting in wet pavement.
- **Check:** Headlight beam pattern must reflect actual optical lens design (German StVZO cut-off beam pattern vs blinding floodlight).
