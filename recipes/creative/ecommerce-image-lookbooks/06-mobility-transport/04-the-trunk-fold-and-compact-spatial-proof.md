# Scale 06 — Archetype 04: The Trunk-Fold & Compact Spatial Proof

> A quick, adaptable visual starting point. Borrow the idea, change the product or treatment freely, combine it with another approach, or skip it when you already have a better direction.
>
> Optional context: [image library index](../INDEX.md) · [brand dialects](../DIALECTS.md)

* **Visual job:** The Specimen
* **Illustrative examples:** Folding commuter e-bike, folding electric scooter, compact cargo bike, collapsible adventure trailer.

## The idea

Make the folded footprint and the remaining vehicle or storage clearance obvious. Show the actual trunk, desk, closet, or other destination when available; otherwise label the vehicle and space as illustrative and avoid implying a fit. A hinge action, measured overlay, or clean side-by-side can all communicate compactness quickly.

## Borrowable execution

- **What to make felt:** A clear spatial read: *"It takes up less than half the trunk—I can take this on road trips easily."* Show smooth folding hinge levers and magnetic clasp closures when they are part of the supplied product.
- **One visual treatment:** Lens: 35mm Prime looking down into the open trunk. Aperture: f/5.6. Lighting: Diffused daylight with soft fill illuminating inside the dark trunk liner.
- **Check:** The destination and folded product must match the actual vehicle or storage geometry; if the destination is illustrative, label it as such rather than implying a universal fit.
