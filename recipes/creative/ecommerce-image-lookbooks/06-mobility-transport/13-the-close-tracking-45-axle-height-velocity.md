> Optional context: [image library index](../INDEX.md) · [brand dialects](../DIALECTS.md)

* **Universal Class:** The Ritual
* **Example Products (Illustrative Guidance, Not an Allowlist):** Belt-drive commuter e-bike, high-speed electric scooter, urban gravel adventurer rig, single-speed city bike.
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   Hero Landing Page Header & Brand Equity (Kinetic Freedom & Engineering Prestige). Puts frame geometry, carbon belt drive, and disc brakes front and center, eliminating the "scenery postcard" trap.
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   Close-tracking commercial hero shot captured from a 45° front-three-quarter profile at axle height. The vehicle frame, hydroformed downtube, and hydraulic disc brakes occupy 75% of the frame in tack-sharp foreground focus.
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 50mm Prime at axle height.  
   * *Shutter Speed:* 1/40s smooth horizontal tracking blur.  
   * *Lighting:* Low-angle twilight or dusk ambient with bridge lights streaking into soft horizontal motion bokeh.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   Pure mechanical precision: whisper-quiet Gates carbon belt drive, hydraulic rotor gloss, smooth rolling velocity.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   The vehicle occupies 75% of frame weight. Background scenery is strictly blurred into smooth motion streaks.
* **Director's Rig Prompt Blueprint:**
