# Scale 06 — Archetype 13: The Close-Tracking 45° Axle-Height Velocity

> A quick, adaptable visual starting point. Borrow the idea, change the product or treatment freely, combine it with another approach, or skip it when you already have a better direction.
>
> Optional context: [image library index](../INDEX.md) · [brand dialects](../DIALECTS.md)

* **Visual job:** The Ritual
* **Illustrative examples:** Belt-drive commuter e-bike, high-speed electric scooter, urban gravel adventurer rig, single-speed city bike.

## The idea

Keep the vehicle’s geometry, materials, and drivetrain as the visual hero while motion suggests speed and smoothness. A close tracking shot with a blurred city background is one strong execution; a locked detail, road-level frame, or different motion treatment may serve another product better. Preserve the supplied components and let the background support rather than obscure them.

## Borrowable execution

- **What to make felt:** Pure mechanical precision: whisper-quiet Gates carbon belt drive, hydraulic rotor gloss, smooth rolling velocity.
- **One visual treatment:** Lens: 50mm Prime at axle height. Shutter Speed: 1/40s smooth horizontal tracking blur. Lighting: Low-angle twilight or dusk ambient with bridge lights streaking into soft horizontal motion bokeh.
- **Check:** Keep the vehicle’s frame, wheels, drivetrain, and rider contact clear enough to inspect; use motion blur only where it supports the intended sense of movement.
