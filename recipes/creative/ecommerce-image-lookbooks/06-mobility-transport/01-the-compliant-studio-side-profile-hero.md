# Scale 06 — Archetype 01: The Studio Side-Profile Hero

> A quick, adaptable visual starting point. Borrow the idea, change the product or treatment freely, combine it with another approach, or skip it when you already have a better direction.
>
> Optional context: [image library index](../INDEX.md) · [brand dialects](../DIALECTS.md)

* **Visual job:** The Specimen
* **Illustrative examples:** Commuter e-bike, electric stand-up scooter, single-wheel electric board, overland cargo trailer, carbon gravel bike.

## The idea

Give the vehicle a clean identity and geometry reference. A true side profile makes frame shape, wheelbase, tires, drivetrain, and supplied decals easy to inspect and reuse across catalogs or comparisons. The seamless studio is a useful execution; the underlying idea is faithful, distortion-controlled visibility.

## Borrowable execution

- **What to make felt:** Machined mechanical precision: the tautness of the drive chain/belt, cold density of hydroformed aluminum tubing, rugged rubber tire tread.
- **One visual treatment:** Lens: 85mm Prime positioned 15 feet back at axle height to eliminate perspective keystoning (both wheels appear identical in size). Aperture: f/8 to f/11 for corner-to-corner mechanical sharpness. Lighting: Commercial automotive studio softbox array casting long, smooth highlight ribbons along the top tube, down tube, and wheel rims. Shadow: Crisp, razor-thin black contact shadow directly under both tire contact patches.
- **Check:** Vehicle occupies a useful share of the frame. Frame decals, motor badges, and battery housing should match supplied CAD/product references; inspect the final render rather than assuming fidelity.
