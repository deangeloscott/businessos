* **Visual job:** The Habitat
* **Illustrative examples:** Hard-shell rooftop tent on SUV, overland adventure trailer, fat-tire backcountry e-bike, expedition rack.

## The idea

Place the vehicle in the kind of remote setting that makes its exploration or self-contained features desirable. Show the actual deployment, mounting, storage, or camp interaction that matters, with the landscape supporting rather than swallowing the product. Golden-hour ridges and rooftop tents are one execution; adapt the route, weather, and activity to the real offering.

## Borrowable execution

- **What to make felt:** Freedom, silence, fresh mountain air; the rugged satisfaction of the supplied deployment sequence. State deployment time or site range only from documentation or observation.
- **One visual treatment:** Lens: 35mm Wide-Angle Prime. Aperture: f/5.6. Lighting: Dramatic golden hour sunset backlighting the ridge, balanced against interior tent illumination and exterior landscape mood.
- **Check:** Tent canvas tension, gas-strut hardware, and vehicle mounting brackets must be structurally accurate to real engineering.
