# Scale 06 — Archetype 02: The Kinetic Urban Commute (Panning Velocity)

> A quick, adaptable visual starting point. Borrow the idea, change the product or treatment freely, combine it with another approach, or skip it when you already have a better direction.
>
> Optional context: [image library index](../INDEX.md) · [brand dialects](../DIALECTS.md)

* **Visual job:** The Ritual
* **Illustrative examples:** Commuter e-bike, belt-drive city bike, high-speed electric scooter, performance electric skateboard.

## The idea

Communicate movement, freedom, and everyday momentum by showing the vehicle traveling through a believable route. Keep the vehicle and rider readable while the background carries the motion through panning or directional blur. Use documented speed only when the image or copy makes that factual claim; an illustrative pace can carry the feeling without a number.

## Borrowable execution

- **What to make felt:** Kinetic wind in your face, the documented or intended sound of the motor, and the feeling of moving through city traffic.
- **One visual treatment:** Lens: 50mm Prime mounted on a tracking chase vehicle. Shutter Speed: 1/30s to 1/60s panning shutter. The vehicle frame, handlebars, and rider’s torso are tack-sharp, while the background cityscape, asphalt, and wheel spokes blur into smooth horizontal motion streaks. Lighting: Low-angle morning sunlight casting golden highlights along the frame and rider's helmet.
- **Check:** Rider must wear safety gear (helmet, clean commuter wardrobe). Frame must remain tack-sharp despite motion-blurred background.
