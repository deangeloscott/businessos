# Scale 06 — Archetype 09: All-Weather Water & Mud Demonstration

> A quick, adaptable visual starting point. Borrow the idea, change the product or treatment freely, combine it with another approach, or skip it when you already have a better direction.

> Optional context: [image library index](../INDEX.md) · [brand dialects](../DIALECTS.md)

* **Visual job:** The Proof
* **Illustrative examples:** Fat-tire trail e-bike, IPX6 sealed commuter scooter, all-terrain electric board, gravel cargo rig.

## The idea

Show weather meeting the vehicle within the limits the product is designed and documented to handle. A splash, rain-slick route, or exposed trail can make the protective construction and user confidence tangible; pair it with the actual rating and test method for a claim. If those are unavailable, keep the moment clearly illustrative and show the limit rather than an unrestricted result.

## Borrowable execution

- **What to make felt:** All-season reliability: make the product's documented weather limits and protective construction visible without turning an image into an unrestricted safety promise.
- **One visual treatment:** Lens: 85mm Prime. Shutter Speed: 1/2000s freeze-frame capturing individual suspended water droplets spraying from the tire tread. Aperture: f/4. Lighting: Directional side light illuminating the water arc and water beading off the powder-coated frame.
- **Check:** Water sealing claims must reflect real IPX testing ratings.
