* **Illustrative examples:** Removable downtube e-bike battery, modular scooter battery pack, portable camping generator power cell.

## The idea

Show how the product solves charging in the user’s actual life. Make the battery path, lock, connector, carrying point, or charging setup legible in one natural action, and choose a home, garage, or workplace context that explains why it matters. Use the supplied weight, dimensions, and charging method rather than inventing an easy lift or universal setup.

## Borrowable execution

- **What to make felt:** The satisfying mechanical “click” of the security lock disengaging; the relief of knowing the supplied battery weight and charging requirements.
- **One visual treatment:** Lens: 50mm Prime. Aperture: f/4. Lighting: Clean, high-clarity natural daylight illuminating the battery connector pins, key lock mechanism, and handle grip.
- **Check:** Battery keyhole, LED charge-indicator lights, and carrying handle should match the supplied production references.
