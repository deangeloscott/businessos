## The idea

Show the vehicle doing a useful real-world job—errands, child transport, deliveries, or gear hauling—so capacity becomes tangible. Arrange the load to reveal the rack, bags, tie-downs, and access rather than relying on an overflowing still life. Use the supplied accessories, ratings, and safety conditions for factual payload claims.

## Borrowable execution

- **What to make felt:** Structural utility: show the supplied rack construction and documented payload conditions; a loaded scene can dramatize relief from car-dependent errands without claiming a payload or frame result from the render.
- **One visual treatment:** Lens: 50mm Prime. Aperture: f/4. Lighting: Warm late-afternoon natural city light.
- **Check:** Cargo accessories, load, rider, and safety setup should match the supplied rating and conditions when making a factual payload claim; otherwise keep the scene illustrative.
