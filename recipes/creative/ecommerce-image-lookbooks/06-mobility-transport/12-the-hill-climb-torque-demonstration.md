# Scale 06 — Archetype 12: The Hill-Climb Torque Demonstration

> A quick, adaptable visual starting point. Borrow the idea, change the product or treatment freely, combine it with another approach, or skip it when you already have a better direction.
>
> Optional context: [image library index](../INDEX.md) · [brand dialects](../DIALECTS.md)

* **Visual job:** The Proof
* **Illustrative examples:** 750W commuter e-bike, high-torque cargo bike, dual-motor electric scooter, electric mountain bike.

## The idea

Make a hill or loaded route feel like a concrete decision rather than an abstract motor specification. Show the vehicle, rider, grade, payload, and resulting experience together; use the actual test inputs for performance claims. Without a test record, a low-angle climb can dramatize intended relief as a concept but should not invent effortless output.

## Borrowable execution

- **What to make felt:** Torque relief: a relaxed posture and smooth momentum can dramatize the desired experience; “effortless,” zero sweating, and a specific grade require evidence.
- **One visual treatment:** Lens: 50mm Prime lens at low angle looking up the hill. Aperture: f/5.6 for sharp focus on the vehicle and rider. Lighting: Crisp, clean morning sunlight casting long directional shadows down the hill, highlighting the steep gradient.
- **Check:** Keep the vehicle, rider, grade, and route readable enough to understand the challenge; preserve supplied geometry and test context when the image supports a performance claim.
