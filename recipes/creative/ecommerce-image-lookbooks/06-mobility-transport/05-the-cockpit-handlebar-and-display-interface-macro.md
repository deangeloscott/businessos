# Scale 06 — Archetype 05: The Cockpit, Handlebar & Display Interface Macro

> A quick, adaptable visual starting point. Borrow the idea, change the product or treatment freely, combine it with another approach, or skip it when you already have a better direction.
>
> Optional context: [image library index](../INDEX.md) · [brand dialects](../DIALECTS.md)

* **Visual job:** The Proof
* **Illustrative examples:** Smart e-bike cockpit, electric scooter heads-up display, GPS cargo console, e-motorcycle digital dash.

## The idea

Give the viewer a rider’s-eye understanding of the controls and display. Keep the supplied interface, readable state, hand position, and road context connected so someone can picture operating it. Use actual UI and readings from the product or a documented state; do not fill a screen with invented numbers just to make the frame look active.

## Borrowable execution

- **What to make felt:** The crisp tactile squeeze of hydraulic brake levers; high-resolution readability of the digital speedometer in full sunlight.
- **One visual treatment:** Lens: 35mm Prime from rider's eye-level. Aperture: f/4 (cockpit controls in tack-sharp focus, front wheel and asphalt road softly falling off ahead). Lighting: Bright overhead daylight arranged to keep the supplied display readable; inspect glare and reflections.
- **Check:** Screen UI, battery percentage font, and speedometer numbers must match supplied firmware references or a documented observed state; do not invent readings.
