# Scale 06 — Archetype 11: The Ergonomic Geometry, Standover & Dimensional Fit Blueprint

> A quick, adaptable visual starting point. Borrow the idea, change the product or treatment freely, combine it with another approach, or skip it when you already have a better direction.
>
> Optional context: [image library index](../INDEX.md) · [brand dialects](../DIALECTS.md)

* **Visual job:** The Specimen
* **Illustrative examples:** Step-through e-bike frame, diamond-frame mountain bike, stand-up folding scooter, cargo bike chassis.

## The idea

Make rider fit and frame geometry easy to inspect before purchase. Combine a measured side view, useful dimension lines, and a representative rider relationship so standover, reach, saddle range, and wheelbase answer real fit questions. Use manufacturer geometry and the chosen unit system; a technical CAD grid is a visual option, not the concept itself.

## Borrowable execution

- **What to make felt:** Engineered reassurance and personalized ergonomic fit: helping the viewer picture straddling the frame and putting a foot down at traffic stops.
- **One visual treatment:** Lens / Perspective: 135mm Telephoto at axle height with zero keystoning or wheel distortion. Aperture: f/11 for absolute corner-to-corner mechanical sharpness. Lighting: Clean, shadowless high-key technical lighting with high-contrast graphic callouts.
- **Check:** Dimensions must be mathematically accurate to manufacturer CAD specs in both Imperial (inches) and Metric (cm).
