# Scale 06 — Archetype 11: The Ergonomic Geometry, Standover & Dimensional Fit Blueprint

> Standalone starting point from the [Scale 06 menu](../06-mobility-transport.md). Adapt the bracketed inputs to the actual product, evidence, platform, and tool; inspect generated geometry, text, and any implied performance claim.
>
> Optional context: [image library index](../INDEX.md) · [brand dialects](../DIALECTS.md)

* **Universal Class:** The Specimen
* **Example Products (Illustrative Guidance, Not an Allowlist):** Step-through e-bike frame, diamond-frame mountain bike, stand-up folding scooter, cargo bike chassis.
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   PDP Slot 4 & Sizing De-risking. Helps address fit anxiety: *"Will this frame fit someone 5'3" or 6'2"? What is the exact standover height and reach?"* Provide sizing clarity from the supplied geometry and fit guidance.
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   A clean, technical studio environment with a subtle architectural CAD grid background (RGB 245, 245, 248). The vehicle is presented in pure side-profile orthographic projection with crisp, high-contrast graphic measurement dimension lines:
   * Standover clearance (ground to top-tube).
   * Minimum and maximum saddle adjustment range.
   * Handlebar reach and cockpit angle.
   * Wheelbase and total bumper-to-bumper length.
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens / Perspective:* 135mm Telephoto at axle height with zero keystoning or wheel distortion.  
   * *Aperture:* f/11 for absolute corner-to-corner mechanical sharpness.  
   * *Lighting:* Clean, shadowless high-key technical lighting with high-contrast graphic callouts.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   Engineered reassurance and personalized ergonomic fit: instantly visualizing oneself straddling the frame, effortlessly putting a foot down at traffic stops.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   Dimensions must be mathematically accurate to manufacturer CAD specs in both Imperial (inches) and Metric (cm).
* **Director's Rig Prompt Blueprint:**
```text
[BLOCK 1: HERO DIMENSIONAL BLUEPRINT]: Commercial technical sizing specification photograph of [HERO PRODUCT: E-bike / scooter] in pure parallel side-profile orthographic projection, occupying 85% of frame width in tack-sharp focus.
[BLOCK 2: GEOMETRY DETAIL]: Clean graphic architectural overlay lines indicate standover height, minimum/maximum seat height adjustment, and total bumper-to-bumper length on an architectural CAD grid.
[BLOCK 3: OPTICS]: 135mm telephoto lens at axle height, f/11 aperture for edge-to-edge optical sharpness with zero wheel distortion.
[BLOCK 4: STAGE & LIGHT]: Seamless high-key technical background (RGB 245, 245, 248) with subtle shadow beneath tires.
[BLOCK 5: EXCLUSIONS]: Negative: perspective keystoning, fish-eye warping, distracting riders, messy backgrounds, unreadable dimension text.
```
