# Scale 02: Handheld & Tabletop E-Commerce Lookbook
### Master Archetypes for Products 6 to 18 Inches (15–45 cm)
*Categories: CPG, Food & Beverage, Specialty Coffee/Tea, Everyday Carry (EDC) Tech, Home Fragrance, Pantry Essentials*

---

## Overview & Scale Dynamics
This is the heartland of consumer packaged goods (CPG) and direct-to-consumer commerce. Products at this scale are held in one or two hands, live on countertops, desks, and shelves, and are purchased frequently. 

Key conversion friction points include taste/scent uncertainty, doubts about packaging durability (e.g. glass breaking in transit), seal failure/leakage anxiety, scale mismatches, and distinguishing artisanal quality from mass-market industrial filler.

All archetypes in this scale adhere strictly to the **5-Block Director's Rig Protocol**.

### Archetype 01: The Amazon & Catalog Compliant Packshot
* **Universal Class:** The Specimen
* **Example Products (Illustrative Guidance, Not an Allowlist):** 500ml olive oil bottle, 12oz roasted coffee bag, hot sauce bottle, insulated travel mug, EDC pocket organizer.
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   Amazon, Google Shopping & Shopify Main Hero (Slot 1). Flawless, distraction-free packaging fidelity. Instant product identification at 160px mobile thumbnail scale.
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   Pure seamless neutral white cyclorama (RGB 255, 255, 255). Product centered horizontally, sitting squarely on the floor plane. Zero extraneous props, badges, or graphics.
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 85mm Prime.  
   * *Aperture:* f/8 to f/11 (complete front-to-back sharpness from front label to rear cap).  
   * *Lighting:* Dual 4x4ft softboxes positioned at 45° to provide even label illumination with a delicate gradient highlight down the curved packaging shoulder. Subtle overhead kicker separating the silhouette from the white background.  
   * *Shadow:* A sharp, razor-thin black contact shadow directly under the packaging footprint, feathering into an authentic 8% soft grey penumbra.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   Crisp readability of matte paper bag folds, gloss varnish on typography, or cold glass reflection proving packaging premiumness.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   Product occupies 82% to 85% of total frame height. Exactly calibrated 5200K daylight white balance to ensure label colors match the physical packaging exactly.
* **Director's Rig Prompt Blueprint:**
```text
[BLOCK 1: HERO]: Commercial hero packshot of [HERO PRODUCT: Brand, form factor, packaging material, finish, volume/weight]. Centered in frame, occupying 82% of vertical frame height, with primary label facing camera in tack-sharp focus.
[BLOCK 2: OPTICAL RIG]: 85mm prime lens, eye-level perspective, f/9 aperture for front-to-back sharpness across cap, body, and base.
[BLOCK 3: LIGHTING RIG]: Balanced 5200K studio softboxes at 45 degrees, subtle overhead kicker rim light, and a crisp, dark contact occlusion shadow grounding the base to the floor plane.
[BLOCK 4: SUBORDINATE STAGE]: Pure seamless non-reflective studio cyclorama background (calibrated pure white RGB 255, 255, 255). Zero props, zero distractions.
[BLOCK 5: EXCLUSIONS]: Negative: floating shadows, colored backgrounds, kitchen clutter, food props, hands, blurry typography, off-center placement.
```

### Archetype 02: The Luminous Morning Kitchen (In-Situ Habitat)
* **Universal Class:** The Habitat
* **Example Products (Illustrative Guidance, Not an Allowlist):** Single-origin coffee bag, ceramic teapot, artisanal olive oil, sourdough bread knife, pour-over dripper.
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   PDP Slot 3 (Lifestyle & Habit Projection) and Top-of-Funnel Social. Connects the product to the comforting, aspirational ritual of morning domesticity.
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   Honed Carrara marble or warm white oak butcher block kitchen countertop. In the soft-focus background: a speckled ceramic mug, pour-over cone or linen towel, and muted cabinetry with soft daylight streaming in.
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 50mm Prime at eye level.  
   * *Aperture:* f/2.8 (shallow depth of field; product is tack-sharp, background dissolves into soft, creamy bokeh).  
   * *Lighting:* Low-angle 45° directional natural morning sunlight streaming from an off-camera window, casting elongated, warm diagonal shadows. Gentle 30% ambient fill on the shadow side to maintain label readability.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   Olfactory and gustatory simulation: amber liquid catching sunlight, visible steam rising from an adjacent cup, natural organic wood grain.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   Product occupies 70% of visual weight in tack-sharp focus. Background props are 2–3 stops darker and blurred into creamy bokeh.
* **Director's Rig Prompt Blueprint:**
```text
[BLOCK 1: HERO]: Commercial in-situ kitchen photograph of [HERO PRODUCT: Packaging, material, finish], resting on the counter and commanding 70% of frame weight in tack-sharp focus with label facing camera.
[BLOCK 2: OPTICAL RIG]: 50mm prime lens, eye-level perspective, f/2.8 aperture for shallow depth of field.
[BLOCK 3: LIGHTING RIG]: Low-angle 3500K morning sunlight streaming from an off-camera window, casting soft diagonal shadows with warm bounce fill and crisp base contact shadow.
[BLOCK 4: SUBORDINATE STAGE]: Grounded on a honed marble or white oak counter. In the distant soft-focus background (f/2.8 bokeh, 2.5 stops darker), a simple ceramic mug and folded linen towel rest softly blurred.
[BLOCK 5: EXCLUSIONS]: Negative: messy dirty dishes, kitchen trash, unwashed pans, harsh overhead fluorescent lights, unreadable label, off-center product.
```

### Archetype 03: The Kinetic Pour & High-Speed Viscosity
* **Universal Class:** The Ritual
* **Example Products (Illustrative Guidance, Not an Allowlist):** Cold-pressed olive oil, craft cocktail syrup, cold brew concentrate, raw wildflower honey, hot sauce.
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   PDP Slot 2 or Paid Social Video/Still. Overcomes taste and sensory hesitation by capturing the physical dynamics of the product in active pour.
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   Clean studio kitchen or architectural stone block. The bottle, carton, or spout is captured mid-pour, with liquid actively streaming into a glass, bowl, or ceramic vessel.
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 85mm Macro.  
   * *Shutter Speed / Strobe:* High-speed sync strobe (1/4000s freeze) capturing individual droplets, air bubbles, and fluid ribbons without blur.  
   * *Aperture:* f/5.6.  
   * *Lighting:* Intense back-rim lighting to illuminate through the liquid stream, making it glow from within.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   Visuomotor salivary response: glistening surface tension, micro-bubbles churning in the glass, rich velvety texture, cold condensation beads breaking on the exterior.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   The pouring bottle must retain 100% accurate brand packaging branding. Stream must look naturally fluid, not gelatinous or synthetic.
* **Director's Rig Prompt Blueprint:**
```text
[BLOCK 1: HERO & RITUAL]: High-speed freeze-motion photograph of [HERO PRODUCT: Bottle, can, or flask], held at a dynamic 45-degree angle mid-pour, occupying 75% of frame area.
[BLOCK 2: POUR ACTION]: A clean, unbroken ribbon of [LIQUID: Color, viscosity, opacity, e.g. luminous golden-green extra virgin olive oil] flows smoothly from the spout into a subordinate glass or dish, caught at 1/4000s shutter speed with glistening surface tension and zero splash mess.
[BLOCK 3: OPTICAL RIG]: 85mm prime lens, f/5.6 aperture, tack-sharp focus locked on the glowing liquid stream and bottle label.
[BLOCK 4: STAGE & LIGHT]: Strong 5500K back-rim lighting shining through the stream to make it glow from within; neutral dark slate surface 2 stops darker than the pour.
[BLOCK 5: EXCLUSIONS]: Negative: murky dirty liquid, messy splatters, chaotic kitchen background, warped bottle logo, motion blur on stream.
```

### Archetype 13: The High-Stakes Inversion & Zero-Leakage Torture Test
* **Universal Class:** The Proof

2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   A high-stakes commercial demonstration set on a clean studio surface. The sealed hero product is turned completely upside down (180° inversion) suspended directly over a high-value, moisture-vulnerable surface (`[HIGH-STAKES SURFACE: open laptop keyboard, crisp white silk shirt, dry legal documents, car leather]`).
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 85mm Prime.  
   * *Aperture:* f/8 for tack-sharp focus across both the inverted cap seal and the dry surface below.  
   * *Lighting:* High-contrast 5500K commercial catalog studio lighting with rim kickers emphasizing the bone-dry seam.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   High tension followed by absolute relief: zero droplet seepage, unbroken airtight silicone gasket seal, bone-dry surface below.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   The product occupies 70% of frame weight. The vulnerable surface below must be visibly pristine and dry.
* **Director's Rig Prompt Blueprint:**
