# Image Direction: Optional Prompt Ingredients & Worked Examples

> Optional craft notes. Open this only when a selected image recipe would benefit from a little more direction; a menu, recipe, native image tool, or a completely different method can be enough.

A useful image brief usually answers four questions: what must the viewer notice first, what action or relationship makes the idea clear, what light and setting help the material read, and which mistakes would damage this particular asset. Put those answers in whatever order and format suits the tool. A compact prompt, a reference image, a storyboard, a real photograph, or a compositing workflow may all be better than a formal scaffold.

When a structured prompt helps, borrow only the ingredients that remove ambiguity:

- **Subject and priority:** supplied product reference, known dimensions, material, finish, label orientation, and the viewer job.
- **View and motion:** camera position, distance, perspective, focus, and any deliberate movement. Focal length alone does not determine perspective; camera distance and framing do too.
- **Light and surface:** key direction, contrast, reflection, color, texture, and a believable contact shadow when the product touches a surface.
- **Context and hierarchy:** setting, secondary props, crop, negative space, or a deliberate equal-weight comparison.
- **Likely failures:** only the relevant risks, such as warped type, wrong parts, extra limbs, false dimensions, impossible reflections, or clutter. A negative phrase is a request to inspect, not a guarantee.

## Borrowable examples

### Example 1: Scale 01 (Micro) — Skincare absorption
```text
A crisp e-commerce skincare illustration or documented demonstration on a clean, seamless neutral white studio surface. The subject is a woman's natural forearm positioned horizontally across the lower half of the frame, occupying 70% of visual weight, with natural unretouched skin pores visible.
A clean two-stage side-by-side demonstration directly on her skin. ON THE LEFT: A single, concentrated micro-droplet of [FORMULA COLOR / TEXTURE FROM SOURCE] resting on the surface of the skin. ON THE RIGHT: The identical formula rubbed smoothly into the skin, showing the supplied or observed finish. If the absorption result is not documented, treat the scene as a concept and avoid a performance caption.
100mm True Macro prime lens, f/8 tack-sharp focus on natural skin pores and micro-texture.
In the background, kept softly out of focus and a little dimmer, the 30ml amber glass dropper bottle rests behind the arm. Clean 5200K high-CRI clinical daylight with subtle soft fill.
Avoid: multiple skin patches, makeup foundation swatches, floating blue blobs, brown oil drops, messy droppers, cluttered bathroom props, airbrushed plastic skin, four arms.
```

### Example 2: Scale 03 (Apparel) — Two-model fit comparison
```text
A commercial e-commerce sizing and fit comparison photograph of two athletic men standing naturally side-by-side on a clean, seamless light-gray studio cyclorama with zero background clutter. The jackets occupy 80% of the visual frame.
ON THE LEFT: An athletic runner (5'11", 170 lbs) wearing the matte charcoal technical mountain shell in Size Medium over a lightweight base layer, showing a clean, tailored athletic fit with sleeves ending cleanly at the wrist. ON THE RIGHT: A taller, broader man (6'2", 210 lbs) wearing the identical jacket in Size Large layered over an insulated hoodie, showing comfortable layering volume across the chest and shoulders without bunching.
75mm prime portrait lens, f/8 tack-sharp focus across both jackets from collar to hem, eye-level perspective.
Clean 5500K commercial catalog studio lighting with soft contact floor shadows, seamless neutral studio cyclorama with zero props.
Avoid: wax figures, stiff mannequins, wooden barn, uncanny valley, CGI render, cartoon, 3D model, four men, identical faces, distorted hands, stiff poses, cluttered background.
```

### Example 3: Scale 04 (Appliances) — Engineering cutaway
```text
A side-by-side e-commerce engineering comparison photograph of two espresso machines on a clean, seamless neutral light-gray studio cyclorama with zero background clutter, occupying 85% of total frame width.
ON THE LEFT (45% of width): A generic budget-style reference machine cutaway, intentionally showing the weak construction the buyer wants to avoid. ON THE RIGHT (55% of width — THE HERO): [HERO PRODUCT] with the side panel removed, showing only the supplied, documented internals. Label the left side “GENERIC LOWER-COST REFERENCE” and the right side with the hero product's verified material claims; do not invent a competitor, component, or test result. The contrast may be deliberately stark to make the pain and relief immediately legible.
60mm prime lens, eye-level perspective, tack-sharp f/8 depth of field across both machines.
High-contrast commercial catalog lighting (5500K) with crisp contact floor shadows and zero reflections on back wall.
Avoid: kitchen background, countertops, blenders, messy wires, dark shadows, tilted horizon, artistic blur, café background.
```

### Example 4: Scale 06 (Mobility) — Axle-height velocity
```text
Close-tracking commercial hero shot of a matte olive-sage commuter e-bike with Gates carbon belt drive. The e-bike frame and drive components occupy 75% of the frame, shot from a 45-degree front-three-quarter profile at axle height.
50mm lens at axle height, 1/40s tracking shutter speed creating smooth horizontal motion blur in background while bike frame remains tack-sharp.
Low-angle crisp twilight lighting, matte olive frame, hydroformed downtube, and hydraulic disc brakes are tack-sharp in foreground.
Soft-focus urban bridge at twilight with smooth 1/40s horizontal motion-blurred lights, keeping full visual focus on the sharp e-bike.
Avoid: distant landscape, tiny bike, wide shot, daytime, parked cars, suburban houses, distracting scenery.
```
