# Scale 01: Micro & Intimate E-Commerce Lookbook
### Master Archetypes for Products Under 6 Inches (< 15 cm)
*Categories: Skincare, Serums, Cosmetics, Fine Jewelry, Fragrance, Supplements, Watches*

---

## Overview & Scale Dynamics
At this physical scale, buyers cannot physically inspect fine seams, feel product weight, smell botanical extracts, or test skin absorption. Purchases are held back by fears of skin irritation, sticky residue, cheap synthetic fillers, poor clasp durability, or mismatched expectations on fluid volume.

Every archetype in this scale is a reference for **macro fidelity, tactile simulation, and careful product geometry**. Use the optional five-block scaffold when it helps, then adapt lens, lighting, crop, and styling to the actual product and tool. A close-up can dramatize texture or the pain of a poor experience, but it does not establish a formulation, absorption, shelf-life, safety, or compatibility result by itself. Before publishing a factual claim, compare the render with supplied packaging/formula references or a documented observation.

### Archetype 01: The Compliant Hero Packshot
* **Universal Class:** The Specimen
* **Example Products (Illustrative Guidance, Not an Allowlist):** 30ml dropper bottle, 50ml moisturizer jar, luxury perfume flacon, fine gemstone ring, supplement capsule bottle.
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   Amazon & Google Shopping Main Image compliance (Slot 1). Primary objective is instantaneous, frictionless identification of packaging shape, volume, and label clarity at 160px mobile thumbnail scale.
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   Pure seamless non-reflective cyclorama background (calibrated RGB 255, 255, 255). Product centered horizontally and positioned in the vertical lower 60% to ground the composition. Zero background props or distractions.
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 90mm or 105mm True Macro lens.  
   * *Aperture:* f/8 to f/11 for front-to-back focal sharpness (entire label and cap in crisp focus).  
   * *Lighting:* Dual balanced softboxes at 45° with diffused frontal fill and subtle kicker rim light to cleanly separate packaging edges from white paper.  
   * *Shadow:* A razor-thin, near-black contact occlusion shadow directly beneath the bottle base, diffusing into a soft 5% gray penumbra. No floating object look.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   Subtle specular highlight along the shoulder of the bottle/jar indicating glass or polished aluminum density; crisp label embossing texture.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   Product occupies 80% to 85% of total frame area. Exact Pantone packaging color calibrated under 5500K daylight balance. Label typography must remain 100% faithful to reference vectors.
* **Director's Rig Prompt Blueprint:**
```text
[BLOCK 1: HERO]: Commercial hero packshot of [HERO PRODUCT: Brand, exact form factor, packaging material, finish, fill volume]. Centered in frame, occupying 80% of vertical frame height, with label facing directly toward camera in tack-sharp focus.
[BLOCK 2: OPTICAL RIG]: 100mm True Macro prime lens, eye-level perspective, f/9 aperture for front-to-back sharpness across cap, shoulder, and base.
[BLOCK 3: LIGHTING RIG]: Balanced 5500K commercial studio lighting, dual 45-degree diffused softboxes with subtle edge rim light, and a crisp, dark contact occlusion shadow grounding the base to the floor.
[BLOCK 4: SUBORDINATE STAGE]: Pure seamless non-reflective studio cyclorama background (calibrated pure white RGB 255, 255, 255). Zero props, zero distractions.
[BLOCK 5: EXCLUSIONS]: Negative: floating shadows, dark background, clutter, food, botanicals, colored tints, blurry label text, off-center placement.
```

### Archetype 02: The Microscopic Actives & Raw Extraction
* **Universal Class:** The Proof
* **Example Products (Illustrative Guidance, Not an Allowlist):** Vitamin C serum, peptide eye cream, cold-pressed face oil, botanical tincture, mineral sunscreen.
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   Brand storytelling and ingredient authority (PDP Slot 2 or 5). Visually justifies premium pricing by making the supplied ingredient story tangible; it is a generic illustration unless the composition and sourcing are documented.
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   Textured raw stone or wet slate surface. The hero product sits grounded in the center, flanked by 1–2 deconstructed raw ingredient specimens (e.g. vanilla pods, fresh rosemary sprig, sea buckthorn berries).
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 85mm Prime.  
   * *Aperture:* f/5.6.  
   * *Lighting:* Soft, natural overcast skylight (5600K) coming from a 60° overhead angle. Gentle contrast with rich deep greens and natural earth tones.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   Olfactory simulation: visible moisture on botanical petals, broken seeds displaying oily sheen, microscopic crystals catching light.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   Raw ingredients must be strictly authentic to formulation. Product maintains 65%–70% visual weight; props are pushed into soft bokeh.
* **Director's Rig Prompt Blueprint:**
```text
[BLOCK 1: HERO]: Commercial still-life photograph of [HERO PRODUCT: Packaging, material, finish, volume], centered and commanding 70% of frame weight in tack-sharp focus with label facing forward.
[BLOCK 2: OPTICAL RIG]: 85mm prime macro lens, f/4.5 aperture, eye-level perspective.
[BLOCK 3: LIGHTING RIG]: Soft diffused 5400K daylight from camera-left with subtle rim lighting carving the packaging silhouette, crisp contact shadow beneath base.
[BLOCK 4: SUBORDINATE STAGE]: Grounded on a honed slate or unsealed limestone slab. In the soft-focus foreground and background (f/2.8 bokeh, 2 stops darker), 1-2 raw botanical elements [BOTANICAL SPECIMENS: e.g., fresh rosemary sprig or cold-pressed seeds] rest softly blurred.
[BLOCK 5: EXCLUSIONS]: Negative: artificial neon colors, synthetic plastic props, cluttered kitchen, floating product, ungrounded shadows, blurry product label.
```

### Archetype 03: The Viscosity, Slump & Micro-Macro Texture Swatch
* **Universal Class:** The Ritual
* **Example Products (Illustrative Guidance, Not an Allowlist):** Ceramide barrier balm, hyaluronic acid gel, clay purifying mask, liquid highlighter, night renewal cream.
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   PDP Slot 2 or 5. Directly addresses the "I can't feel it" objection through tactile simulation. It can show observed viscosity or a deliberately idealized texture concept; only a documented sample supports a factual formula claim.
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   Frosted white acrylic plate, matte ceramic tile, or unsealed honed limestone slab. A single generous, tactile smear or droplet of the product is placed directly adjacent to the open jar or dropper tip.
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 105mm Macro at 1:1 reproduction ratio.  
   * *Aperture:* f/4 (ultra-sharp focus on the apex of the cream smear or liquid bead, with soft, creamy background falloff on the packaging).  
   * *Lighting:* Raking low-angle side lighting at 20° from the left to sculpt microscopic ridges and peaks of the formula swatch.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   Glossy, dewy specular highlights on the formula apex; visible richness and cohesion of the emulsion; a bead of liquid hanging with unbroken surface tension.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   Formula color and opacity should follow a supplied sample or reference. If the composition is unknown, use neutral descriptive texture language and avoid labeling the image as a chemical or performance result.
* **Director's Rig Prompt Blueprint:**
```text
[BLOCK 1: HERO]: Macro formulation swatch and packshot of [HERO PRODUCT: Open jar, bottle with dropper, or tube]. Centered, occupying 75% of frame area in tack-sharp focus.
[BLOCK 2: FORMULA SWATCH]: Directly beside the packaging on the stage surface, a generous, sculptural smear of [FORMULA: Texture, color, opacity, finish, sourced from the real sample when available] displays microscopic peaks, ridges, and a luminous specular highlight that communicates the intended sensory texture. Do not label this as a hydration result unless supported.
[BLOCK 3: OPTICAL RIG]: 105mm True Macro lens, f/4 aperture, low 25-degree perspective locking sharp focus on the formula swatch apex.
[BLOCK 4: STAGE & LIGHT]: Honed matte neutral ceramic tile surface. Raking directional 5200K side light from camera-left casting dimensional shadows across the swatch, background falling off into soft creamy bokeh.
[BLOCK 5: EXCLUSIONS]: Negative: runny watery mess, dirty fingerprints, multiple conflicting swatch colors, plastic skin, cluttered props, floating shadows.
```

   * *Lens:* 105mm Macro.

   Visual weight and temperature of cold glass; the pure, luminous golden refraction of oil or perfume liquid; clean optical brilliance.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   Backlighting must not blow out or obscure label typography. Label printing must remain crisp and readable against the illuminated liquid background.
* **Director's Rig Prompt Blueprint:**
```text
[BLOCK 1: HERO]: Architectural packshot of [HERO PRODUCT: Bottle material, glass thickness, liquid color, label details], occupying 75% of vertical frame height in tack-sharp focus.
[BLOCK 2: OPTICAL RIG]: 100mm prime lens, eye-level perspective, f/5.6 aperture for crisp front-to-back glass wall definition.
[BLOCK 3: LIGHTING RIG]: Hard directional backlight angled at 45 degrees through the rear of the bottle, projecting intricate, glowing golden-amber caustics and liquid refractions across the foreground surface. Crisp contact base shadow.
```

### Archetype 09: The Formulation Purity Split
* **Universal Class:** The Proof
* **Example Products (Illustrative Guidance, Not an Allowlist):** Pure unrefined marula oil, concentrated peptide ampoule, mineral zinc sunscreen, raw honey lip nectar.
1. **Strategic Intent & Conversion Role:**  
   PDP Slot 6 (Differentiation). Uses a controlled generic lower-quality reference beside the hero formula to make the buyer's pain and the premium relief immediately legible. Use only supplied composition facts for the hero; do not imply that the generic reference is a named competitor or laboratory result.
2. **The Physical Stage (The 95%):**  
   A clean side-by-side on neutral acrylic. On the left: a deliberately generic weak or unsatisfying reference state. On the right: the hero formula rendered from supplied color/texture references or an explicitly conceptual ideal. The contrast may be strong; the label should identify the left state as generic or illustrative.
3. **Optical & Lighting Directives:**  
   * *Lens:* 100mm Macro.  
   * *Aperture:* f/8 for equal sharpness on both comparison samples.  
   * *Lighting:* Neutral clinical lab lighting (5500K) over pure white surgical acrylic.
4. **Sensory & Synesthetic Triggers:**  
   Visceral perception of value: visible density, natural color saturation, and unbroken surface tension can communicate a premium sensory difference. They do not prove potency without a source or test.
5. **Invariant Guardrails & Crop Safe-Zones:**  
   Recommended comparison grammar: generic/diluted reference on the left and the hero formula on the right, with clear text zones. Adjust the direction if the platform or reading context calls for it.
* **Director's Rig Prompt Blueprint:**
```text
[BLOCK 1: HERO COMPARISON]: A side-by-side macro formulation comparison photograph on a clean white surgical acrylic stage.
[BLOCK 2: COMPARISON GRAMMAR]: ON THE LEFT (45% width): A thin, runny, watery chemical filler liquid spreading flat and messy with dull color. ON THE RIGHT (55% width - THE HERO): A rich, dense, concentrated droplet of [HERO FORMULA: Color, viscosity, density] holding a perfect convex dome with unbroken surface tension and luminous active radiance.
[BLOCK 3: OPTICS]: 100mm True Macro lens, f/8 aperture, tack-sharp edge-to-edge focal plane across both droplets.
[BLOCK 4: STAGE & LIGHT]: 5500K high-CRI clinical laboratory daylight with soft top diffusion and crisp micro-shadows beneath each droplet.
[BLOCK 5: EXCLUSIONS]: Negative: messy smudges, blurry focus, confusing text, dark shadows, domestic clutter, yellow color cast.
```
