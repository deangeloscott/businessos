# Video Commerce Jobs

> Optional craft notes. Use these labels when you need an idea quickly. Combine them, rename them, or choose another structure when the viewer's question calls for it.

Most product videos make one of four jobs easier:

| Job | Viewer should be able to… | Useful starting moves |
|---|---|---|
| **Kinetic specimen** | inspect form, scale, finish, or assembly | turntable, orbit, dimensional view, exploded arrangement |
| **Documented demonstration** | understand a supported result or compare a problem with relief | deluge, inversion, abrasion, incline, mechanism action |
| **Sensory ritual** | imagine texture, sound, appetite, or craft | pour, spread, melt, click, steam, tactile application |
| **Kinetic habitat** | picture the product in a meaningful environment | commute, stride, room reveal, daily ritual, outdoor setup |

## Kinetic specimen

Use a controlled motion study when form or spatial truth matters. Keep the product neutral when that serves the job; context is optional. A fixed turntable, a gentle orbit, or an assembly separating along a supplied path can reveal geometry. Do not invent internal parts or measurements simply because an exploded view looks convincing.

## Documented demonstration

Use motion to make a performance, durability, fit, or mechanism question easier to decide. Match the scene to the supplied conditions and observed result when making a factual claim. Without that record, a generic poor-versus-ideal contrast or a conceptual stress scene can still dramatize the problem and relief when it is clearly illustrative.

## Sensory ritual

Use one close, legible action to make a material or experience tangible: a ribbon of sauce, a cream spreading, a lid snapping, a lever moving, or steam releasing. The action can create desire without claiming a universal psychological or conversion effect. Sound, slow motion, and macro detail are optional tools.

## Kinetic habitat

Show the product operating in its natural or aspirational setting during purposeful movement. Separate the mood from unverified claims about speed, safety, capacity, durability, or results. A quiet loop, real footage, or a short reveal may be stronger than a busy cinematic sequence.

Choose from the viewer's hesitation and the available evidence. If the product already has a clear story, use that; the existence of these four labels should never force a different route.
