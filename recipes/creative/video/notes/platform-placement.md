# Video Placement & Platform Notes

> Optional craft notes. Use this when the destination changes the opening, pace, crop, sound, or amount of explanation. Verify current platform details in the destination; the platform names here are navigation aids, not permanent specifications.

## Match the video to the viewer's state

| Placement | The viewer usually needs | Useful starting point |
|---|---|---|
| Cold social | immediate context, tension, or a sensory reason to keep watching | short hook, ritual, habitat, or clear generic contrast |
| Retargeting or consideration | an answer to a price, quality, fit, or performance hesitation | supported demonstration, mechanism, or problem-to-relief contrast |
| Product page | calm inspection of form, use, fit, and finish | specimen, utility loop, or short focused demonstration |
| Post-purchase | confidence about unboxing, setup, care, or next steps | unhurried real footage or a clear step sequence |

These are useful tendencies, not fixed mappings. A direct product reveal can beat a hook; a quiet loop can beat a dramatic ad; a real setup clip can be more useful than a generated sequence.

## Adapt the edit

- **Opening:** earn attention with the viewer's actual question. A sudden movement or high-stakes setup is one option; immediate clarity may be better.
- **Information density:** social can benefit from an early readable proposition; product pages often benefit from fewer cuts and more inspection time.
- **Sound:** keep the essential route visible when the placement may begin muted. Add narration, music, or Foley when it improves the physical moment.
- **Crop and controls:** keep the product, labels, comparisons, and important text clear of the destination's current interface. Validate each intended crop in the actual app or marketplace.
- **Next step:** use the supported offer, guarantee, availability, or action for the placement. Do not invent scarcity, reviews, or marketplace claims.

## Platform prompts

**TikTok, Reels, and other vertical feeds:** Native, lo-fi, polished, or demonstration-led treatments can all work. Choose the opening that matches the audience and make the action legible quickly. Prepare alternate crops when the distribution plan needs them.

**Shorts and in-stream video:** Make the premise understandable before a skip or attention change, then let sound and motion deepen the explanation. Check player controls, crop, and the actual skip context.

**Amazon or other marketplace product video:** Follow the current marketplace policy and accepted encoding requirements. Keep the physical product prominent, readable, and consistent with the listing. Check current moderation rules for superlatives, promotional language, reviews, URLs, and off-marketplace references.

**Shopify or headless product pages:** Match the loop, poster, payload, autoplay, and accessibility behavior to the page. Test the actual page on representative devices and check loading and interaction performance. Use the codecs and file sizes the current delivery stack supports rather than copying a fixed budget from this note.

Consult the relevant AURA workflow or current destination documentation when useful for technical requirements and policy details. These notes make common creative adaptations easier to consider; the model and user still choose the appropriate route.
