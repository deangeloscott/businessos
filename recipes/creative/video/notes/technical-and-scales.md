# Video Technical Checks & Scale Map

> Optional craft notes. Use these when the destination or physical product makes technical choices material.

## Compose for the destination

For a vertical placement, keep the core action, product, labels, and important captions clear of the destination's current controls and crop. The exact top and bottom safe areas vary by platform, account, and interface, so treat any guide as approximate and validate the actual preview. A square, landscape, or product-page crop may need a different composition.

## Choose technical settings for the source

- Pick aspect ratio and delivery size from the destination, source, and intended crop; `9:16` and `1:1` are common starting points, not universal defaults.
- Choose frame rate, shutter or motion-blur treatment, and slow motion for the action and available footage. If capture and playback rates are specified, keep the math explicit; 120 fps played at 24 fps is 0.2×.
- Use denoising, upscaling, stabilization, or sharpening only when inspection shows a problem and the pass preserves detail, type, texture, and identity.
- Test the finished file at the target display size, with and without sound, and after the destination crop or interface overlay.

## Physical scale as a creative cue

The paired image menus can suggest useful motion and failure modes:

| Product scale | Motion starting points | Inspect for |
|---|---|---|
| Micro/intimate | drop, spread, pour, click, surface tension | disappearing applicators, melting fingers, unstable labels |
| Handheld/tabletop | inversion, pour, unboxing, lid or closure | warped packaging, rubbery containers, false liquid behavior |
| Body-worn/apparel | stride, drape, weather, stretch | floating garments, anatomy, seams, fabric phase-through |
| Countertop/appliance | lever, stream, disassembly, interface | impossible parts, melting geometry, unreadable controls |
| Architectural/high-ticket | door, room reveal, steam, use context | warped walls, inconsistent perspective, false dimensions |
| Mobility/transport | climb, track, fold, road or load | oval wheels, flickering spokes, unstable rider contact |

Use another motion vocabulary when it better matches the product. The scale map is a quick source of ideas, not a constraint.
