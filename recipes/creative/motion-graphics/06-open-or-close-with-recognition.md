# Open or Close with Recognition

Give a series, product, or brand a compact visual moment that viewers can identify and remember.

## Borrow this treatment

Build one short entrance or exit around a recognizable element: a wordmark, color relationship, shape, product silhouette, recurring phrase, or visual motif. Let the element appear from a meaningful action or resolve into a clear title, name, or next step. Keep the idea legible before making it decorative; a title, reveal, or end card should still work when the motion is removed.

## Adapt it

Use a title when orientation is missing, a product reveal when the object is the story, or a concise ending when the viewer needs a next step. Match the treatment to the existing identity and placement. A recurring motif can be subtle and flexible; it does not need to become a long branded sequence. Give the audience enough time to read names, URLs, or calls to action, and provide a still or text alternative when the asset requires one.

## Example

Open a tutorial with the topic entering alongside the first useful diagram, or close a product clip by returning to the same visual mark as the product and action settle. A recurring series can use the same restrained entrance across episodes without turning every piece into a long title sequence.

## Try it when

Repeated content needs a recognizable frame, or the audience needs quick orientation before or after the main idea.
