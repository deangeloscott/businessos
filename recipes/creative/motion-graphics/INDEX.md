# Motion Graphics Starting Points

A compact menu of ideas for animated type, numbers, diagrams, callouts, interface demos, and short branded moments. Use motion graphics as the whole piece or as a layer over footage, a presentation, a product page, or another video. If a clear visual plan already exists, use it and skip this menu.

Choose the idea that does a useful communication job, borrow the treatment, and adapt it to the actual message, evidence, audience, format, and tool. The recipes are starting points, not a required sequence, effect library, or runtime instruction.

## Choose a starting point

| Job | Starting point |
|---|---|
| Make a statistic or result understandable | [Make one number matter](01-make-one-number-matter.md) |
| Give a headline, quote, or announcement a memorable beat | [Make a statement land](02-make-a-statement-land.md) |
| Show a problem, feature, or process moving from one state to another | [Show the change](03-show-the-change.md) |
| Help viewers follow a screen or product demonstration | [Guide the eye through a demo](04-guide-the-eye-through-a-demo.md) |
| Explain an invisible mechanism, system, or sequence | [Make the mechanism clear](05-make-the-mechanism-clear.md) |
| Create a recognizable title, product reveal, or ending | [Open or close with recognition](06-open-or-close-with-recognition.md) |
| Make the difference between two choices easy to understand | [Compare two choices clearly](07-compare-two-choices-clearly.md) |
| Identify a speaker, source, or quote without interrupting the content | [Put a person or quote in context](08-put-a-person-or-quote-in-context.md) |
| Show where something happens, moves, or is available | [Show place, route, or coverage](09-show-place-route-or-coverage.md) |

## Fast use

Describe the message and the asset you have. If you have no visual plan, open one recipe and ask the model to adapt it directly to the task. It can create the motion, write a tool-specific prompt, storyboard the idea, or combine it with another approach depending on what the user needs. A number can come from a real result, price, date, or comparison; use the supplied source rather than inventing a value. A conceptual illustration should be recognizable as illustrative when that distinction matters.

Motion graphics work well with sound, but they do not require it. For music, narration, Foley, sonic identity, or audio-first pieces, see the [shared audio starting points](../audio/INDEX.md). For product demonstrations and physical continuity, the [e-commerce video menu](../ecommerce-video-lookbooks/INDEX.md) and its optional craft notes may be more useful.

When the destination makes readability, captions, contrast, or motion safety material, use AURA's [content accessibility workflow](../../../systems/content-synthesis/workflows/qa/accessibility/CONTEXT.md) alongside the selected idea.

For a concrete example of preserving a shared focal element through a transition, see Google's [Material choreography guidance](https://m1.material.io/motion/choreography.html). Borrow the continuity idea when it helps; the Material system is not a requirement for these recipes.
