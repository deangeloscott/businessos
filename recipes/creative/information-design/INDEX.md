# Information design and presentations

A compact shelf of static graphics and short presentation structures. Use one when the message is clear but the best visual or narrative shape is not. Borrow the structure, replace the content with supported facts, and change or skip it when another format will communicate better.

## Choose a starting point

| Need | Open |
|---|---|
| Help someone compare options quickly | [At-a-glance comparison](01-at-a-glance-comparison.md) |
| Explain a sequence, service, or system on one page | [Process on one page](02-process-on-one-page.md) |
| Show how a component, layer, or mechanism works | [Annotated detail or mechanism](09-annotated-detail-and-mechanism.md) |
| Make one verified figure meaningful | [One number that carries the message](03-one-number-story.md) |
| Show a trend, comparison, or distribution honestly | [Trend or distribution graphic](08-trend-or-distribution.md) |
| Make a system or dependency visible | [System or relationship map](05-system-relationship-map.md) |
| Help a group understand and choose | [Short recommendation deck](04-short-recommendation-deck.md) |
| Give a busy reader the decision in one page | [One-page executive brief](06-one-page-executive-brief.md) |
| Present a proposal or pitch | [Pitch or proposal deck](07-pitch-or-proposal-deck.md) |

## Use with AURA

These are optional creative starting points, not a presentation method or an execution contract. The model or user can combine them with a better plan, build the artifact directly, or use another tool. When the work needs broader guidance, the relevant AURA references are [format and platform selection](../../../systems/content-synthesis/workflows/strategy/format-platform/CONTEXT.md), [narrative structure](../../../systems/content-synthesis/workflows/strategy/narrative-structure/CONTEXT.md), [desired action](../../../systems/content-synthesis/workflows/strategy/desired-action/CONTEXT.md), and [accessibility QA](../../../systems/content-synthesis/workflows/qa/accessibility/CONTEXT.md).

Use supplied data, source material, brand context, and audience knowledge. Keep illustrative examples visibly illustrative; do not turn a recipe's sample number, comparison, or outcome into an organization claim.
