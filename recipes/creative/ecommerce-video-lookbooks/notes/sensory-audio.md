# Video Sensory & Audio

> Optional craft notes. Use these when sound or a muted placement materially changes the idea.

For broader audio ideas that also apply to podcasts, presentations, interfaces, and audio-only pieces, see the [shared audio starting points](../../audio/INDEX.md). This note stays focused on pairing sound with a commerce video's visible action.

## Give sound a useful role

Sound-on and sound-off behavior varies by audience, placement, device, and account. If the video may begin muted, let the essential situation, action, contrast, and next step remain understandable visually. When audio is available, Foley or music can deepen the physical moment. Neither route is universally better.

For a tactile treatment, synchronize sound with the visible contact: a dropper release and glide, a lid snap, a fabric strike, a lever pull, a latch, a tire on asphalt, or water moving across a surface. Choose the tone and intensity for the product and audience. These cues can shape attention or interpretation; they do not guarantee trust, calm, appetite, or purchase intent.

## Borrowable sound pairings

| Product or action | Visual cue | Optional sound direction |
|---|---|---|
| Cosmetic or formula | drop, spread, squeeze, or cap | soft release, glide, or quiet tap |
| Food or beverage | pour, fizz, crack, or bite | crisp pour, fizz, snap, or close texture |
| Apparel or weather | fabric movement, rain, zipper | clean fabric snap, rain texture, zipper pull |
| Appliance or tool | lever, dial, switch, steam | solid click, mechanical clunk, hiss |
| Furniture or habitat | door, water, room movement | restrained latch, displacement, or room tone |
| Mobility | tire, latch, motor, road | tire hum, pin click, or low mechanical texture |

Use captions or micro-labels when they clarify a visible or documented detail. Pull values from the supplied record; do not use text to invent ratings, purity, airtightness, or safety. Inspect the mix and the silent version in the actual placement.
